# Spawn camp and first mine

Home named camp is at (-169, 64, 201) in the overworld. Genesis is claimed. Four diamond hammers and both travel cakes arrived. The first successful iron strike was at (-187, 59, 199), below a grass and dirt bank west of camp. Magnetite also occurs there. The surface scan identified coal near (-169, 55, 185) and rubber trees within 48 blocks of camp. A city building southeast of camp contains chests and furnaces on upper floors.

The first goal here is to run multiple cobblestone furnaces, keep twelve iron ingots on hand and re-smelt twelve others for Iron Supply. Keep mining while the first batch smelts.
