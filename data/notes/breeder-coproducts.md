# Keep the uranium coproduct for the next two flights

Page 669 unlocks Element 092. It pays a quarter for 64 Stabilized Uranium. The quest book offers no Stabilized Uranium as a reward. JEI lists three casting recipes. Each uses the ball mold and 144 mB of Uranium 238, 235 or 233 for one piece at 16 EU/t for 500 base ticks. The MV caster already has the ball mold for the Tier Five fuel.

Processing nine depleted LEU-235 items for the first Naquadah flight also yields 360 tiny Uranium-238 clumps. Nine tiny clumps pack into one full clump, giving 40. Preserve those for the uranium casts. The remaining 24 can come from ordinary Uranium 238 dust or ingots at the thermal centrifuge. Keep the rare fuel clumps for the breeder. Cast the two plutonium batches first so the Naquadah flight can leave.

The first Tier Five marble-ore flight and the Tier Six uranium, osmium and iridium flight each take 32 Stabilized Uranium and sixteen Quantum Flux. Completing the 64-piece retrieval before either launch covers the uranium for both. The Tier Six recipe also needs sixteen Wither Realm Data. The first Tier Five ship is already committed to the Naquadah flight, so the marble shipment will need another ship.

The controller finished all nine fuel and the lever is off. The HV centrifuge accepted the whole lot and ran each item for 154 ticks at 384 EU/t. All 360 tiny uranium clumps arrived in ME. The other three returns were 72 tiny clumps apiece, enough for eight full items of each kind. The workshop has also made the separate Fission Controller for the ship.

Both eight-clump batches yielded 1,152 mB apiece in the LV fluid extractor. The MV ball caster made sixteen from each. All 32 Stabilized Plutonium for the Tier Five flight reached storage. For the later uranium delivery the stock check found eight common dust, fourteen rare dust and another 255 tiny piles of the rare dust. The same ball mold accepts either fluid. Use the 40 common clumps first, then the eight common dust. The surplus rare dust can cover the last sixteen while keeping at least 27 full equivalents for future breeder runs. This uses materials already on hand and saves another ore purchase.

The caster finished its last two balls from two already-refined ingots. Both had been omitted from the count of surplus metal. Page 661 paid an OmniQuarter with the full 64 Stabilized Uranium in inventory. The original reserve for future breeder fuel is still covered. The extractor returned to its normal fluid-output setting.
