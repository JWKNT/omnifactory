# Renewable workshop metals

Copper has been the largest metal order for the cutter. A Pristine Spider Matter gives twelve copper ingots directly in the existing Loot Fabricator. Skeleton gives eight tin, Zombie gives twelve iron, and Witch gives sixteen redstone. Spider, Skeleton and Zombie each draw 64 RF/t in the chamber. Witch draws 512. Ordinary matter from these models can replace the Slime model's Overworldian Matter while their pristine drops supply metals. Bank the advanced Shulker and Enderman models between runs. They still cover diamonds and pearls.

Start with Skeleton after crafting the 22-bone batch for the two blaze rods. The model needs only a bone and a blank. Reserve the first nine additional Overworldian Matter for Spider. One string, a slimeball and two matter craft four cobwebs, then one web and seven more matter upgrade a blank to Spider. Five string and 32 slimeballs were in void storage at the first survey. For Zombie, the camp chest last held one raw chicken; a chicken and one Overworldian Matter give twelve rotten flesh. One flesh upgrades a blank. Witch takes a glass bottle, a blank and seven matter. No purchased mob drops are required for these routes.

Each blank needs four electrical steel plates, three fine gold wires, one pulsating mesh and a Basic Circuit. Keep the current cutter order moving, then use the first metal model during the long wafer and plastics jobs. Early pristine output is random. Count the actual fabricated metal before reducing an ore order. The four metal models have not yet been built.

The sixteen Slime simulations have finished. The chamber returned to Shulker with 24 Overworldian Matter accounted for. The workshop keeps a request for the first Skeleton and Spider batches after the cutter quest. Each uses up to 32 polymer and the corresponding metal choice in the fabricator. The existing Slime, a Skeleton, a Spider and five other overworld models are all required together for quest 686, which pays ten quarters. Keep all reusable models. Guardian's first prismarine shard can come from nether quartz plus Overworldian Matter; Creeper's gunpowder can come from coal plus one matter.

The camp pickup returned the raw chicken along with four lead ingots and twelve galena ore. The chicken is in workshop bulk storage. Reserve one Overworldian Matter for its rotten-flesh recipe. The next metal-model orders can now include Zombie for iron. The Pyrolyse Oven bill is a reason to do this early: a stock-adjusted dry run for the full oven indicated 180 iron ingots across plates, casings, rods and mechanisms. Keep the Skeleton, Zombie and Spider models available as reusable capital. The clathrate helper now scales the polymer reserve with the outstanding metal-model runs.

The eighth clay-and-dust pair in a polymer charge finishes 96 seconds after the first load at LV. During the processor order that occupied the same alloy smelter needed for red alloy. One batch already supplied more than the 32 polymer needed for a model change. The tending script now checks the recovered stack before making more clay. After the first ME Drive, the brewery order will place a second Basic Alloy Smelter at (5,80,7) below the existing LV cable for polymer. Its JEI recipe takes one LV hull, two Basic Circuits, four quadruple copper wires and two single tin cables. The socket was empty when surveyed. The original smelter remains available for metals.

The first Skeleton Data Model has been crafted. While the Drive parts were being assembled, the chamber switched from Shulker to Skeleton and loaded 32 Pulsating Polymer Clay. It produced its first Overworldian Matter, with 30 clay still in the fuel slot and the second simulation running. Watch the first Pristine Skeleton Matter through to the fabricator's tin output before counting the metal.

The configured pristine chances are 5%, 10%, 20% and 30% from Basic through Self Aware. A 32-simulation Basic run can finish without a pristine drop. Keep using the same model so its experience carries forward. The first Skeleton model had completed 26 runs and produced 26 ordinary matter while the workshop was still making its new smelter.

Once the dedicated polymer smelter is installed, keep 64 clay in reserve. Use 32 at a time for a metal model while reserves hold at least 40 diamonds and 16 pearls. The first requested Skeleton, Zombie and Spider runs take priority. On later runs choose the metal with the lowest fraction of its target: 64 iron, 48 copper or 32 tin. Use only models already owned for these repeats. The other 32 clay can support the fuel models if the diamond or pearl supply falls. The chamber is shared; each metal has its own durable model.

The first run ended with all 32 ordinary matter and zero pristine. A second 32-clay charge went into the same Skeleton model. At 37 lifetime simulations its output held one Pristine Skeleton Matter. The fabricator accepted it, switched from Ender Pearl to Tin Ingot, and its output slot showed eight GregTech tin ingots. This is the workshop's first verified renewable tin.

At the end of the second batch the Skeleton model showed 64 lifetime simulations and Advanced tier. The fabricator had already delivered four separate stacks of eight tin ingots to the player. The stock survey can be lower than 32 tin because the wiremill is feeding tin cable and capacitor orders.
