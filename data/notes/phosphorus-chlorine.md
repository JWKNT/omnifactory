# Chlorine and phosphorus from the same ore

For the next PVC charge, use the phosphorus line to make the two buckets of chlorine. The first charge took rock salt and gave potassium. Apatite electrolysis gives chlorine plus the phosphate needed for phosphorus and the calcium used for bone meal. The workshop has 26 Apatite Ore and three loose gems. Smelt three ore for fifteen gems, grind all eighteen, and electrolyze the dust in the spare MV machine at (6,82,1). The two charges give two buckets of chlorine, six phosphate dust and ten calcium dust. The LV machine can continue splitting water.

The chest survey found another two phosphate dust and one Tricalcium Phospate Dust. Smelt two of the 27 Tricalcium Phospate Ore for six dust. Electrolyze five dust for two phosphate and three calcium. That brings the phosphate to ten. Electrolyze the ten for two phosphorus and eight buckets of oxygen. Hold both phosphorus for page 539 and its nickel. This is the queued phosphate-reserve order. It uses the electric furnace and macerator when they have room.

Two phosphorus dust, three buckets of water, five of oxygen and an integrated circuit can make two buckets of phosphoric acid in a chemical reactor. Page 276 pays two nickels for a bucket. Two buckets plus one aluminium dust and one Energetic Blend give four Luminessence for page 275. Reserve the current Energetic Blend for the HV converter. For later scale, nine apatite dust with five buckets of sulfuric acid and ten of water give three phosphoric acid and one hydrochloric acid. That hydrochloric acid can make another vinyl chloride bucket on circuit 3. Keep the fresh sulfuric acid for board coating and ethylene until that line has a surplus.
