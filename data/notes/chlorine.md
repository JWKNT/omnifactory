# First chlorine for the PVC branch

Quest 507 asks for a bucket of chlorine and pays a nickel. Rock salt and ordinary salt each cost five pennies for 32 ore. One ore in an ordinary furnace gives three salt. The LV electrolyzer takes two rock salt for a bucket of chlorine and a potassium dust in 288 ticks. The regular salt route yields sodium, which is already plentiful at this workshop. Salt water first needs salt in the mixer; the other early routes need magnesium chloride or an acid supply.

Use the LV electrolyzer between other charges. The hydrogen centrifuge above it has no chlorine recipe in JEI. Take a bucket from the electrolyzer's displayed chlorine tank, claim quest 507 and keep the bucket for PVC. The oven order now includes this first batch.

The first ore smelted into three rock salt. Two went into the electrolyzer and produced 1,000 mB chlorine. The machine has a display switch for its six output slots. The bucket helper now selects the fluid display and clicks the named tank. Quest 507 accepted the bucket and paid one nickel. The bucket is in workshop storage and 31 raw rock salt ore remain.
