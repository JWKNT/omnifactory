# What follows the energy acceptor

One charger batch and four glass electrolysis batches supplied eight fluix. The acceptor needs four of those as compressed plates, four electrical steel plates and an LV hull. Keep at least one unpressed fluix crystal for quest 282. The powered charger's adjacent RF conduit has an open east face at (8,81,9) for the acceptor.

Next, the Crystal Growth Chamber takes another LV hull, four charged certus and four dark steel bars. Three dark steel ingots at the lathe make six rods; six rods at the bench make eight bars. Its quest (1) asks for the chamber, three kinds of seed and all three pure crystals. Make at least two seeds of each type, preserve one of each for detection, and grow the others. One sand plus the appropriate certus, nether quartz or fluix dust makes two seeds. The LV macerator can make each dust from its corresponding crystal.

An ME Chest will need two dark steel blocks, three different pure crystals, quartz glass, an illuminated panel and a small storage crate. An ME Drive then consumes that chest with an MV emitter, MV sensor, basic circuits and aluminium plates. A storage bus also reaches into MV: it consumes an MV piston and an ME Interface, whose bench route needs an MV hull with an MV robot arm. Prioritize producing repeatable circuits and more dark steel alongside the first AE2 network. The seven dark steel left after the charger covered the growth chamber rods and can cover the four controller plates; a chest needs a further eighteen ingots.

The terminal quest pays a nickel. The ME Chest and ME Drive quests each pay a quarter. Fit the terminal to AE2 glass cable. Reserve one pure crystal of each type for the chest. The small storage crate takes four ordinary chests, four planks and a wood casing. The casing takes another four planks, four sticks and one log. The crafting table makes four chests together from eight logs, and a rubber log makes four jungle planks. No quest lists dark steel ingots or the crate as a reward. Form each dark steel block at the bench from nine ingots.

Terminal (62) is complete. Eight more fluix from four matched sets supplied the panel and cable. The first cable at (10,81,9) connects to the east face of the growth chamber; its north face carries the terminal. scripts/terminal_face.py verified that a player can open the ME monitor from (10,80,7). The cable leaves the charger's east output face accessible. The factory photo is screenshots/shot-20261119-163441.jpg.

The next combined order is appliedenergistics2:chest and appliedenergistics2:storage_cell_1k. In addition to the four red alloy plates, basic circuit and four glass in the 1k component, the housing takes four electrical steel plates and four fine red alloy wires. scripts/lv_order.py now knows both recipes. scripts/factory_round.py --continuous --work-ahead can keep counting the chests, dispatching machine batches and working at the bench until it completes an order or runs out of available work. The terminal and cable were produced by this route. Verify the first cell by storing an item through the terminal, closing the screen, reopening and withdrawing it.
