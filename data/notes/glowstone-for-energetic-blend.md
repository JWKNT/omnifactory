# Glowstone for the two projector lines

The medium projector's Microversium and the elite table's Luminessence both consume Energetic Blend. Redstone is plentiful, but the mixer had no glowstone dust at 100h53m. The third simulation chamber was busy with the Thermal Elemental model for Petrotheum. Switching to the Witch eventually yields sixteen glowstone per pristine matter.

Thirty-eight tricalcium phosphate dust were already in storage. In this instance the LV mixer combines one with one gold dust to make two glowstone in 80 ticks at 15 EU/t. Install this recipe beside Energetic Blend on the same interface. Mill up to eight surplus gold bars at a time. Keep four bars for other recipes and one loose gold dust for black bronze. Recovered redstone lamps also yield four glowstone and four redstone in a macerator when available. The Witch can continue replenishing bulk glowstone after the thermal shift.
