# The eighth Overworldian model

The workshop already owns Slime, Zombie, Skeleton and Spider models. Quest 686 pays ten quarters for those four together with Creeper, Witch, Guardian and Thermal Elemental. Each remaining model takes a blank and seven Overworldian Matter. Creeper needs gunpowder, obtainable from coal and one extra matter. Guardian's prismarine shard can come from nether quartz and one extra matter. Witch needs a glass bottle. Use the ordinary matter from the queued Zombie and Spider simulation batches for these models.

Thermal Elemental needs Cryotheum Dust. On Peaceful, the route in quest 309 uses the MV Chemical Reactor. The first MV Electrolyzer can go at (6,82,1), above the fourfold copper cable that already powers the cutter. The socket was air. It takes an MV hull, two Primitive Processors, four silver wires, one insulated copper cable and glass. Quest 71 pays a nickel. Leave the LV electrolyzer and its deuterium centrifuge working. Make the MV machine after the oven's processor order.

The MV electrolyzer can separate six Bastnasite Dust into a bucket of fluorine and three buckets of oxygen. The alternatives use thirteen Topaz or Blue Topaz Dust, nineteen Mica, twenty Lepidolite or twenty-two Biotite Dust. The acid-splitting LV recipe would require the acid this line is meant to produce. A purchase of 32 bastnasite ore costs five nickels. Keep the blue lens in the engraver kit. Use one bucket each of hydrogen and fluorine in the reactor, then the resulting acid and one Pulsating Dust in an MV reactor. The bucket of Elemental Reduction Fluid completes quest 309.

JEI shows that the MV reactor takes snow and 100 mB of reduction fluid per Blizz Powder recipe. A ball mold and 250 mB plain water in the planned solidifier can make a snowball. Keep enough snow for the Blizz Powder and the final dust recipe. One snowball, two powder and redstone craft Cryotheum Dust. Register quests 308 and 301 before using their items. Retain the finished model for the eight-model bounty. The separate fluorine bounty 621 costs 64 solidified fluorine, a bucket per piece, so leave that for a larger supply.
