# The eighth Overworldian model

The workshop already owns Slime, Zombie, Skeleton and Spider models. Quest 686 pays ten quarters for those four together with Creeper, Witch, Guardian and Thermal Elemental. Each remaining model takes a blank and seven Overworldian Matter. Creeper needs gunpowder, obtainable from coal and one extra matter. Guardian's prismarine shard can come from nether quartz and one extra matter. Witch needs a glass bottle. Use the ordinary matter from the queued Zombie and Spider simulation batches for these models.

Thermal Elemental needs Cryotheum Dust. On Peaceful, the route in quest 309 uses the MV Chemical Reactor. The first MV Electrolyzer can go at (6,82,1), above the fourfold copper cable that already powers the cutter. The socket was air. It takes an MV hull, two Primitive Processors, four silver wires, one insulated copper cable and glass. Quest 71 pays a nickel. Leave the LV electrolyzer and its deuterium centrifuge working. Make the MV machine after the oven's processor order.

The MV electrolyzer can separate six Bastnasite Dust into a bucket of fluorine and three buckets of oxygen. The alternatives use thirteen Topaz or Blue Topaz Dust, nineteen Mica, twenty Lepidolite or twenty-two Biotite Dust. The acid-splitting LV recipe would require the acid this line is meant to produce. A purchase of 32 bastnasite ore costs five nickels. Smelting just three ore gives all six clean dust for the first charge. A macerator gives four crushed ore per block if throughput becomes more important than startup speed. Keep the blue lens in the engraver kit. Use one bucket each of hydrogen and fluorine in the reactor, then the resulting acid and one Pulsating Dust in an MV reactor. The bucket of Elemental Reduction Fluid completes quest 309.

JEI shows that the MV reactor takes snow and 100 mB of reduction fluid per Blizz Powder recipe. A ball mold and 250 mB plain water in the planned solidifier can make a snowball. Keep enough snow for the Blizz Powder and the final dust recipe. One snowball, two powder and redstone craft Cryotheum Dust. Register quests 308 and 301 before using their items. Retain the finished model for the eight-model bounty. The separate fluorine bounty 621 costs 64 solidified fluorine, a bucket per piece, so leave that for a larger supply.

The wafer campaign now has a separate request for the MV Electrolyzer after the pattern hardware and MV assembler. It checks quest 71 before placement, looks for usable dust already in the stores, and verifies fluorine in the output tank. This branch is queued.

The workshop can now queue Creeper, Guardian and Witch one blank at a time while it makes the oven. A Creeper pristine yields 32 coal for phenol or furnace fuel at 64 RF/t in the Simulation Chamber. Guardian yields 12 aluminium dust at 1,024 RF/t, and Witch yields 16 redstone at 512 RF/t. These are secondary to the first Zombie and Spider orders. Keep every model. All eight must be carried together to collect the reward.

After the plastic solidifier and first pattern hardware are commissioned, scripts/snow_stock.py is queued with the MV electrolysis order. Three water buckets and the ball mold give twelve snowballs, then the plate mold returns to the solidifier. Two snow blocks take eight snowballs. The MV ethylene reactor can combine them with 200 mB from the first reduction-fluid bucket. Two Blizz Powder, one snowball and one redstone then give two Cryotheum Dust. scripts/first_cryotheum.py uses the LV reactor's surplus hydrogen and the separate MV electrolyzer's fluorine; the hydrogen centrifuge stays supplied. The model and chemical steps are queued.

The first snow run shares the solidifier with polyethylene. It now collects the three finished sheets and recovers the remaining 80 mB of PE in the portable Fluid Tank before loading water. The installed MV assembler can accept that PE on its input side. The transfer checks the amount received and frees the tank for the later solder and water transfers. Once the snowballs are made, restore the plate mold for PVC. The residue transfer is queued for its first live run.
