# Collecting drops in the field

The last bedrock run involved walking over scattered Grains of Infinity; quartz crystals have also landed on top of the MV equipment. A Basic Fluxomagnet is a reusable pickup tool for those trips. Its new-item recipe takes three iron, two redstone and two lead. The other bench recipes recolor an existing magnet, and the Wired Charger recipe charges one. Quest 415 pays one nickel. Two spare lead ingots and twelve galena ore were in workshop storage, and both factory CEFs already have their own capacitors.

The north MV capacitor can recharge the 40,000 RF tool while the engraver and cutter are idle. Take that capacitor briefly, enable equipment charging by sneak-using it in the air and hold the magnet. Return the capacitor to its CEF before resuming production. Check the tool's energy and the CEF's capacity separately.

The magnet is made and quest 415 paid a nickel. The idle MV capacitor supplied exactly 40,000 RF to the held tool. It returned to the north CEF, which showed its full 2,504,096 EU capacity again. The charger and bedrock collection routines can pulse the magnet for loose charged certus or Grains of Infinity when the item is within the magnet’s displayed radius. A field pickup with it is still to be verified.
