# Refinery gas for the workshop grid

At 126h25m the board reactor repeatedly fell to the first tick of a 480 EU/t recipe. Its HV converter held about 1,100 EU despite a fitted capacitor. The diesel generator had 448 mB of Light Fuel, below its 512 mB cycle, and ME had no more. The lapidary dynamos were already supplying the shared grid. Giving the freezer a capacitor helped its short heats. The board, eye bath and other machines also need more generation.

ME holds 1,872 buckets of Refinery Gas. This instance's JEI lists the gas as a Gas Turbine fuel. A Turbo Gas Turbine takes one HV hull, two HV motors, three steel rotors, two Advanced circuits and one insulated gold cable. Two spare hulls and five eligible Nanoprocessors are in stock. Use the assembler recipe for rotors with steel plates, rings and solder. The MV model would produce one quarter of the HV model's rated output on the same converter.

Fit the turbine at (36,82,-10) facing south directly into the existing four-amp HV CEU at (36,82,-9). Its north socket was air when surveyed. The diesel generator feeds the same converter from the opposite side. A west fluid export can mount on existing glass at (35,82,-10). Test one tank of gas and observe fuel consumption and CEU output before enabling a sustained feed. Keep a gas reserve for chemistry while evaluating the fuel rate.
