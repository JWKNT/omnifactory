# Galena from the HV mill to the MV washer

The second Pattern Expansion Card opened nine more slots on the HV ore-mill interface at (12,83,1). The first new card takes one Galena Ore and promises two Crushed Galena Ore. I ordered eight through ME. During that order the mill showed galena processing at 192 EU/t for 100 ticks. The MV washer at (12,82,3) received crushed galena through its existing filtered inlet and started its 200-tick cycle at 64 EU/t.

I counted 19 Purified Galena Ore in stock before the order and 20 in ME while the washer was still running. No hand transfer was needed between the mill and washer. The same inlet already accepts sphalerite. The purified ores feed the indium line for the Quantumprocessor wafers.
