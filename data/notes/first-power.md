# What the first power station costs

The Steam Dynamo recipe uses an iron furnace, two wrought iron gears, two copper plates, an excitation coil and a redstone transmission coil. The gears alone take eight wrought iron rods and eight wrought iron plates. Save five red alloy plates for the excitation coil. The transmission coil needs three silver rods and four redstone. A surface scan in the area showed exposed silver ore around (-192, 44, 165). Omnicoins are another option.

The LV CEF needs four conductive iron energy conduits, a basic circuit, two 4x conductive iron wires, an LV hull and a chest. The hull requires tin cables. Craft six energy conduits to cover the CEF and the short RF connection from the dynamo. Reserve the remaining conductive iron wires for the larger wire bundles and check the cable task on the CEF page.

For the first circuit, keep three insulated red alloy cables, one wrought iron plate, two resistors, two vacuum tubes and a coated board. Vacuum tubes require glass tubes, each made from four panes. Resistors and tubes both use paper. The cane plot is a permanent paper supply; books from the city convert to two paper each.
