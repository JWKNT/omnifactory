# Polymer production beside the ME interface

The old smelter finished its ten clay pairs and had forty polymer in its output. That shelf still needed a player to empty it. After collecting the forty and feeding the Enderman I moved the empty Basic Alloy Smelter from (5,80,7) to (6,83,6). It sits on the fourfold LV cable beside the wiremill interface at (7,83,6). The machine reports 1,024 EU, east item output enabled, and input disabled on its output face. The north aisle over the macerator gives access to its inputs. The move used the existing machine, power cable and interface.

The clay mill took 32 sand and sent Dust Blocks to the water-fed reactor. I collected fresh clay and charged the smelter with four clay and four Pulsating Dust. While that ran, I collected seven polyethylene sheets for blank patterns. At the crafting terminal, the sum of network polymer, queued Shulker fuel and completed Shulker runs rose from 767 to 771. Four freshly made polymer reached ME without a player touching the output. The kiln and the chamber now both handle their own output transfers. Clay and Pulsating Dust are still loaded by the player. scripts/polymer_station.py locates the kiln for the workshop routines.
