# Mixer supplies and the first Energetic Alloy

Quest 273 pays a nickel for the LV Mixer. It takes four glass, a tin rotor, an LV motor, two basic circuits and an LV hull. The current order uses the stock already in the main workshop and its digital cell. Two nickels bought a fresh 32 copper ore. Four were put straight into the east furnace for the immediate shortfall; the other 28 are in metals for doubling. West and north each received six iron ingots for wrought iron. Nine of the twenty-six recent aluminium dust were loaded into the EBF while scouting the city.

One redstone and one glowstone dust make two Energetic Blend in the mixer. Each blend plus a gold ingot makes an Energetic Alloy in the EBF at 120 EU/t for 400 ticks. One energetic ingot plus one ender pearl makes a Vibrant Alloy there in 600 ticks. Keep at least one energetic ingot for its quest before converting it. Two pearls are available after the library chest trip. The fallback glowstone recipe in the same mixer uses one gold dust and one Tricalcium Phospate Dust for two glowstone dust. Five pennies buy 32 Tricalcium Phospate Ore (gregtech:ore_phosphor_0), and a single ore smelts into three dust. The spelling is the one used by the game.

For the first Deep Mob Learning chamber, four carbon meshes need sixteen string and four coal dust. Four pulsating dust per carbon mesh then produce the required pulsating meshes. The chamber also consumes a dark steel hull (eight dark steel plates around an LV hull), three basic circuits and a glass pane. If city chests and cobwebs fall short of sixteen string, a sword and a spare rubber sapling give one plant fiber; two fiber give three string. Void metals already holds uraninite ore for the pulsating dust. The loot fabricator also needs Energetic Alloy, so keep a reserve.

The city trip yielded another four ender pearls, three gold ingots, twelve string and one dark steel ingot. The found capacitors and golden apple were banked at camp. No glowstone showed on the camp surface scan, so five pennies bought 32 Tricalcium Phospate Ore. Three went into the north furnace. The west and east furnaces received sixteen sand for the mixer and circuit panes. The nine new aluminium ingots were collected from the EBF.

The mixer is installed at (6,81,3), east of the cable at (5,81,3). Quest 273 paid its nickel. Two gold dust and two phosphate dust gave four glowstone dust. All four went back through the mixer with four redstone for eight Energetic Blend. The blast furnace accepted all eight with eight gold ingots, and its controller identified Energetic Alloy as the product at 120 EU/t. Reserve four ingots for the loot fabricator.
