# Air and nitrogen for the refinery

The first twenty saltpeter dust came from a 95-dust stock made renewable by Thermal Elemental matter. Four MV electrolysis cycles produced four buckets of nitrogen, twelve of oxygen and four potassium dust. The shared oxygen network started moving the oxygen before the player reached its GUI. A portable tank drew sixteen buckets from the full oxygen inlet at the PVC reactor instead. The programmed MV chemical reactor at (8,81,1) finished four buckets of nitrogen dioxide. The naphtha recipe needs exactly that amount for the twelve-bucket reserve.
