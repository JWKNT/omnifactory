# First AE2 machine patterns

The five patterns for the first Molecular Assembler cover bench work. Wrought iron plate is a good first processing pattern. The brewery, distillery and auxiliary power order alone called for 45 plates. A Basic Compressor makes one per wrought iron ingot. A crafting hammer consumes two ingots, and the other JEI routes need a mold, a block of nine ingots, a higher-tier extruder or a Thermal Compactor. Aluminium plate can use the same compressor with a second pattern.

I surveyed a separate compressor socket at (5,82,8) directly above the existing fourfold LV cable. The block above it at (5,83,8) is free for a second ME Interface. Four Ender IO ME Conduits would connect that interface to the top of the commissioned ME Energy Cell: place them at (8,83,9), (8,83,8), (7,83,8) and (6,83,8). Those four spaces were air. The last two also carry the proposed pristine-matter route. An ME Conduit can share a block with an Item Conduit. The Energy Cell and ME Interface are both full blocks. Verify the interface comes online before encoding patterns. The neighboring route at z9 already carries the farm timer.

Keep the original compressor available for hand-fed workshop jobs. Give the dedicated compressor an upward output, enable item auto-output and use its screwdriver setting to admit inputs through that output face. An interface on top can then deliver ingots and collect plates from the same face. A separate interface also keeps processing inputs away from the Molecular Assembler. The two blank patterns left after the first five bench recipes and the Pattern Terminal are enough. Budget another MV robot arm and hull for the second interface. Commission the crafting CPU and prove the first pane job before spending those parts. The plate test is two additional plates in the terminal after ordering two, with two ingots consumed. This branch is surveyed and has yet to be built.
