# Library and lower city store

The library south of camp has another floor below the first book room. Enter at (-166,65,249). At (-168,60,254), scripts/open_floor.py 54 opened the terracotta floor and dropped safely to the lower room. Two shelves around (-170,55,245) gave six books. A chest at (-172,54,253) yielded thirteen manganese ingots, three dark steel ingots, a Drill Core and Black Quartz. Dark Steel asks for twelve ingots; keep these three. There are several more chests on level 72 towards x=-155, z=263, worth checking when working on the EnderIO branch.

The upper building now has a verified route and loot survey. At x=-169 approach from z247, jump south against the ladder at z249, then keep moving toward its backing wall with jump held. The ladder climbs at about 0.12 blocks per tick. Step east once the player's feet pass y72. A long pause on it lets the player slide all the way back down. From the y72 floor, scripts/city_loot.py reached the rooms around (-153,72,263). The chest at (-169,66,251) and the upper rooms together supplied four ender pearls, twelve string, three gold, one dark steel, a golden apple and eight found capacitors. That string covers three of the first four carbon meshes and those pearls can go straight into Vibrant Alloy. No glowstone or redstone lamps appeared in the camp surface survey out to 70 blocks.
