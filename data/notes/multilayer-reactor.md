# A separate reactor for multilayer boards

The second Quantum Processor Array is in storage. Its final ME order scheduled the remaining circuit grades, boards and IV assembly. The current shared EV reactor handled both QBit wafers and multilayer boards, but it had to drain usable sulfuric acid twice to admit radon. It also held the foil and substrate idle until the player supplied acid. Future Assembly Line casing crafts alone need 35 elite circuits. Give boards a separate reactor so those changes of fluid stop interrupting the wafer line.

JEI gives one board per 16 electrum foil, one reinforced circuit board and 250 mB sulfuric acid. The recipe takes 100 ticks at 480 EU/t. An HV reactor suffices. The QBit recipes require 1,920 EU/t. Keep the EV machine for those and for indium. A second EV hull and converter would cost more titanium and another circuit.

The HV roof has a powered double gold cable at (11,84,1). The surveyed block immediately above it is air, and the position south of that at (11,85,2) is open with clear space above. Fit the gold tap and place the HV reactor there. Its eastern neighbor at (12,85,2) can take a full interface, with a fluix connection east to (13,85,2) from the existing wafer interface. The upper faces stay accessible from the south. Stage its steel rotor, HV motor, hull, two advanced circuits, glass and gold cables. Keep the first spare ME Interface for the west furnace. Supply a measured acid charge while commissioning the board reactor, and verify that a board returns before moving the existing EV pattern. A continuous acid export would fill the machine with up to 64 buckets. The indium line needs 36 buckets per full batch, so a permanent fluid connection needs a stock cutoff.

At 82h27m the roof tap at (11,85,1) was in place. The cable read 512 V and four amps. The reactor site immediately south is still open. A nine-dust stainless batch from six iron dust and one each of nickel, manganese and chrome can cover the casing without waiting for 36 stainless dust.

At 83h11m the roof reactor was installed at (11,85,2). It charged to 16,000 EU on the 512 V tap. The Ender Fluid Conduit directly beneath its floor immediately filled it with 64 buckets of oxygen. Disconnect the upward fluid connection, bank that oxygen, then charge one bucket of sulfuric acid for the first four boards. Both faces of the new item route are open. The EV board interface at (18,82,−10) has a single network connection below and only one card. Keep the interface there for indium work. Retire its board card after the roof interface advertises the same recipe and any active board order finishes.
