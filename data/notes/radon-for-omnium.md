# Radon for the elemental bounty and Omnium

The four-salt charge for the miner field generators raised radon in ME from 450 to 4,450 mB. The EV electrolyzer's installed recipe takes one Radium Salt and returns one Rock Salt and 1,000 mB radon at 2,000 EU/t for 200 ticks. There were 240 salts left. The first Improved Ender Eyes use only 500 mB per eye.

The MV ball mold takes 1,000 mB per Solidified Radon. Page 659 pays a quarter for carrying 64, and the 9×9 table uses the solid in a Mote of Omnium. The quest leaves the material available for that recipe. Use eight-salt native orders when the EV cell is free and both lapidary dynamos have ample fuel. Keep 128 salts untouched and four buckets of radon liquid for the eye bath. Once 64 further buckets are funded, use four 16-bucket charges in the existing gas caster. Chlorine can use the same mold after 64 buckets plus a separate 32-bucket process reserve are present.
