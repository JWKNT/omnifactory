# The LuV assembler's two niobium-titanium leads

The two final grid leads are Niobium-Titanium Cable. The hull itself takes Vanadium-Gallium Cable. There are three of the latter, 128 niobium ore from the miner, and no niobium-titanium in storage. One niobium ingot and one titanium ingot make two hot niobium-titanium ingots in the 4,500 K furnace. JEI gives 7,000 ticks at 120 EU/t before overclocking. The freezer cools each ingot in 281 base ticks. Two cooled ingots give four single wires in the wiremill. Wrap two for the assembler using 144 mB silicone rubber in total. Draw the other two into eight fine wires for the first sixteen Crystal Circuits.

Mill four of the existing ore in the HV machine. Each yields two crushed ores, with an additional chance of clean dust. Mill the crushed ore and centrifuge the impure dust. Niobium alone needs 2,750 K and 480 EU/t for 600 base ticks. The east Nichrome furnace can run this while the 4,500 K furnace makes HSS-G. The freezer is already connected to both. Once a cooled sample is in hand, teach each furnace its combined smelting-and-freezing recipe. ME already knows titanium ingots, and 282 rutile dust remain. The reward index has no direct payout for a niobium-titanium ingot.
