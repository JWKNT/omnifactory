# Reserve two mainframes for the HV assembler

The first EV circuit opens the Vacuum Freezer, PackagedAuto and HV Assembling Machine pages. Quest 132 requires the SMD Diodes and Second Tier Three Circuits pages, and pays two OmniQuarters. The HV assembler recipe has two circuitExtreme slots. JEI accepts a Refined Processor Mainframe in each. A first batch of two mainframes can therefore pay the quest and supply both slots. No quest in this book awards the mainframe itself.

For the pair, reserve 32 SMD resistors, 16 capacitors, 12 transistors, eight diodes, eight RAM, eight stainless steel frames, sixteen Vibrant Alloy wires and four Refined Processor Arrays. Each mainframe takes 500 ticks at 110 EU/t. The two JEI recipes differ only in their fluid. The pair takes 144 mB of soldering alloy or 288 mB of tin. Keep the polyethylene in the MV assembler until the SMD batches finish. The first 24-resistor batch falls eight short of the pair even before counting the circuits used to make the arrays.

The HV machine itself also needs two HV robot arms, two HV conveyors, the HV hull and two insulated gold cables. Keep the MV machine available after the upgrade so it can continue making SMDs.
