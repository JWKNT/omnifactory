# A short reactor job for the oven program

The Basic Chemical Reactor has a full water tank and room for hydrogen. The clay line has already supplied a stack of sodium dust. In this instance one dust, 1,000 mB water and a reusable Integrated circuit set to 1 yield three Sodium Hydroxide Dust and 1,000 mB hydrogen in 40 ticks at 8 EU/t. Quest 170 asks for one dust, pays a nickel and opens Bio Diesel and Glycerol (172). Three dust give the biodiesel branch a starter supply.

The saltwater alternative uses the Basic Electrolyzer for 720 ticks per three dust. That machine is supplying aluminium and oxygen. Sodium bisulfate and small piles of hydroxide are other JEI inputs, but the workshop already has sodium from clay. Make the first reusable program when a Basic Circuit is available above the Drive's two reserved circuits. After this batch, recover the program for the Pyrolyse Oven's first phenol charge.

The reactor completed the charge. Its output slot held three Sodium Hydroxide Dust while the other input still held the Integrated circuit with Configuration 1. All three dust and the program were recovered, and quest 170 paid one nickel. The distillery and polyethylene orders check for configuration 0 when loading their own programs. The oven and annealing orders request configuration 1.
