# A second destination for hot metal

Four hot Iridium Ingots were waiting at the west freezer while its controller ran liquid air. Each air batch there takes 143 ticks. The east freezer already runs at 2,048 V and its floor output has an active ME import bus. The furnaces share a red Ender IO item channel that passes above the fifth furnace at (40,84,−11). Six new item conduit segments can connect that channel to the east freezer. This reuses the ore-dictionary extraction covers on the furnaces.

Use a second item input bus at (44,82,−12). The first input stays on its ME interface for Draconium. Replacing that one frost-proof casing leaves eighteen in the shell, above the minimum fourteen. The LV bus has four slots. Its chest and prebuilt LV casing were available at the survey. Fit it between cooling jobs and verify the formed controller before enabling a transfer. Run the conduit east along y84 from x40 to x44, turn to z−12 and drop onto the new bus. Use red insertion with a higher priority on the east branch and leave the west chest as overflow. The first useful delivery is an ordinary hot metal entering the new bus and its cold output reaching ME. Until that happens, keep the west air-pause service working.

At 124h36m the new LV Input Bus replaced the top west casing. The controller formed again with both item inputs installed. A flight from the east aisle caught on the overhead cables. The west approach allows the bus to be placed against the neighboring top casing. The conduit branch and a cold-metal receipt are still outstanding.

At 124h54m all six item conduits were in place. The final downward connection at (44,83,−12) is set to insert on red with positive priority. The freezer is formed. The next test is to follow an ordinary hot ingot from a furnace through this inlet and verify that the cold ingot reaches ME.

At 125h06m the fourth blast furnace finished three Vanadium Ingots and one Gallium Ingot. The new top bus received four Hot Vanadium-Gallium Ingots. I watched the east controller cool the first with three waiting, saw the cold ingots pass through its output bus, and counted ME stock rising from zero to four. The west controller remained idle throughout. This verifies ordinary hot-metal routing and automatic cold return, including multiple ingots from one furnace cycle.
