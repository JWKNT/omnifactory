# Lead from the Wither Skeleton model

At 77h59m the quest book paid ten omniquarters for Hellish Mobs, page 689. The Blaze and Ghast models were already available. A Wither Skeleton Skull from the parts chest and seven Hellish Matter turned the new blank model into the third model. All three survived the quest check.

The blank took one Pulsating Mesh, four electrical steel plates, three fine gold wires and one Refined Circuit. The wiremill turns one ordinary gold wire into four fine wires. The existing materials chamber can now run the Wither Skeleton model for lead. Its first simulation request is for 24 cycles. Keep the power chamber on Shulkers when its diamond reserve needs fuel. The materials chamber and loot fabricator handle the lead separately.
