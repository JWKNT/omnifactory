# Pristine matter transfer

Three Ender IO Item Conduits now run from the north face of the Simulation Chamber at (6,82,9) to the top of the Loot Fabricator at (7,82,8). The route occupies (6,82,8), (6,83,8) and (7,83,8). The installer used the visible Ender IO port screens. The lower conduit extracts on its south face with redstone set to Always Active; its down and east ports are disabled. The middle piece has no external inventory connections. The outlet on the fabricator inserts downward. The new default was extraction with redstone Never, so merely placing the conduits would have left the machines disconnected in practice.

The fabricator only accepts pristine matter on top. Following commissioning its input held one Pristine Enderman Matter with Ender Pearl selected. The next collection returned six pearls. Ordinary Extraterrestrial Matter accumulated in the chamber. The tending script leaves pristine in the chamber for the route whenever all three pieces and the two active port settings are present. It collects ordinary matter and the fabricator's finished items. Spare pristine banked before installation can still be fed by hand when the input is free. Model switches still use the model's own output choice.

Six binder and three pulsating iron wires made eight conduits in the LV assembler. With the four older pieces and three now installed, nine remained in storage. Reserve three for the Ender IO Crafter inside the first Molecular Assembler. The two upper conduit blocks also lie on the planned AE2 route; see processing-patterns.md.

Diamond return is a separate job. The Numismatic Dynamo at (7,80,8) faces up. Its top exposes zero item slots and its four horizontal faces and bottom expose one apiece. A conduit added to the RF bundle directly above it would therefore need a branch to another face. Keep the measured diamond deliveries until there is a way to reserve diamonds for crafting while fueling the dynamo.

The first Zombie run confirmed the transfer end to end. With 16 Overworldian Matter still in the chamber, one Pristine Zombie Matter appeared in its second output slot. Two seconds later it was in the fabricator input; all 16 ordinary matter were still in their original slot. The fabricator produced 12 iron ingots. This was observed from both machines while the route was running.

The first two manual transfers put 512 Extraterrestrial Matter in the east drawer at (15,80,10). The next extension can carry this ordinary simulation output directly. From the fabricator's top conduit, run eight pieces east along y83 z8 to x15, two south to z10, and two down to the drawer. All twelve positions were air when surveyed. The only adjacent tile connections along the new path were the existing west conduit, the bundle below (8,83,8), and the drawer below the last piece. Set the last port to insert downward and disable other new external inventory ports. A drawer already containing Extraterrestrial Matter accepts only that material.

The installer is queued after the MV assembler so the first three of the nine spare conduits are available for the Ender IO Crafter. One more eight-conduit assembler batch covers the extension. scripts/matter_conduits.py sets up the route. The storage survey then compares the drawer count with the post-install count, holding manual deposits until it sees the first automatic delivery. The route has been surveyed and still needs in-game commissioning.
