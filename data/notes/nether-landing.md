# First Nether trip

Quest 780 paid a nickel for the Nether Cake. It stands at (2,80,1) in the void factory, beside the Overworld Cake. The first bite placed the player at (0,97,0) in the Nether. The platform has an obsidian landing and a netherrack shelf extending north. The southern edge drops away. The nearest surveyed glowstone was overhead at (7,109,8).

For netherrack, walk north toward (-2,97,-11). The first quarry face starts at (-3,98,-12). The diamond hammer worked horizontally, keeping the walking floor at y96. scripts/nether_rack.py recovered 42 netherrack before detecting exposed lava near the next face. Leave that face alone until surveyed again. Forty-two blocks suffice for the whole manual Shulker bootstrap, so the quarry drum can stay at the factory. The normal /home factory warm-up brought the player back from the Nether.
