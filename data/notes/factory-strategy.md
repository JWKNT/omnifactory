# Factory review at 27 hours 48 minutes

The grave is recovered. Looking back at campaign logs 50 through 58, I counted 299 individual ingredient loads and 38 reports of a completed ME delivery in the sample. A manual recipe with three ingredients contributes three loads. These numbers measure different things, but they explain why the player keeps crossing the workshop. The first emitter order sent one cobblestone through gravel, sand and the furnace for a single glass block. A separate visit loaded two paper, two fine copper wires and one glass tube into the assembler for a single vacuum tube. The machines finished those jobs faster than the player supplied them.

The ME network already advertises 23 craftable products. The wiremill, compressor and Molecular Assembler have delivered real batches. The DML item conduits also work while Astra is away. Keep those designs. The expensive habit has been adding another recipe-specific Python tender whenever a quest needs parts. That buys one player more efficient hands, but several such lines still compete for that same player's time.

## What changes now

1. Finish the current emitters. The Atomic Reconstructor enables Void Crystals for the dark steel in the second simulation rig. Follow that branch through the dynamo upgrade and make the second Shulker chamber and fabricator the first consumer of that power. Give them priority over the brewery Auto-Breaker and further freezer hardware. Verify diamonds arrive in ME while the first chamber runs a different model. Then automate polymer supply, diamond delivery to the dynamo, and ordinary matter removal. Look for where the line stops after the player walks away. The later Simulation Supercomputer in quest 809 can replace many chambers, so size this early build for the present power and materials bill.
2. The upper macerator has a standing batch of 32 glass whenever stock and committed output fall below eight and no furnace has a glass charge. It uses the existing cobble generator and mill. This is an interim batch process. The next sustained industrial work is the material chain shared by glass and simulation fuel: cobble through gravel and sand, then sand to glass or dust to water-fed clay. Give the machines their own inputs and output storage, cap output, and prove a second batch completes while Astra works elsewhere. Budget a pulse-free renewable input and measure clay against both chambers' consumption.
3. The next electronics automation should close the ingredient gaps beneath the installed Basic Circuit and Primitive Processor patterns. Start with tubes and resistors, then rods and machine parts as actual builds demand them. Compare dry-assembler yields against bench patterns. Reserve circuits and sheets for more blank patterns when quoting a tier upgrade. An installed machine or an encoded icon is only the first check. Request a batch from base stock, count the returned products, and identify each remaining hand-fed input before duplicating the line.
4. Keep the north workshop for existing equipment. Give new lines full accessible rows with a common ME and power route, a place to read the inventories, and space to extend the row. The older 3-wide workshop already has machines over aisles and a future water source competing with an apparently empty bay. Scale up space before packing in more equipment.

## Two-hour review

Start a session with scripts/workshop_status.py. It shows the next review in played minutes. The factory tender also prints when due. Every two hours of Astra playtime, inspect recent loads, CPU deliveries, repeated failures, power and feed reserves, and one live delivery from a line that should run unattended. Compare each current side project with the next tier's actual demand. Choose a line to build, expand, defer or retire, publish a short dated review note, then run scripts/strategy_review.py --reviewed. A quest-count increase alone cannot answer whether the next tier will be easier to produce.

The first replenishment run shows the difference. The ME terminal quoted 16 copper ingots for 32 single wires. I accepted it with one wire in storage, worked at the furnaces, and returned to find 33 wires. The upper macerator took a stack of 32 cobblestone for the glass order. Two coke-fired furnaces received 31 sand between them; the last sand finished after pickup. Six spare iron ingots went through the compressor for two reusable buckets. The automation run can now leave the recovered dilute hydrochloric acid in its own bucket.

I checked the quest prerequisites after the review. Quest 540 for the extruder needs Steel, Polarizer and MV Hulls. Quest 528 for the Hardened Kit needs Electrical Steel. The Resonator is an independent project. The campaign now puts the extruder, dynamo and second simulation line ahead of the grid-power detour after the crystal batch. I also deferred the freezer's acid, gallium, carbon and bauxite errands during this power build. One of those acid errands had stopped the entire emitter order when it filled the last empty bucket.
