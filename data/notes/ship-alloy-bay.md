The engine core needs an HV alloy smelter. The first planned socket would leave the smelter surrounded by other machines and cables. The new location at (10,84,-1) touches the double gold feeder on its east face. The south face has space for an ME interface directly above the existing ME cable. Its interface and first AE delivery are still pending. The interface supplier now counts this socket when ordering arms, hulls and cores.

All seven cables reached ME. After the engine order, the same smelter can make conductive iron from iron and redstone and dark steel from steel and Void Crystals. The existing MV alloy smelter keeps the electrical steel recipe.

The engine frame can use that press while the lathe turns its rods.
