# A continuous oxygen supply for windings and acid

The two-hour review counted 192,000 mB moved in oxygen containers. That is 1,600 mB per played minute across all transfers, including gas carried through more than one operation. The west blast hatch and its big iron drum were both empty when the winding bill was priced. The air separator is feeding the resin reactor and the EV electrolyzer has started recovering oxygen from tungstate. These lines can supplement a water-fed machine without consuming ore specifically for oxygen.

JEI water electrolysis takes 1,000 mB water for 2,000 mB hydrogen and 1,000 mB oxygen at 30 EU/t over 1,500 ticks. The installed HV machine at (11,85,0) measured 192 ticks at 480 EU/t for one bucket. At full power and 20 ticks per second that would supply 6,250 mB oxygen and 12,500 mB hydrogen per minute. Both output tanks cleared through the west ME Fluid Interface at (10,85,0). The first charge was a manually supplied water bucket.

Each ordinary NuclearCraft source supplies 1 mB/t. Two together would supply 2,400 mB water per minute. A compact source at 8 mB/t supplies 9,600 and can keep up with the HV machine. Its bill is eight normal sources, 48 wrought iron plates in total, reusable buckets and one bronze ingot. Set a 64,000 mB oxygen target using a north Machine Controller and an ME Fluid Level Emitter. scripts/water_stock_control.py handles construction and checks for both shutdown and restart. The permanent source and cutoff still need commissioning. For the windings, 320 buckets at the measured machine rate would take about 51 powered minutes before allowing for the acid reactor's demand.
