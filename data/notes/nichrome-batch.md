# The furnace after Kanthal

The first north blast is reserved for titanium to prove its freezer delivery and salt recovery. Nichrome can follow without changing coils. Current JEI gives five hot Nichrome from four nickel ingots and one chrome ingot at 480 EU/t for 800 ticks. The original EBF can turn chrome dust into chrome ingots alongside the north furnace. The north furnace takes the two metals directly. Each new furnace has a reason to stay in operation after a hotter one comes online.

Sixteen Nichrome coils need 128 double wires. At the current wiremill yield that is 128 ingots, or 26 blast cycles for 130 total. Budget 104 nickel ingots and 26 chrome ingots for the complete ring. The survey after Kanthal held two nickel ingots, eight nickel ore and eight chrome dust. Before buying nickel in quantity, price its full ore-processing route and the washer's by-products. The first cold ingot pays a nickel on page 158. Show the first stack to page 603 before winding the coils. Page 159 pays a quarter for sixteen coils and raises available heat to 3,600 K.

The first twenty Nichrome can use the eight nickel ore already in ME. JEI gives sixteen crushed nickel for those eight ore. The water-fed washer makes sixteen purified ore, 48 tiny cobalt and sixteen stone dust. Grinding the purified ore and centrifuging the resulting dust yields sixteen nickel dust and 48 tiny platinum. Direct smelting of the eight ore yields only eight nickel and loses both byproducts. Quest 727 describes the alternatives. The sodium persulfate bath offers iron instead of the guaranteed cobalt. The thermal centrifuge can supply the platinum, but the ordinary centrifuge is already powered and accepts the purified dust. The macerator has accepted all eight ore. The work order is in scripts/nichrome_batch.py. It books four chrome dust in the original EBF after there is wire for the interface patterns, holds the north furnace for the first titanium and then requests four Nichrome cycles.

Quest 270 explains the next useful macerator upgrade. The HV machine has three output slots, so it can keep the additional cobalt from raw nickel ore and platinum from purified nickel ore. On a large nickel run every missed byproduct is another order elsewhere. Put the upgrade on an ME outlet for all three products, beside the existing water-fed washer. Size it against the full 104-ingot nickel bill before buying ore.
