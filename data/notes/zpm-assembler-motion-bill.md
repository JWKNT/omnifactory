# Ten motors for the first ZPM assembler

The bench accepts two Crystal Processor Mainframes in the Superconductor circuit slots. JEI lists the Wetware Supercomputer as the other choice. The first of those machines enables wetware manufacturing, so reserve two crystal mainframes. Two Robot Arms, two Conveyor Modules, two single Naquadah Cables and the existing ZPM hull complete the machine.

The pack's AssemblyLine.zs gives the physical bus order and distinguishes reusable selectors. An arm uses two motors directly and a third inside its piston. A belt uses two. Building two of each therefore takes ten ZPM motors and two pistons. Across the sixteen Assembly Line runs the bill is 2,560 fine platinum wires, 44 quadruple vanadium-gallium cables, 12,000 mB lubricant, 5,760 mB solder and 5,760 mB styrene-butadiene rubber. The reusable selector goes to 2 for a piston and 1 for a belt. The belt's polymer needs a free hatch while the other hatch supplies lubricant.

The electrical bill for those runs is 491.52 million EU before cable losses. Keep the motor runs individual while the capacitors recharge. The present IV and LuV energy hatches accept 8,192 and 32,768 V respectively, two amps each. The installed feeds currently deliver one high-voltage packet each per tick through their transformers. At full load the stronger feed or second parallel supply must cover 61,440 EU/t for the pistons and belts, and 81,920 EU/t for the arms. Keep the same hatches to preserve the tested overclock schedule of the crystal line.

The 320 platinum ingots in the winding bill make the first nickel campaign a pilot. Each purchased batch of 32 platinum ores costs two quarters. Macerating gives 64 crushed platinum, another pass gives 64 impure dusts, and these smelt directly into 64 ingots. Centrifuging instead returns iridium as a by-product. Five such purchases cover all ten motors if no nickel or iridium by-products contribute. The first nickel lot already left 48 purified ore, and the workshop held 70 fine wire and four loose platinum ingots at 123h32m. Finish those before spending coins.
