# Aluminium and rutile from one MV charge

The new ME Interface needs fourteen more aluminium ingots than the current stock. Two more ingots are needed for the compressor pattern test. Ten gray terracotta remain and both the polymer line and LV electrolysis use clay. The new MV electrolyzer opens another route that also produces a titanium precursor.

Two nickels buy 32 bauxite ore. Smelting thirteen ore in an ordinary furnace gives 39 clean bauxite dust. The mill gives six crushed bauxite per ore, at the cost of hammering and washing. Use the furnace for the first charge while the mill supplies the new compressor. The MV charge takes 2,496 ticks at 60 EU/t. It returns sixteen aluminium dust, six rutile dust, 10,000 mB hydrogen and 11,000 mB oxygen. All sixteen aluminium can then go through the blast furnace. Keep the rutile for the HV tier. The request is in scripts/interface_bauxite.py.
