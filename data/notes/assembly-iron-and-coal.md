# Feeding the next casing order

The planner for the second pair of Assembly Line Casings requested iron ore, bare tungsten wire and one coal after the fine platinum wire became available. The first quarter bought five nickels. Two of those bought 32 iron ore, leaving three nickels. Carbon dust is already stocked for steel and glass fibers, but AE2 still needs ordinary coal for this order.

A Pristine Creeper Matter would give 32 coal. There is a Creeper model but no pristine drop in storage. One nickel buys the five pennies for 32 coal ore. The Multi Smelter can smelt the stack in one 32-item cycle. Use the existing smelter between orders and keep the remaining coal for phenol and electronics. For later replenishment, use stored pristine matter first when available.
