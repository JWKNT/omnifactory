# The 5,400 K furnace

Page 313 calls for sixteen HSS-G coils, pays one OmniNickel and opens Engraved Crystal Chips, Naquadah Ingots and two later high-speed steels. Keep the four completed HSS-G frames for the tier-seven mainframe. Three cold ingots remain after making those frames.

Each coil takes eight double wires. The wiremill and wire extruder both give two single wires per ingot. Cutting a pressed plate gives only one. Combining singles in the grid and winding them around a reusable wrench needs 128 ingots for sixteen coils. The bundler and assembler are alternatives when their programmed patterns are in place.

The mixer and grid both yield nine HSS-G dust from five tungstensteel, two molybdenum, one chrome and one vanadium dust. Fourteen batches would supply 126 fresh ingots. Together with the three left from the frame order that covers the coil set with one spare. Reserve 70 tungstensteel, 28 molybdenum, 14 chrome and 14 vanadium for that order, separately from the Assembly Line hardware. The east furnace makes two tungstensteel per tungsten and steel pair. At the fourth furnace's measured 1,125 ticks per HSS-G, 126 ingots take about 118 minutes before cooling. Start in batches after the tungstensteel supply catches up. Keep the existing 4,500 K furnace available for Lumium and use the new set for a dedicated furnace as the quest book recommends on page 580.
