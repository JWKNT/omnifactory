# Give clathrate precedence at the Multi Smelter

The north smelting interface at (10,83,−5) had two patterns for Pulsating Dust. One takes Uraninite Dust and the other takes Resonant Clathrate. ME’s last rejected single-dust order specifically asked for one missing Uraninite Dust. The fuel tender then made a trip to deliver clathrate directly to the same Multi Smelter’s feed chest. The MV reactor already has working input and output connections for the renewable clathrate recipe.

Keep the clathrate card in that interface and store the distinct uraninite card in ME. An emergency stock of uraninite ore can still be smelted through the feed chest. The change also frees one processing-pattern slot. The tender checks both encoded ingredients on screen so it removes the correct card, then requests a lot from stored clathrate. Check the receipt against physical dust arriving or polymer leaving the kiln; a fast consumer can empty ME before the next visit.
