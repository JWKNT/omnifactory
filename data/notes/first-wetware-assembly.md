# The first Wetware Assembly

Page 333 opens after the first Wetware Processor and the Soul Binder. The binder page is already complete. The sole JEI recipe uses the Assembly Line and gives one assembly for 400 ticks at 120,000 EU/t before overclocking. The present controller reports 81,920 V, so check the hatch layout and upgrade its input before loading any parts. Reserve 48 million EU for the measured base recipe. Page 333 pays one OmniNickel and opens the Tier Eight circuit page.

Keep two of the first four processors and a second Wetware Circuit Board. One assembly also needs six single Draconium Wires, two Frank'N'Zombies, 16 each of SMD Resistors, Thin Silicone Rubber Sheets and SMD Transistors, eight each of SMD Capacitors and SMD Diodes, four RAM and 2,000 mB sterile medium. The live wiremill and wire extruder both give two wires per cold Draconium ingot. A wire cutter takes a plate for each wire. With the lens already made, heat and freeze three more ingots and send them to the component wiremill. The last 2,000 mB per ingot of Cryotheum belongs in the east freezer. Keep the Naquadah Alloy heat ahead of new Draconium heats once its IV hatches and argon inlet are commissioned.

The Soul Binder returns its empty vial with each Frank'N'Zombie. Query JEI with the zombie NBT to see the Peaceful bench recipe. Four Zombie Heads around an empty vial produce one filled vial. Each splicer operation needs another head. Eleven heads and one empty vial were on hand, enough for both bindings. The Loot Fabricator gives six heads per Pristine Zombie Matter if more are needed. One Slice'N'Splice operation uses two Soularium, a head, two wafers and redstone. It accepts ordinary, glowstone-doped or Naquadah-doped wafers. The bench alternative needs four Soularium, a Primitive Processor and a Red Alloy Block. Build the Slice'N'Splice for page 512 after the two furnace hatches have their Tungstensteel Plates. It pays a quarter. Neither Draconium Wire nor Frank'N'Zombie appeared as a quest payout.

The live twelve-slice Assembly Line preview shows an energy hatch at layout coordinate (1,3,4), the solid steel casing at (24,85,-19) in this build. That casing is directly south of the new four-amp LuV converter. Stage a third input hatch from one LuV hull and one single Vanadium-Gallium Cable. With the second LuV hatch accepting two amps, its rated contribution is 65,536 EU/t beyond the present 81,920. The converter can then select the egg reactor to its north, the ZPM transformer to its east, or the new line hatch to its south. Keep the hatch loose until the first Assembly recipe is ready. An installed hatch changes the controller's overclocking for existing jobs. Confirm formation, actual capacitor discharge rates and the 48-million-EU budget before submitting the assembly.

The splicer tender now stages its components during the capacitor recharge. One Industrial Machine Chassis was on hand. It takes eight Soul Powder to upgrade it. Two further Soularium ingots are sufficient for one doped-wafer operation. Grind Soularium in the macerator for the powder. Press four bones for the skeleton skull. The tender leaves the tungstensteel plates available to the furnace until both IV hatches exist.

JEI gives one controller with ordinary wafers, two with glowstone-doped wafers and four with Naquadah-doped wafers. The survey counted zero ordinary wafers, 20 glowstone-doped and 58 Naquadah-doped. Use two Naquadah-doped wafers for four controllers. The first two bindings take eight more heads and return their vials. See [silicone sheet casting](wetware-silicone-sheets.md) for the passive components.

The first filled Zombie Soul Vial was crafted from four heads and one empty vial. The player inventory showed the exact minecraft:zombie tag, and the first-vial receipt is filed. Keep it for the Soul Binder.
