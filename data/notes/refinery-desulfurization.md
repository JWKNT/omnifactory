It also returns the desulfurized fraction at its input volume. The inputs per batch are eight buckets of sulfuric heavy fuel, twelve of sulfuric light fuel, twelve of sulfuric naphtha or sixteen of sulfuric gas.

Ten tower charges made from thirty buckets of ordinary oil should fund one batch of every recipe.
