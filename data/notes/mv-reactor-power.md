# Power and motors for renewable polymer

The next two workshop goals after the diamond dynamo are the LV Polarizer and an independent MV feeder. Quest 147 accepts the LV Polarizer. The book says its recipes all fit LV, and the installed JEI confirms that a steel rod in it makes a magnetic steel rod for MV motors. The recipe takes an LV hull, four double tin wires, two insulated tin cables and two iron rods. Quest 147 paid its nickel. The installed polarizer at (4,81,3) opposite the mixer holds 320 EU and connects at 32 V.

The four-amp MV CEF is gregtech:machine@10669. It needs an MV hull, one Primitive Processor, four Energetic Alloy Energy Conduits, two quadruple Energetic Alloy Wires and a chest. The other CEF recipe substitutes sixteenfold wires for the quadruple wires. After fitting three of the first four conduits to the RF branch, use one additional energetic ingot for the second conduit batch and four more energetic ingots for the eight single wires that make the two quadruple wires. Reserve five new energetic ingots for that CEF. Keep its 128 V output on its own circuit.

The MV Chemical Reactor (quest 227) requires its own MV hull, two Primitive Processors, two single copper cables, two glass, a bronze rotor and an MV motor. The bench motor takes two aluminium rods, a magnetic steel rod, four double copper wires and two single copper cables. The two hulls need eighteen aluminium plate equivalents altogether. The workshop held twelve ingots and another 25 dust. The EBF received sixteen dust and delivered the first eight ingots. Reserve two aluminium rods for the motor. Clay electrolysis can now be sized for silicon. Reserve two separate hulls and reactor parts before spending on another MV machine. The LV Fluid Extractor required for Molten Ender is a third machine, with an LV hull, two Basic Circuits, an LV piston, an LV pump, two tin cables and two glass.
