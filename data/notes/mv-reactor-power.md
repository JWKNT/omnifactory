# Power and motors for renewable polymer

The next two workshop goals after the diamond dynamo are the LV Polarizer and an independent MV feeder. Quest 147 accepts the LV Polarizer. The book says its recipes all fit LV, and the installed JEI confirms that a steel rod in it makes a magnetic steel rod for MV motors. The recipe takes an LV hull, four double tin wires, two insulated tin cables and two iron rods. The space at (4,81,3) opposite the mixer was empty on survey and touches the LV trunk. scripts/install_polarizer.py targets that space. Claim 147 while carrying the machine.

The four-amp MV CEF is gregtech:machine@10669. It needs an MV hull, one Primitive Processor, four Energetic Alloy Energy Conduits, two quadruple Energetic Alloy Wires and a chest. The other CEF recipe substitutes sixteenfold wires for the quadruple wires. After fitting three of the first four conduits to the RF branch, use one additional energetic ingot for the second conduit batch and four more energetic ingots for the eight single wires that make the two quadruple wires. Reserve five new energetic ingots for that CEF. Keep its 128 V output on its own circuit.

The MV Chemical Reactor (quest 227) requires its own MV hull, two Primitive Processors, two single copper cables, two glass, a bronze rotor and an MV motor. The bench motor takes two aluminium rods, a magnetic steel rod, four double copper wires and two single copper cables. Nine aluminium plate equivalents per hull means the feeder and reactor together exceed the remaining twelve aluminium ingots. The LV electrolyzer's clay-dust recipe supplies additional aluminium dust, then the EBF smelts it. Reserve two separate hulls and reactor parts before spending on another MV machine. The LV Fluid Extractor required for Molten Ender is a third machine, with an LV hull, two Basic Circuits, an LV piston, an LV pump, two tin cables and two glass. See polymer-clay.md for the actual clathrate recipe.
