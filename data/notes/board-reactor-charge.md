# Restarting the roof board reactor under load

At 126h37m the shared RF grid delivered 15,692 RF/t with both lapidaries running. The roof reactor still fell back to tick one of a 100-tick, 480 EU/t board. Its HV converter's basic capacitor held 468 RF. The fifth furnace converter held a full 10,000,000 RF basic capacitor and a full 40,000,000 RF hardened capacitor. The refinery turbine can supply the grid once the fuel trial succeeds.

Exchange the two basic capacitors once. The fifth furnace retains its hardened capacitor throughout and gets the depleted basic one back for charging. Move the full one first so the HV converter stays online. A full basic capacitor carries 2.5 million EU, enough for the 48,000 EU board and the other HV machines. Compare the charge of both items in the machine slots during the exchange. Check for a completed board after power returns.

The swap completed at 126h38m. The receiver held 8,500,244 RF and the returned capacitor had already recharged to 240,000 RF. The board reactor reached tick 98 and all three multilayer boards then appeared in stock. The component wiremill finished the second motor's fine platinum winding order during the same interval.

At 127h45m the west furnace received a basic capacitor from the same donor. The west converter held 10,000,000 RF in its battery and the returned basic held 1,550,768 RF at the fifth furnace. The west furnace finished its queued heat and its controller recharged to 33,280 EU. The fourth furnace also finished the Lumium heat.
