# Four outputs from the first oil

Quest 184 introduces oil. The quest page paid its nickel. Quest 160 then asks for a bucket each of sulfuric heavy fuel, light fuel, naphtha and gas. The quest book lists finite overworld wells, Oilsands Ore and the renewable Oil Drilling Rig as sources. Keep the operating biomass distillery on ethanol for the first plastics order.

Live JEI lists a single-block LV distillery recipe for each fraction of ordinary Oil. Each 50 mB of Oil produces 15, 50, 20 or 60 mB of the selected fraction on circuits 0 through 3 respectively. Each cycle takes 40 ticks at 24 EU/t. Reaching one bucket of each takes at least 7,700 mB of ordinary Oil across 154 small cycles.

The MV Distillation Tower takes 3,000 mB of the same Oil per 1,200 ticks at 96 EU/t and delivers all four fractions together. Two batches produce 1,800 mB heavy fuel, 6,000 mB light fuel, 2,400 mB naphtha and 7,200 mB gas. Plan four separate output tanks before commissioning the tower. Leave room for the desulfurization machines beside it.

The next major build after the first EV hull is the tower and epoxy supply. Page 526 also requires the tier-five mainframe on page 192. Pages 185, 188 and 191 were already claimed on the earlier epoxy pilot. Its six boards were used. Rebuilding a steady epoxy supply restores the four-per-batch Microcircuit recipe, and page 520 is open. A four-output tower needs five layers, 34 Clean Stainless Steel Casings, four fluid outlets, and clearance for further slices. Twelve casing crafts give 36 casings for 72 plates and twelve frames. The controller separately needs four large stainless pipes, two HV pumps, two advanced circuits and an MV hull. Page 154 pays one quarter and checks components together before placement.

Start a 72-ingot stainless reserve alongside the EV hardware. Four pyrolusite ore yield 24 crushed for 72 tiny manganese dust at the water-fed MV washer. Washed crushed iron supplies tiny nickel at the same rate. Keep both purified ores for the next stage. Eight complete dust of each metal, eight chrome and 48 iron give 72 stainless dust. The west furnace can process two loads of 36 while the north furnace remains available. scripts/tower_stainless.py records each charge.

The builder’s wand extended the north floor to z = −15. Ground under all nine tower positions is verified. The washer supplied the manganese and nickel. Eight full dust of each, eight chrome and 48 iron made 72 stainless dust. The west furnace took the first 36 at 120 EU/t, and its output bus yielded the first two ingots. While it ran, the north furnace took 36 aluminium dust at 480 EU/t for the cable assembler’s arms and conveyors.

The north furnace finished all 36 aluminium ingots. Three collections returned eleven, ten and fifteen. It immediately took the other 36 stainless dust, overlapping the original west furnace charge. The north controller measured 215 ticks per stainless at 480 EU/t and the west measured 600 ticks at 120 EU/t. Hot metal still has the separate automated path to the freezer. Collect ordinary stainless from each output bus. See run/tower-stainless for the active charges.

The first 72 stainless cover the casing plates alone. The next reserve is 54 for the frames, controller, large pipes and HV pump parts. Keep 24 ingots for the four extruded pipes and four plates for the wrapper’s advanced circuits. The cutting saw takes blocks in batches of nine plates. Three frame operations use fifteen rods and nine plates for twelve frames. See refinery-feed.md for the 30-bucket pilot and the renewable alternative.
