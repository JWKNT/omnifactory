# Four outputs from the first oil

Quest 184 introduces oil. The quest page paid its nickel. Quest 160 then asks for a bucket each of sulfuric heavy fuel, light fuel, naphtha and gas. The quest book lists finite overworld wells, Oilsands Ore and the renewable Oil Drilling Rig as sources. Keep the operating biomass distillery on ethanol for the first plastics order.

Live JEI lists a single-block LV distillery recipe for each fraction of ordinary Oil. Each 50 mB of Oil produces 15, 50, 20 or 60 mB of the selected fraction on circuits 0 through 3 respectively. Each cycle takes 40 ticks at 24 EU/t. Reaching one bucket of each takes at least 7,700 mB of ordinary Oil across 154 small cycles.

The MV Distillation Tower takes 3,000 mB of the same Oil per 1,200 ticks at 96 EU/t and delivers all four fractions together. Two batches produce 1,800 mB heavy fuel, 6,000 mB light fuel, 2,400 mB naphtha and 7,200 mB gas. Plan four separate output tanks before commissioning the tower. Leave room for the desulfurization machines beside it.

The next major build after the first EV hull is the tower and epoxy supply. Page 526 also requires the tier-five mainframe on page 192. Pages 185, 188 and 191 were already claimed on the earlier epoxy pilot. Its six boards were used. Rebuilding a steady epoxy supply restores the four-per-batch Microcircuit recipe, and page 520 is open. A four-output tower needs five layers, 34 Clean Stainless Steel Casings, four fluid outlets, and clearance for further slices. Twelve casing crafts give 36 casings for 72 plates and twelve frames. The controller separately needs four large stainless pipes, two HV pumps, two advanced circuits and an MV hull. Page 154 pays one quarter and checks components together before placement.

Start a 72-ingot stainless reserve alongside the EV hardware. Four pyrolusite ore yield 24 crushed for 72 tiny manganese dust at the water-fed MV washer. Washed crushed iron supplies tiny nickel at the same rate. Keep both purified ores for the next stage. Eight complete dust of each metal, eight chrome and 48 iron give 72 stainless dust. The west furnace can process two loads of 36 while the north furnace remains available. scripts/tower_stainless.py records each charge.

The builder’s wand extended the north floor to z = −15. Ground under all nine tower positions is verified. The washer supplied the manganese and nickel. Eight full dust of each, eight chrome and 48 iron made 72 stainless dust. The west furnace took the first 36 at 120 EU/t, and its output bus yielded the first two ingots. While it ran, the north furnace took 36 aluminium dust at 480 EU/t for the cable assembler’s arms and conveyors.

The north furnace finished all 36 aluminium ingots. Three collections returned eleven, ten and fifteen. It immediately took the other 36 stainless dust, overlapping the original west furnace charge. The north controller measured 215 ticks per stainless at 480 EU/t and the west measured 600 ticks at 120 EU/t. Hot metal still has the separate automated path to the freezer. Collect ordinary stainless from each output bus. See run/tower-stainless for the active charges.

The first 72 stainless cover the casing plates alone. The next reserve is 54 for the frames, controller, large pipes and HV pump parts. Keep 24 ingots for the four extruded pipes and four plates for the wrapper’s advanced circuits. The cutting saw takes blocks in batches of nine plates. Three frame operations use fifteen rods and nine plates for twelve frames. See refinery-feed.md for the 30-bucket pilot and the renewable alternative.

Page 154 checks the controller, MV energy hatch, LV output bus, LV fluid input, two LV fluid outputs and twenty clean casings before placement. The four-fraction build takes four fluid outputs. The pack's quest rewards supply none of these machines. Queue six LV hulls for one bus and five fluid hatches, twelve insulated tin cables in all. The EV cable assembler uses 36 mB silicone per tin wire and gives the tower's twelve priority when the EV hull is complete. Plan the base of the tower at y82, leaving two blocks above the void deck for access to the central bottom feed. Four large stainless pipes have a single JEI route, six ingots each at the MV extruder. Its large die is already in ME.

The tower's two HV pumps have three assembly-machine recipes at 480 EU/t, consuming 144 mB rubber, 108 mB styrene-butadiene or 72 mB silicone each. The dedicated silicone wrapper is MV and the HV assembler has solder for electronics. For this pair use the bench route and four rubber rings from one extrusion. The campaign can reuse the freezer pump tooling for two steel rotors, two screws and two medium stainless pipes. The four large controller pipes are already made. After the hatches, the next campaign invocation orders the controller and brings the whole set to quest page 154 before placement.

The cutter took seven stainless blocks for the first 63 casing plates while the west furnace processed vibrant alloy for patterns. Each block produces nine plates at 120 EU/t in 315 ticks. The controller pipes were already in storage before loading the cutter.

The second cutter charge delivered 27 more stainless plates. Seven ingots on the lathe gave fourteen rods, joining the single rod in storage. Fifteen rods and nine plates made twelve frames. Twelve frames and 72 plates produced 36 Clean Stainless Steel Casings. The five-layer shell needs 34. Keep the whole stack available for the quest until the controller and hatches are present.

The four-output shell layout is in scripts/refinery_shell.py. Comparing all positions with JEI's sample gives 34 clean casings for the five-layer tower, including a solid cap. The controller faces west at (12,82,−13). The central input remains accessible underneath. The energy hatch is on the southwest corner near the RF branch, and the four fluid outlets are stacked at (12,83..86,−14). Construction follows the component check on page 154. The actual formation check is run/refinery-shell-formed.

The workshop crafted one LV output bus, one fluid input hatch and four fluid output hatches together. Six LV hulls took 66 wrought iron plates and twelve insulated tin cables. The ME compressor processed plate orders in lots of 24 to fit the 1k CPUs. The separate LV wrapper used renewable rubber sheets while the MV assembler kept its silicone for pumps. The MV extruder also delivered the two medium stainless pipes. The two HV pumps share a batch of six gold cables, four for their motors and two for final assembly. The north blast furnace delivered 36 aluminium ingots. The compressor and MV polyethylene assembler supplied the hull for the MV energy hatch, which is now in storage. A second MV casing is crafted for the controller. Eight bundles of four copper wires, four gold cables, four stainless rods and two magnetic steel rods made both HV motors. The first steel rotor charge ran in the solder-fed HV assembler while ME worked on steel and cable orders. The finished pumps, circuits and controller are outstanding.

The first HV pump is crafted. The diamond lens engraved one of the sixteen silicon wafers into a RAM wafer. The water saw returned 32 RAM, four times the requirement for the controller's two arrays. ME supplied eight Refined Processors and made the first Refined Processor Array. The second needs another transistor batch. The workshop consolidated 36 tiny piles saved from gallium processing into four dust, enough for four plates and 128 SMD transistors. The second steel rotor is in stock and the rubber bar for the remaining two pump rings has finished smelting.

Both HV pumps and arrays went into the controller with one MV hull and four large stainless pipes. Page 154 completed and paid its OmniQuarter. The book only requires two fluid outputs and twenty casings, but the construction inventory covers the full four-fraction tower. The player has started the permanent east access stair. The reserve of 30 buckets of oil is still in the workshop electrolyzer.

The five-layer tower is formed. The live controller at (12,82,-13) reports west and formed, and run/refinery-shell-formed records the check. The east stair climbs from (15,80,-9) to a standing height of 86 above (15,85,-14). During construction the player stepped into the hollow center. The normal factory home returned the player to the workshop. Building each remaining upper block from the stair completed the cap. Each of the four outlet hatches can push fluid through its front into an adjacent iron drum.

At 43h the east-facing four-amp MV CEF powered the west-facing hatch directly. The first 16-bucket shipment started the tower at 96 EU/t for 1,200 ticks. The second 14-bucket shipment went in a portable tank at (13,81,-13). Turning the input hatch down made it draw oil automatically, freeing the player to handle the outlets.

The four 256-bucket iron drums stand at (12,83..86,-15). Bottom to top they receive sulfuric heavy fuel, light fuel, naphtha and gas. The first charge measured 900, 3,000, 1,200 and 3,600 mB respectively. Each hatch pushes north into its own drum. Two wand-built rows extended the floor to z=-17 so the lower drums are reachable from the north side. Page 160 recognized all four filled buckets and paid a nickel. It left all four samples intact. The iron wand finished its durability on the second row.
