# Automating the miner tables after the first osmium cargo

Build the first tier-four miner by hand in the 7×7 table. Its osmium and iridium open the powered extended crafters described on page 406. For repeat ore and gem expeditions, give the 5×5 and 7×7 crafters their own three-block-high columns with ME access. Each column has the matching extended Package Crafter at the bottom, an Unpackager above it, then a Packager. Keep the existing tables available while testing the first repeat order.

Page 408 pays a quarter for a Packager, Unpackager, two Recipe Holders and a Package Recipe Encoder. The encoder itself consumes another two holders. A fresh holder costs one Crystaltine block, three blank patterns, two quartz glass, two titanium plates and an ME Packaging Component. JEI also shows holder conversion recipes that begin with an existing holder. Budget four fresh holders for the first matched pair plus encoder. The two package machines each take a Mechanical Crafter, four titanium plates, two Extreme circuits, a packaging component and the appropriate ME item bus. Bank the first mission cargo before assigning it to automation.

One identically encoded holder goes in each machine. Each holds twenty recipes. Start with the repeated miner and its lasers, and verify the finished ship returns to ME. For future Assembly Line orders with ten to sixteen ingredients, page 813 offers an Extended Processing Pattern Terminal. It handles sixteen ingredients and six outputs directly. Keep the working nine-input arm pattern in place.

The 9×9 table comes before the powered extended-crafter page. Its 7×7 recipe uses four emerald blocks, twenty black steel plates, four Conflux Catalysts, sixteen Ultimate Components, four Crystaltine Components and an elite table. A Conflux Catalyst combines one of each of the five preceding catalysts. Each one therefore embeds another four Ultimate and four Crystaltine Components. The full table needs **32 Ultimate Components** and **20 Crystaltine Components** before counting a spare for display. The Ultimate components need 32 emerald plates, 32 Uranium 235 Plates and 32 Luminessence. Keep ten separate emerald plates for the first crystal chips.

The twenty Crystaltine Components consume twenty Osmiridium Plates and twenty Crystaltine Ingots. Five alloy heats cost fifteen iridium, five osmium and five buckets of helium. The twenty Crystaltine Ingots alone require 160 diamonds, 200 lapis, 80 Nether Star Nuggets, 80 iron and 40 gold. JEI's other ingot recipes unpack stored blocks or nuggets. Hold the 9×9 order until the gemstone flight and reserve its diamonds separately from the power dynamos. The Package Recipe Holders add their own Crystaltine bill. Page 389 pays a dollar. The first physical checkpoints are the component and catalyst on pages 388 and 387, each paying a nickel.

The ore line now has enough by-product to try the first Ultimate Component. Reserve one full dust for the fuel quest. Smelt surplus into GregTech ingots and use a reusable plate die in the MV extruder. Its 64 EU/t recipe takes one ingot per plate. The hammer costs two, and JEI lists no GregTech compressor recipe for this plate. Make the die from four steel plates if necessary. Combine each plate with one emerald plate, one black steel plate and one Luminessence. Show the first component on page 388 before spending four on the first Ultimate Catalyst for page 387. The factory job is scripts/ultimate_table_parts.py.

At 134h30m two Ultimate Components were in stock, and the quest book confirmed page 388 complete and its OmniNickel claimed. Each used a Black Steel Plate, Luminessence, Uranium 235 Plate and Emerald Plate. Page 387 is now open. Its Ultimate Catalyst needs four components and one more Black Steel Plate. To finish from the current two components, make three Black Steel Plates and two more Uranium 235 Plates. The two loose Uranium 235 Dust and nine separate NuclearCraft uranium items leave the earlier fuel reserve intact. Nine Luminessence and eight Emerald Plates were available after the first pair.

At 135h03m the MV extruder returned the last two Uranium 235 Plates. The next two Ultimate Components and a fifth Black Steel Plate went into the first Ultimate Catalyst. Page 387 paid an OmniNickel. The catalyst remains available for a Conflux Catalyst. The 9×9 table still depends on the larger crystal and gemstone reserves above.
