# Titanium for the second miner

Six Titanium Heavy Plating, three Electrical Steel Thrusters, a Basic Guidance System, two Basic Mining Lasers, two LV Field Generators, a Micro Miner Engine Core, an Advanced Diesel Generator and a Stainless Steel Chest go into the tier-two ship on the 5×5 extended table. Page 10 describes filling the table from JEI and prestocking the components. It opens Indium, Radon and the optional space-station branch. The first ship consumed its own lasers and guidance. The new assembler patterns will serve both classes.

Page 245 pays a nickel for one plating. JEI has one route, two titanium plates for one heavy plating at 500 EU/t for 200 ticks. The whole ship therefore costs twelve titanium plates in its plating alone. Nine titanium plates were on hand during the first lunar run. The Basic Compressor for the starting line cannot supply this voltage. The HV compressor requires an HV hull, two HV pistons, two gold cables and two advanced circuits, filled by Nanoprocessors. The EV version needs two EV pistons and two higher-tier circuits and could overclock the same job. The six HV runs would take one minute of machine time. Compare the new press with the hull and motor orders for the other HV reactors before committing. Keep the installed low-voltage press on continuous plate work.
