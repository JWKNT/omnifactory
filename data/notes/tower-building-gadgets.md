# Building the tower columns

The live tower layout has four pairs of solid steel columns. Above the base these run in the same eight positions from level 1 through level 10. A Building Gadget can place casings from the player's inventory in a vertical column. Test its preview against one column before repeating it on the other seven. The controller, hatches and four-block cap need individual placement.

Page 755 asks for the Building, Exchanging, Copy-Paste and Destruction Gadgets. It offers no item reward. Their grid recipes together use sixteen iron, seven redstone, five lapis, four diamonds, two emeralds and two ender pearls. The existing jetpack and diamond reserve cover access and power. Smelt the spare emerald ore to keep one emerald for the HV emitter. Hold the four fresh gadgets together for the page before configuring or charging the Building Gadget.
