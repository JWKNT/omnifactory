# The first workshop disk

The ME Chest is at (10,80,9), directly below its glass cable. The terminal on the north face opens from (10,80,7). A single 1k item cell occupies chest slot zero. Its first test deposited nine marble, reopened the terminal, and withdrew all nine. Keep portable inventories and other storage cells out of this disk.

The first family stored digitally is GregTech meta_item_2, including electronic components and silicon boules. scripts/stock_void.py shift-clicks these stacks into the terminal before sorting the rest into the physical stores. Its first electronics pass stored six fine tin wire, two fine copper wire, two diodes and one silicon boule. scripts/stores.py lists digital holdings as “network holds” alongside its ordinary chest records. scripts/picklist.py can withdraw exact amounts from the same terminal, so the order calculator can use digital parts. During an order its current listing tells the player which chest to try first. The ordinary parts chest still handles overflow. Leave the bulk building materials in physical stock until there is more cell space.
