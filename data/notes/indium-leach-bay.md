# Separate the two indium reactions

A pair of washed galena and sphalerite takes four buckets of sulfuric acid and yields one bucket of indium concentrate at 150 EU/t. Four aluminium dust then reduce that bucket at 600 EU/t and yield one tiny indium pile. The existing EV reactor runs both recipes and makes QBit wafers with radon. The output-to-input transfers and changes of liquid leave it idle. The remaining casing orders alone call for 77 QBits before using what's already on the shelf.

The new EV transformer at (19,83,−10) will take 2,048 V from the south and supply 512 V north into the new reactor. The transformer in this instance only outputs on the opposite face to its input.
