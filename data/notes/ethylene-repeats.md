# Scaling the ethanol route after the first plastic sheets

Save the diluted sulfuric acid from the MV reactor. Each bucket of ethanol consumes a bucket of acid and produces a bucket each of ethylene and diluted acid. At three buckets of diluted acid, an LV distillery on circuit 0 recovers two buckets of sulfuric acid in 1,200 ticks at 30 EU/t. A distillation tower also returns the water, at 120 EU/t for 600 ticks. Leave the ethanol still connected to the brewery for normal operation. Site a second still when the first 3,000 mB of diluted acid accumulates.

A later three-bucket ethylene charge supports 20 oxygen-fed polymer reactions, yielding 4,320 mB polyethylene, enough for 30 sheets, and leaving 120 mB ethylene. Budget 20 buckets of oxygen, three buckets of ethanol and one fresh bucket of acid per steady-state charge once the recovery still is working. Start the first charge with all three buckets of acid. This is a later production target. The first nine sheets still go toward AE2 and its prerequisites.

Live JEI gives both rubber saplings and sugar cane 100 mB biomass each in the brewery. The still produces 700 mB ethanol per 1,000 mB biomass. Fifty surplus crops therefore cover a three-bucket ethanol charge, with 500 mB left over. The brewery tender can now use the more abundant crop when its input is empty. It preserves twenty saplings for the grove and sixteen sugar cane for other workshop use.

After the sulfur bounty, the water-fed reactor held 58,880 mB water and 7,000 mB hydrogen. Precharge three more buckets of sulfuric acid there for the next ethanol batch. This costs three sulfur and 12,000 mB water and adds 18,000 mB hydrogen. Give the shared reactor this order when the polymer line has at least 24 simulations’ worth of clay or polymer in hand, in storage or in the chamber. The first acid bucket in storage stays available for commissioning the separate ethylene reactor.

The repeat charge finished while the reactor hardware was still on the bench. The water-fed reactor's output tanks showed 3,000 mB sulfuric acid and 25,000 mB hydrogen. The first acid bucket is still separate in storage.
