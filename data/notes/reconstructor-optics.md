# Optical parts and the first Grid Power mill

The Aluminium Casing from page 74 goes into the Atomic Reconstructor. Its recipe also takes five lead plates, two MV emitters and one Endereye Lens. The bench recipe for each emitter takes four electrum rods, two MV circuits, one nether quartz and two insulated copper cables. The 120 EU/t assembler variant takes one cable and a circuit selector per emitter. Reserve the six ingots of electrum for the three HV converter and arm arrays first.

Use one of the 28 pearls and one of the two blaze powders in the LV chemical reactor. In 100 ticks at 16 EU/t this makes an Eye of Ender. The other JEI routes need an Implosion Compressor. The LV autoclave turns the eye and 1,000 mB water into the lens in 800 ticks. Neither the eye nor this lens is paid by a quest. Page 107 pays a nickel for the completed machine and opens the crystal, Canola Press and Nether branches.

The same autoclave can make the Manual Mill’s two Polished Stone from two ordinary stone and 500 mB water. The mill also needs a Redstone Gear and a Resonating Redstone Crystal. Once the reconstructor works, make Restonia from redstone. Use four Restonia and a Conductive Iron Gear for the Redstone Gear. A Thermal Compactor with a gear die can instead press four Resonating Redstone Crystals. The LV alloy smelter makes a resonating crystal from an Ender Shard and either a Restonia or a full redstone block. There are six Ender Shards in ME. Keep the Machine Block from page 75 for the Resonator. Pages 114 and 229 each pay a nickel, followed by the Resonator on page 113. These machines are planned.

The LV reactor finished the Eye of Ender from one pearl and one blaze powder. The autoclave finished both Polished Stone, and both were collected. The autoclave also finished the Endereye Lens. The storage survey found it in ME. The remaining reconstructor parts are the five lead plates and two MV emitters.

The four galena ore from camp went into the macerator for the HV buffer. Once those three lead ingots and the stored ingot have supplied its two lead, the reconstructor still needs three more. Two nickels buy 32 galena ore. Double and wash four, then separate the eight dust into three lead, three silver and two sulfur. Save the other 28 ore. Plain lead ore costs three nickels per 32, and the unlocked cave is still an option when travelling through camp. One Pristine Wither Skeleton Matter would give twelve ingots, but there is no running model for it. The reconstructor-lead order waits for the buffer before touching its materials.

The three Processor Assemblies consume nine of the twelve planned Microcircuits. Use the three spare Microcircuits and one Primitive Processor across the four MV circuit slots for the two emitters. The JEI list also accepts Electronic and Refined Processors. Basic Circuits do not satisfy these slots. The workshop already has a pattern for the Primitive Processor. The polymer clay supply has bought 32 nether quartz ore and put sixteen in the furnaces. Reserve two quartz for the emitters. The queued reconstructor craft follows the titanium reactor, so it can use the actual leftover circuits.

The terminal delivered the missing Primitive Processor. That and the three spare Microcircuits made both MV emitters, which went into the Atomic Reconstructor. Pages 107 and 233 each paid one OmniNickel. The machine is installed at (9,82,5), facing east. Standing on the floor too close to the machine pointed it down. A temporary support underneath the socket and a one-block step at (12,80,5) allowed placement from the east. The lever at (9,82,6) inhibits the beam when powered. Place a block at (11,82,5) against the permanent cobblestone backstop at (12,82,5), toggle the lever, then collect the converted block. Iron made the Enori block and coal made nine Void Crystals. Page 112 paid its OmniNickel. A second coal block supplied another nine Void Crystals for the simulation hulls.
