# Two Basic Quantum Chests for the Tier Six miner

The miner recipe uses two Basic Quantum Chests. Each chest needs an MV hull, three aluminium plates, four Good circuits and one MV field generator. The generator takes four more Good circuits, four Energetic Alloy wires and an Eye of Ender. Its assembler alternative replaces those wires with 288 mB of molten Energetic Alloy. The workshop already has eight wires and three eyes, so use the crafting table.

Sixteen Microcircuits cover both chests and both generators. The installed IV SoC recipe returns eight Microcircuits per Epoxy Circuit Board, one SoC and two Fine Red Alloy Wires. At 132h18m there were 21 SoCs, 76 fine wires and no boards. The older four-output recipe also spends transistors, capacitors and resistors. Use the two-board route.

The crop reactor already holds a bucket of glycerol. The resin reactor has 10,600 mB naphtha and 4,000 mB nitrogen dioxide but only 24 mB epichlorohydrin. Send one bucket of hydrochloric acid to the glycerol reaction. When both the board and cable lines need acid, make an extra bucket in the HV reactor. Draw from the silicone reactor once it has at least three buckets and leave two for its next silicon charge. The foil coater already holds 750 mB sulfuric acid. Verify the crop reagent and cast epoxy sheets before committing the circuit order.

At 132h36m the player was carrying a normal bucket of epoxy resin. The plate caster already had its mold and 136 mB of resin, eight short of the first sheet. Its feeder had only checked for a portable tank. One 1,000 mB bucket can pay for seven sheets at 144 mB and still leave some resin in the caster. Use the carried bucket while the glycerol line waits for acid. The reinforced-board bath held 296 mB and had no fibers queued.

The caster consumed 1,008 mB of resin, leaving 128 mB. ME subsequently held four finished Epoxy Circuit Boards. The IV assembler was idle with 960 mB soldering alloy. The two eight-output microcircuit charges use 144 mB total. If the five crafting CPUs are occupied, the same three ingredient stacks can go directly into that machine. Its regular processing pattern remains the first route.

At 132h49m ME held all 16 Microcircuits and two remaining boards. The IV assembler's solder fell from 960 to 816 mB. Both MV field generators are funded by those circuits, the eight wires and the eyes. One MV hull and five aluminium plates are on hand. The missing hull needs an eight-plate casing. The table route also spends one aluminium plate, two wrought iron plates and two single copper cables. All wrought iron and cable inputs are already available. With the six chest plates included, the remaining ten aluminium plates can come from 21 stored ingots. Use the native plate order if possible; the basic compressor can press an otherwise idle lot while all five CPUs are occupied.
