# Cooling the HSS-G campaign

The west vacuum freezer accepts both hot ingots and air. Liquid air can occupy it continuously while ten hot ingots sit in its item bus. Pausing the air export and banking the remaining inlet air allowed five more HSS-G ingots to reach storage, taking the balance from eight to thirteen. The freezer also handles the tungsten and tungstensteel for the same production chain. At least 115 more cold HSS-G ingots are needed for the next coil set.

The first response is to drain the inlet only when metal is waiting and keep the air filter off until the input bus clears. Watch the actual cold returns and the liquid-air balance. If metal continues queuing while the distillation tower also needs air, fit a second freezer. A hot-only unit beside the fifth furnace can use the existing furnace hot-feed and cold-return conduit colors. Check the multiblock's minimum casing count and allowed hatches before ordering materials. The original unit took twenty frost-proof walls, two item buses, two fluid hatches, an energy hatch and its controller. A second build also needs power under load. The two EV furnaces already exceed the present dynamos' combined rating.
