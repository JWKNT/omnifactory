# Circuits for the two LuV robot arms

The arms together need twelve basic Quantumprocessors, four upgraded Quantum Processors and four Quantum Processor Arrays. The two finished mainframes are reserved for the final assembler.

At 95h30m storage had one basic processor and three upgrades. Including the circuits inside the arrays, that leaves seventeen upgrades and 62 basics to produce. Sixteen four-output runs cover the basic deficit. The circuit bill includes 50 QBit CPUs, sixteen Nano CPUs and 37 multilayer boards. The four arrays also need sixteen Power ICs, sixteen single Signalum wires, eight chrome plates and four Improved Ender Eyes.

Two engraved Power IC wafers are already available. The EV cutter takes 22 mB lubricant per wafer and gives four Power ICs. Four wafers cover the arrays. Keep the cutter supplied while the Assembly Line makes motors. The remaining Signalum wire shares furnace time with HSS-G and Lumium. Aluminium and stainless steel can move to the west furnace to clear time on the hotter coils.
