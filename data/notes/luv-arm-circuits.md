# Circuits for the two LuV robot arms

The arms together need twelve basic Quantumprocessors, four upgraded Quantum Processors and four Quantum Processor Arrays. The two finished mainframes are reserved for the final assembler.

At 95h30m storage had one basic processor and three upgrades. Including the circuits inside the arrays, that leaves seventeen upgrades and 62 basics to produce. Sixteen four-output runs cover the basic deficit. The circuit bill includes 50 QBit CPUs, sixteen Nano CPUs and 37 multilayer boards. The four arrays also need sixteen Power ICs, sixteen single Signalum wires, eight chrome plates and four Improved Ender Eyes.

Two engraved Power IC wafers are already available. The EV cutter takes 22 mB lubricant per wafer and gives four Power ICs. Four wafers cover the arrays. Keep the cutter supplied while the Assembly Line makes motors. The remaining Signalum wire shares furnace time with HSS-G and Lumium. Aluminium and stainless steel can move to the west furnace to clear time on the hotter coils.

The two engraved wafers returned eight Power ICs to storage. The first cut ran at 768 EU/t for 39 ticks, and the Power IC processing pattern is now installed on the EV cutter. The same saw can cut a plain CPU wafer into eight chips with 22 mB lubricant, or a glowstone-doped boule into 32 wafers with 20 mB. The glass-lens engraving run produces sixteen CPU wafers from four doped wafers. Ten doped wafers remained at 95h40m.

At 96h01m all sixteen Power ICs had reached ME. The laser can move to the glass lens for CPU wafers.

At 96h09m the laser returned sixteen CPU wafers from four glowstone-doped wafers. Its blue topaz lens and Power IC pattern are back in place. ME then accepted a four-basic-Quantumprocessor job that included cutting a Nano CPU wafer.

At 96h23m the first of four new Quantum Processor Arrays was in ME, alongside twelve of the original sixteen Power ICs. Its native order included a QBit wafer on the EV reactor. A sample after the motor run found both north power converters full. The live controller calculates 15,360 EU/t for 600 ticks for both pistons and conveyors, and 20,480 EU/t for 600 ticks for arms.

At 97h41m the two pistons, both conveyors and both insulated niobium-titanium leads were in ME. The HV and MV extruders can work the full and small HSS-G gear dies simultaneously. Eight basic Quantumprocessors, six upgraded processors and one array were visible after the board orders. Two of the upgraded-processor builds subsequently consumed six basic processors. Keep counting the three grades separately, including during crafting jobs. For the first arm, the outstanding array needs Signalum wire and chrome plates. The circuit tender can now use the native red steel and Signalum Blend patterns. A 32-ore silver shipment yielded 64 crushed ores and enough clean silver by-product to restart board work before the second crush.
