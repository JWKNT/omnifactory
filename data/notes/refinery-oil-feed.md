# Oil supply for the remaining EV circuit boards

The first twelve buckets of naphtha yielded eight epoxy boards. Forty more boards at that rate need sixty buckets of naphtha. The tower produces 1,200 mB of sulfuric naphtha per three buckets of crude in sixty seconds. Fifty tower cycles therefore need 150 buckets of crude, with about fifty minutes available to build the rest of the EV components while they run.

JEI gives a bucket of oil per Oilsands Dust in 344 ticks at LV. One LV electrolyzer narrowly keeps up with the current tower at 3.49 buckets per minute. MV doubles that throughput and fits the existing four-amp bus. Five pennies buy 32 oilsands ore, and each ore smelts to one dust. Five purchases cost one quarter in change and provide 160 buckets. The alternative Soul Sand centrifuge gives 100 mB per ten seconds at MV. Five would be needed to supply the present tower. Soul sand is renewable from the Atomic Reconstructor and sand, or four at the bench per sand and Hellish Matter. Page 549's Oil Drilling Rig takes drilling fluid, pipes and 875 EU/t for its 1,000 mB recipe. Revisit it once the EV power supply can sustain that load. The purchased ore makes sense for this run of fifty tower cycles.

For a permanent feed, the refinery inlet hatch at (13,82,-13) has a Fluid Tank under it. Check its contents before moving it. An MV electrolyzer directly beneath the hatch can push oil upward. An MV electric furnace at (12,81,-13) can auto-export oilsands dust east into the electrolyzer. The cobblestone pier at (12,81,-12) sits beside the live branch at (11,81,-12). A cable in that pier and one more at (13,81,-12) can power both devices without crossing an aisle. The two machines together require four Good circuits and two MV hulls. Connect ore storage to the furnace after verifying the first three-bucket transfer into the tower. Protect the naphtha drum and keep an eye on gas storage over the 50 cycles.
