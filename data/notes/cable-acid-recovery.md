# Reclaim the acid that holds up EV cable

The MV cable wrapper had 12 mB of silicone rubber. One aluminium wire needs 48 mB. Its reactor already held 64,000 mB methanol, but ME held only 1,000 mB hydrochloric acid. Adding methanol or another wiremill would leave the same shortage. ME also held 36,000 mB diluted hydrochloric acid from other chemistry, with another 4,000 in the silicone reactor's outlet.

JEI gives two distillation options. The idle Basic Distillery at (6,81,-5) takes 2,000 mB dilute acid and returns 1,000 mB acid on circuit 1 in 1,200 ticks at 16 EU/t. The tower can do the same with a water by-product in 600 ticks at 64 EU/t. Keep the tower on refinery work for now. One 16-bucket tank of dilute acid will fund eight buckets of acid. The silicone reactor needs six for a nine-dust batch.

scripts/cable_acid_recovery.py checks the still, loads the selector and recovers acid into ME. Check run/cable-acid-recovered for a measured stock increase. The silicone reactor already receives methanol through its south bus. Adding acid to that bus is the next step once the reserve returns reliably.

The first collection raised ME hydrochloric acid from 1,000 to 5,000 mB. The silicone reactor consumed the recovered acid. Nine polymer dust and one sulfur supplied 1,296 mB rubber. The cable wrapper now holds 1,308 mB, enough insulation for 27 single aluminium wires at 48 mB each.

The wrapper finished all twelve bare aluminium wires and retained 732 mB rubber. All four energetic conduits, both quad wires, the chest and the Nano Processor Assembly for the east converter are on hand. The EV hull is the last ingredient. The MV assembler has 44,540 mB polyethylene and the EV hull pattern is visible in ME.

At 63h12m the silicone reactor's export bus gained a capacity card. Its two active filters are methanol and hydrochloric acid. The first reading showed 7,000 mB acid in the reactor and another 7,000 in ME. The filter tool now checks where a ray actually strikes the installed part. On this crowded branch, aiming at the right coordinates through the middle of the cable clicked the cable instead of the bus. The still's output is the remaining manual transfer in this loop.

During a separate workshop job, acid in the reactor rose from 7,000 to 11,000 mB while ME fell from 7,000 to 3,000 mB. The reactor later reached 14,000 mB. The item-card and bucket test therefore measured a four-bucket delivery without moving a container.

At 66h54m the still had 1,000 mB of diluted sulfuric acid in its only input, while ME held 18,000 mB of diluted hydrochloric acid. Sharing that tank between the two acids interrupted cable insulation even with 64 buckets of methanol at the silicone reactor. The hydrogen-fed desulfurizer at (12,81,-11) was idle with 64,000 mB hydrogen, two empty inputs and a working unfiltered fluid import bus. Its JEI recipe takes 1,000 mB each of chlorine and hydrogen and produces 1,000 mB hydrochloric acid in 60 base ticks at 8 EU/t. scripts/refinery_hydrochloric_acid.py takes spare chlorine from the LV separator while the refinery is free. The HV titanium reactor also holds 21,000 mB after a full fluid drive prevented a 13,000 mB deposit. A tank transfer of sixteen buckets would still leave five there. Check the silicone reactor's acid and a fresh rubber delivery before counting this as commissioned. The silicone tender can now combine up to four sulfur batches when the preceding ingredients are available.

At 81h36m the recovery still at (6,81,−5) gained an active ME fluid import bus on its east side. The cable below its neighbor supplies the connection. Leave the import unfiltered so program zero can return sulfuric acid and program one can return hydrochloric acid. The still had an empty input and output after installation. On the next diluted hydrochloric charge, compare feed consumption with the combined ME and silicone reactor acid gain.
