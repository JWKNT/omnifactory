# Six Basic RF Capacitors

Quest 272 pays a nickel and opens the Basic Capacitor Bank, Double Capacitor and Farming Station branches. The RF capacitor is an Ender IO item. The electronic capacitor for GregTech circuits is a separate part. JEI offers three bench recipes for the RF capacitor. Use one vacuum tube, two bare tin wires and three electrical steel nuggets. The alternative electronics are a GregTech capacitor or an SMD capacitor. This instance lists no RF capacitor quest reward.

Make six. Two electrical steel ingots divide into eighteen nuggets at the bench. Twelve tin wires and six vacuum tubes finish the batch. The factory order can share the wiremill and assembler with the polymer smelter and brewery. Four RF capacitors belong to a future basic capacitor bank, the other two can make one Double Capacitor with two energetic alloy ingots and coal dust. A bank also takes four dark steel plates and a Power Buffer. The buffer uses four iron plates, four red alloy plates and one redstone block. The current MV CEF already holds 2,504,096 EU, so the buffer and bank can wait until an RF branch needs local storage. The Farming Station also needs a Z-Logic Controller and a Soul Machine Chassis.

The parts chest contains two energetic alloy ingots left after the fabricator and MV CEF were installed. After all six basic capacitors are made and quest 272 registers, the bench can use those ingots, two capacitors and one coal dust for quest 44. Keep the four remaining capacitors available for the bank.
