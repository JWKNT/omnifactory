# Pick up at the LV cable line

Astra has working LV power. Putting It All Together (56) is claimed and the LV Wiremill (53) is unlocked. At this checkpoint the book shows 27 Genesis quests, one End Game quest and three Progression quests done. The generator, CEF, water and cable positions are in lv-power.md. The camp and both double chests are in workshop.md. Use scripts/camp.py chest for bulk goods or parts for components. Both cakes remain available for the permanent void base.

Build the wiremill first, followed by the compressor. The wiremill quest asks for four LV motors and a wiremill. Read each machine's recipes and rewards before batching. One Basic Circuit was consumed by the CEF. The parts chest and the older chest contain leftover circuit boards, vacuum tubes and resistors. Count those before making more. Complete the four Crafting Stations quest while preparing a batch of motors; the older chest has two crafting tables, and the library provides books. Access the shelves by the opening around (-176, 70, 238), leading down to (-172, 66, 241).

Collect the rest of the iron from west. The parts chest has additional raw iron, silver, metal stock, spare binder, conduit, rubber wood and fertilizer. There are two additional omniquarters in storage from the iron and first circuit quests, besides the quarter paid for Putting It All Together. Coal stock is low. A repeatable local wood source is now available from the rubber tree plot south of the furnaces. More coal ore is available in the northern gallery or via the coin recipe. Keep both a fuel batch and a metal batch moving.

Use scripts/grid_job.py for a camp crafting batch, scripts/tend.py to operate a furnace, and scripts/quest_page.py to visit specified quest pages. Before consuming an intermediate that the book requires, close the crafting screen and check its individual count with the quest_items library described in field-tools.md.
