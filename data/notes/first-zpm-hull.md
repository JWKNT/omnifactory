# First ZPM hull

Page 536 opened with the first cold Naquadah Ingot. It pays an OmniDollar and opens the ZPM assembler and compressor. Eight iridium plates make the casing. The machine assembler recipe takes the casing, two insulated Naquadah leads and 288 mB PTFE. Use it to save the ninth iridium plate required at the bench. Preserve the first cold Naquadah for the boule. The two leads take one additional ingot through the wiremill and two cable-wrapping operations.

Use the lepidolite already recovered for the breeder's boron. The factory has 154 clean dust. Two 20-dust charges in the spare MV electrolyzer supply four buckets of fluorine for the first PTFE batch. They also yield eight aluminium dust for the freezer, six lithium and two potassium. Bastnasite also works but the twelve clean dust on hand give only two buckets.

The in-game recipes for one bucket of tetrafluoroethylene need four buckets of hydrofluoric acid and two of chloroform. The latter need twelve buckets of chlorine and two of methane. ME has nine buckets of chlorine, with four more in the LV electrolyzer. Keep the silicone cable wrapper supplied. The oxygen polymer recipe gives 216 mB PTFE from 144 mB monomer. The 2,160 mB recipe with titanium tetrachloride gives twice the input volume as plastic and suits a future dedicated line once several hulls are due. Run the first recipe on the smaller charge. The surveyed stocks include 24 crushed iridium and 104 more ore.

The first bauxite charge was still running in the MV cell. The shared EV electrolyzer can take the same lepidolite charge when idle. The LV centrifuge has an empty feed tank and four spare product tanks. JEI gives four buckets each of methane and LPG from eight buckets of refinery gas in 200 ticks at 5 EU/t before overclocking. ME has 1,680 buckets of refinery gas, so one portable charge covers the first hull with gas left over. Bank both products. The existing west-side Light Fuel export already keeps the diesel generator full while these machines run.

The monomer recipe draws 256 EU/t, above the MV refinery reactors. Use a dedicated HV reactor at (34,82,−8) beside the diesel branch. The deck already supports both it and a 512 V CEF at (34,82,−9) directly below the Vibrant Energy Conduit. Put the converter’s output south into the reactor. The existing Fluix cable at (35,82,−8) touches the future reactor’s east face. This gives the new equipment its own converter and a short fluid return. The MV methanol reactor has a full hydrogen tank for the first precursor. The program-one MV reactor at (11,80,−13) can do the other after its remaining hydrogen is banked. Use the LV casing assembler for the 288 mB hull recipe once the polymer and both insulated leads are ready.

The lepidolite actually returned 4,000 mB fluorine in the MV electrolyzer. The refinery gas produced 8,000 mB each of methane and LPG in the centrifuge. At 119h19m the nitrogen reactor at (11,80,−13) was receiving oxygen alongside hydrogen again. Leave it on nitrogen chemistry. The Nitro-Diesel page is paid, so use the program-one fuel reactor at (13,81,0) for chloroform instead. Its only remaining feed is one bucket of nitric acid, which can go back to ME. The chlorine charge can use native rock-salt electrolysis while the other reactor makes hydrofluoric acid.
