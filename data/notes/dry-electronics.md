# A dedicated assembler for tubes and resistors

A Basic Circuit takes two vacuum tubes and two resistors. The ME network can make the circuit, draw the fine copper wire and craft the glass tubes. The player still loads the shared assembler for the two electronic parts. Live JEI gives one vacuum tube for two paper, one glass tube and two fine copper wires in a basic assembler. The bench takes three wires. The assembler also makes twelve resistors from four fine copper wires and one coal dust. The bench needs eight wires, four coal dust and eight paper for the same twelve. Both assembler recipes run without fluid.

The factory has three powered assemblers. The LV machine at (4,81,8) has 144 mB solder, the MV machine at (7,82,1) has 72 mB, and the HV machine at (10,82,3) was idle after its processor order with an empty tank. The book confirmed its page 532 reward. The original idea was to build a fourth assembler for these two dry recipes. Its bill included ten LV motors and two Primitive Processors. Reuse the HV machine for ME processing instead. Put the interface directly above it at (10,83,3). Four cables run north from the emitter cable at (8,83,7) to z3, and a fifth to (9,83,3) joins the interface. Its seven additional processing pattern slots can serve later HV orders. The two solder assemblers retain their existing jobs.

Reserve two blank patterns. Five polyethylene sheets, three fine silver wires and any circuitAdvanced make eight blanks. JEI accepts an Electronic Processor Array, Refined Processor Array, Processor Assembly or Nanoprocessor. The order now uses a stocked circuit first and quotes the LV Electronic Processor Array when all four are absent. This lets the dry assembler use the existing phenolic-board process even when the epoxy board shelf is empty. The first commissioning orders are two vacuum tubes and twelve resistors through the crafting terminal. Check the returned quantities before marking the line online. scripts/dry_assembler.py holds the surveyed route and commissioning order.
