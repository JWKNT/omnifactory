# Platinum from the iridium shipment

The MV ore washer now accepts Crushed Iridium Ore through the same item conduit that supplies crushed galena and sphalerite. The insertion filter's third sample is iridium. The third stocking slot of the ME interface at (12,83,3) supplies it. The two older samples remain in place. The HV mill already had the corresponding ore-to-crushed pattern.

The first automatic batch started with 32 crushed ore. The washer had 22 left when ME showed 30 tiny piles of platinum. Each nine tiny piles pack into a full dust. A 32-ore wash therefore pays ten platinum dust with six tiny piles toward the next ingot. The purified iridium returns to ME through the existing outlet and can be milled later for iridium and osmium. Use scripts/iridium_washer_feed.py to check the filter installation. At 138h10m the next motor had 178 of its 256 fine platinum wires.
