# Cable insulation for EV

Page 785, New Cable Coverings, pays a nickel for the first single aluminium cable and is required for the EV hull page. The book recommends silicone rubber because it insulates every voltage tier. JEI gives 48 mB per single aluminium wire with circuit 24, compared with 72 mB of styrene-butadiene rubber. Standard rubber cannot make this cable.

One LV reactor cycle combines nine polydimethylsiloxane dust and one sulfur into 1,296 mB silicone rubber, enough for 27 single cables. The first hull only takes two. Give the cable assembler its own silicone supply and keep it available for the next upgrades. The current cable maker repeatedly swaps molten solder and rubber and borrows the circuit selector.

The direct MV dust recipe takes one silicon, two buckets each of methanol and HCl, returns three dust and two buckets of diluted HCl. Three cycles fund the first 27 cables. Live JEI also offers a circuit-2 methane route. Our centrifuge has 2,100 mB of methane, enough for one of the three cycles. Titanium's carbon monoxide can instead be combined with four buckets of hydrogen per bucket to make methanol on circuit 0. There were 50 buckets of hydrogen at the MV electrolyzer. Diverting six carbon monoxide from an eight-bucket titanium batch would save six carbon's worth of fresh methanol feed but reduce the carbon returned by titanium recovery. Compare that with the pyrolyse and distillation side quests before committing the long-term supply. Page 801 describes collecting the several wood products in a distillation tower. No silicone or EV cables have yet been produced.

The first bulk titanium run recovered ten carbon dust. Reserve the second run’s eight buckets of carbon monoxide for methanol. That makes eight buckets with 32 buckets of hydrogen, enough for twelve polydimethylsiloxane dust by the direct route. The acid reactor has 59 buckets of byproduct hydrogen and needs headroom to keep making sulfuric acid. The chlorine reaction can make HCl locally, one bucket each of chlorine and hydrogen per bucket. The first nine dust only need six buckets of HCl. Distilling their diluted acid opens a further return.
