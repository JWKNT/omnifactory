# Bedrock trip for Grains of Infinity

Quest 296 asks for flint and steel and one grain. Its nickel and its prerequisites for the Numismatic Dynamo, Soul Binder, and farming station make it worth visiting bedrock while the silicon boule spends five minutes in the blast furnace. The actual lighter recipe takes one steel nugget and one flint. One steel ingot gives nine nuggets. Two gravel with the mortar give one flint. A spare steel ingot came out of the alloy smelter during cell production.

Use the known natural cave to the diamond ledge at (-148,17,122), then descend from solid stone clear of the lava lake. The earlier iron pick and diamond mining hammer both remain serviceable. scripts/mine_stairs.py cuts one step at a time with an iron pick, checks the floor, and stops at an opening. On reaching exposed bedrock, light several separate spots and let the fires burn out: the book gives each a 50 percent chance of dropping grains. The access route and actual bedrock coordinates still need to be established. The macerator and electrolyzer can resume full production when the EBF's 120 EU/t load finishes.

Walking south for 16 ticks with yaw zero reached the lower ledge at (-148,12,125). From there a southbound iron-pick staircase reached bedrock at (-148,2,140). The first sixteen fires yielded eight Grains of Infinity. Let the fire die naturally. The return lane at z141 became walkable first; from (-144,2,141) scripts/collect_grains.py could reach the scattered drops. Keep this gallery for future runs.
