Make two blank models. Each needs one pulsating mesh, four electrical steel plates, three fine gold wires and one refined circuit. One pristine Spider Matter yields 32 string for the mesh. One subsequent Enderman payout will fund the Dragon. Each model also takes seven Extraterrestrial Matter. The workshop drawers already contain thousands.
