# Supplies shared by the reactors and first pattern terminal

At the start of the plastics hardware order I costed the two reactors and solidifier together with the first ME Interface, Molecular Assembler, 1k Crafting Storage, Pattern Terminal and MV assembler. The stock survey came up short 54 paper, 37 copper ore, 16 iron ore, 66 slimeballs, five coal, ten aluminium ingots, two certus quartz, three fluix crystals and the five polyethylene sheets. Machine inputs and unfinished products can reduce those shortfalls. In particular, the macerator was doubling fifteen iron ore already.

Order one more 32-ore copper lot for two nickels. It feeds both the remaining cable and the AE2 hardware. Run a second 32-paper bath charge and sixteen Slime simulations during machine production. Leave the Shulker and Enderman power and pearl reserves in effect. Stored certus ore can supply the two crystals. Recount aluminium once the copper and circuit work has advanced, before starting a new blast. The first array for blank patterns and the other two for the MV assembler are already made.

The first extra copper purchase brought the ore stock to 53. The macerator took nine for doubling. For faster wiring, buy one further 32-ore lot and send up to 64 raw copper ore through spare furnaces in sixteen-ore charges. Leave eight raw ore for doubling and let the electric furnace finish other metals. This trades some yield for throughput while the wiremill needs feed. The Spider model can also supply twelve ingots per pristine. Quest 641 pays a quarter if the workshop gathers 64 copper ingots together. Keep making components as the copper arrives.

All four 16-ore copper charges finished. Quest 641 accepted the stack in hand and paid one OmniQuarter. The next terminal survey counted 76 copper ingots, enough to start the red alloy and fine wire together. The redstone ore and impure copper dust used separate ovens.

After nine sheets, the stores held three arrays and three Fluix Crystals. Count seven blank patterns at the end of the combined bill. The eighth from the single craft goes into the Pattern Terminal. Counting eight blanks in addition to the terminal incorrectly asks for a fourth array and a second plastics batch. With the clay mill and MV assembler included, the survey estimated shortages of 27 iron ore, 17 copper ore, 16 aluminium ingots and 30 paper before ongoing jobs are credited. Queue one lot each of iron and copper, a 32-sheet paper bath and a 24-ingot aluminium charge.

For the extra three fluix, make four in the workshop pool from two charged certus, two redstone and two nether quartz. The furnace gives two quartz per ore for both certus and nether quartz. Two ores of each cover the pool and leave crystals for the cores and quartz glass. Use stored certus ore and purchase a nether quartz ore lot for the cable line. The autoclave converts GregTech certus to the AE2 charger input using 250 mB water. The workshop can also electrolyze four glass for one nether quartz, but the combined order already consumes the available glass. scripts/pattern_supplies.py handles the ore orders, firing, charging and pool work alongside machine production.

The three ore lots arrived at 32 each and cost six nickels total. Two nether quartz ore yielded four quartz. The workshop also fired two certus ore, started a sixteen-polymer Slime run, and loaded one furnace with sixteen raw iron ore and one coke. Six sand are reserved for glass while the first pattern hardware is outstanding. The immediate macerator needs glass panes for its vacuum tubes. Dye-free glass also feeds the first pane autocraft.
