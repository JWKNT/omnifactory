# Tier-one miner parts

The first mining mission consumes the ship. Page 217 pays one OmniDollar. In this instance JEI gives one ship recipe: a guidance system, two lasers, an LV field generator, two Conductive Iron Thrusters and a Basic Diesel Generator. None of the laser, guidance system, generator or thruster comes directly from a quest reward.

Each pair of lasers needs two redstone blocks, eight nether quartz and four glass. The guidance system takes the three stocked Steel Heavy Plating, two LV sensors and four qualifying circuits. Use the assembler for the sensors; each uses one steel plate instead of the bench's four. Compare the stored circuit grades before making more. The field generator can use four conductive iron wires and an Ender Pearl on the bench, or 288 mB molten conductive iron and Enderpearl Dust in the assembler. The workshop already has fourteen wires. Keep the construction jetpack assembled; its thruster recipe is reusable for the ship.

Two Ender Shards and two Restonia entered the alloy smelter for the ship's thrusters. The redstone ore recipe costs five pennies for 32 ore; the existing ME furnace pattern yields six dust per ore. One purchase covers 192 redstone while Witch simulations can still supply glowstone. The miner also returns dense iron, nickel and rutile together on the first cargo mission. Use those returns when pricing the next stainless and titanium runs.

The bench finished the LV field generator using four Refined Circuits, four stocked conductive iron wires and one Ender Pearl. The sensor order can use basic circuits one at a time. Three printed logic circuits and three printed silicon also finished for the workshop interfaces.

JEI prices a full steel gear at four ingots in the MV extruder and eight in the alloy smelter. The miner order now uses the existing gear die in the MV extruder. It batches tin cable for all four motors, both pistons and the generator together. The LV assembler accepts the magnetic steel rods already produced for the MV motor line. The projector keeps its first hull.

Both tier-one lasers are finished. The pair used two redstone blocks, eight nether quartz and four glass. The field generator is stored with the ship supplies.
