# Four small coils for the first Processor Assembly

Quest 136 needs one Small Coil, pays one OmniNickel and leads to the third tier-three circuit. Keep four coils for one Processor Assembly. The bench uses eight fine copper wires and one steel bolt for two coils. The assembler makes the same pair with one wire and one bolt. Nickel Zinc Ferrite bolts double the yield, but the workshop already has steel and two fine copper wires.

The order in scripts/small_coils.py uses one of the three steel ingots. Lathe it into two rods, cut both rods into eight bolts in the water-fed MV cutter, and feed two bolts and the two wires to the MV assembler. Six bolts remain for later electronics. The run/small-coils file tracks which feed has been loaded across factory rounds.

The run delivered eight bolts from the two rods. The MV assembler used two bolts and two fine copper wires and delivered all four Small Coils. Page 136 paid one OmniNickel. Reserve the four coils for the Processor Assembly.

The MV assembler wound the second coil order after the passives. Four steel bolts and four fine copper wires made eight more coils. The player collected all twelve for the three HV Processor Assemblies. The cutter produced eight additional Central Processing Units and brought the ME count to nineteen. A RAM wafer entered the same cutter for a further 32 RAM.
