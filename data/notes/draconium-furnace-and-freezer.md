The End trip supplied four Draconium Dust and page 336 paid. Page 288 also requires the sixteen Naquadah Coil Blocks from page 324 and a bucket of Nitro-Diesel from page 179. Budget the coolant, verify the freezer’s voltage and arrange its fluid input before heating Draconium.
