The fifth furnace already meets the heat requirement, but its controller currently reports only 4,096 input voltage. Its EV hatch and basic capacitor suffice for the Naquadah metal heat at 1,920 EU/t. Verify the controller reports at least 8,000 input voltage and can sustain 11,520,000 EU before charging the boule. The Assembly Line's separately powered hatches are a working reference.

The silicon blocks need 81 ingots. The Multi Smelter already has the dust-to-ingot pattern. Keep a separate dust allowance for silicone insulation and plates for printed silicon. The factory had 30 silicon dust and 199 buckets of argon at 116h07m. Avoid diverting silicon into storage blocks while the breeder and ship still depend on insulated cables.

Use 240 mB lubricant in the cutter for 400 base ticks at 384 EU/t. Water needs 960 mB and 1,600 ticks. Keep an uncut wafer for comparing later recipes.
