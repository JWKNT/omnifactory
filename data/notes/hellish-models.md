# Twenty Blaze simulations for the Nether models

Quest 689 asks to see the Blaze, Wither Skeleton and Ghast Data Models together. The reward is ten OmniQuarters. All three models remain available afterward. The three blanks cost twelve electrical steel plates, three Basic Circuits, nine fine gold wires and three pulsating mesh in total. Start this order once the brewery has registered and its two blaze rods have served their purpose.

Each model takes seven Hellish Matter. Bootstrap the Blaze model with ordinary matter from the metal simulations. Four Overworldian Matter and one netherrack make one Hellish Matter. A spare rod supplies blaze powder. If all rods went into the brewery, one bone and two extra Hellish Matter make a replacement rod. Making seven to nine Hellish Matter from the initial stock is cheaper than waiting for a new mob drop in Peaceful.

Load twenty polymer into the Blaze model. At 1,024 RF/t for 300 ticks, twenty simulations consume 6,144,000 RF. That is a little over four lapidary diamonds before other machines draw power. The rotation checks the diamond and pearl reserves before loading it. Twenty ordinary Hellish Matter cover the fourteen for the other two models, one for the wither skull and two for the ghast tear. A pristine Blaze can yield ten blaze rods. Sulfur dust and magma blocks are alternatives in the fabricator.

For the Wither Skeleton model, compress twelve bones into three skeleton skulls, then add one Hellish Matter. For Ghast, use one spider eye, sugar and two Hellish Matter. The first available Spider pristine is earmarked for eight eyes; subsequent Spider drops return to twelve copper ingots. The alternate eye recipe needs rotten flesh, an apple, a mushroom and one Overworldian Matter. All three model recipes and the rewards were checked against this instance. The three models and the reward are still ahead.

The store survey found seven Hellish Matter, and the player already held two Blaze Powder. That covers the Blaze model's non-blank ingredients. The previous order waited for nine matter even when the powder already existed. The planner now accounts for the powder, existing rods and how much extra matter can be made from the stored netherrack. Blaze Powder goes on the bulk shelf with the rods.

The first Spider model run produced a pristine that transferred through the overhead conduits. The fabricator was set to Spider Eye, and the workshop collected all eight eyes and stored them on the bulk shelf. One eye is reserved for the Ghast route. Subsequent Spider pristine can go back to copper.
