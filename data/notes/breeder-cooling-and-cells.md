# The eight cells and twelve coolers for page 350

The eight Fission Reactor Cells need 32 Tough Alloy and 32 Reinforced Glass. Either alloy-smelter recipe gives four reinforced glass from one Advanced Alloy Plate and three glass blocks or glass dust at 4 EU/t for 400 base ticks. Use 24 glass blocks and eight alloy plates. The plates each take one Mixed Metal Ingot in the compressor at 2 EU/t for 400 base ticks. Keep the renewable sand and glass line available to the polymer kiln while it supplies that batch.

Twelve Empty Coolers take six grid crafts, 24 Tough Alloy and 24 steel ingots. The canner takes one empty cooler and sixteen lapis dust or glowstone dust per block at 2 EU/t for 400 base ticks. The eight Lapis Coolers need 128 lapis dust. The four Glowstone Coolers need 64 glowstone dust. Preserve enough glowstone for the quantum wafer and Lumium reactors before loading a full canner run.

Together with the 116 Tough Alloy for Basic Plating, the cells and coolers bring the first reactor's total to 172 Tough Alloy. Four graphite moderator blocks cost 36 graphite ingots. There were 28 loose ingots after making the first Hard Carbon Alloy. Finish page 347 and the IV assembler parts first. The longer tungsten plate and alloy orders belong on native machine patterns; the canner and glass batches can run beside them.
