# Getting enough Hellish Matter for diamonds

The three Hellish models each draw 1,024 RF/t. Their recipes require a blank model, seven Hellish Matter and a mob-specific ingredient. A Blaze model could produce the 28 Hellish Matter for the Shulker route after seven are crafted by hand, and its pristine drops would feed the later lava line. The two existing turbines peak at 320 RF/t, though. Until power improves, run Slime at 64 RF/t and keep the extra blank for Shulker. A Shulker simulation costs 512 RF/t. Gather the first 28 stone equivalents of netherrack through the Nether Cake if a cake can be made early.

Quest 688 needs one Hellish Matter and pays a nickel. For the first netherrack, feed the LV chemical reactor one Dust Block and a bucket of lava. The same Dust Block with water makes a clay block for polymer fuel. A cheap bulk carrier for quarry lava is the Extra Utilities Stone Drum: six cobblestone, two cobblestone slabs and one bowl. Test recovering one bucket's worth in the drum before taking a batch. JEI also lists a GregTech bronze drum but that costs eight bronze plates and a bucket. Keep bucketed lava away from the water used to cool the obsidian quarry.

The first Hellish Matter is made and quest 688 paid its nickel. At the quarry, the southern cobble extension (-189,63,244) was close enough to take back the cooling water source. From (-196,63,246) the hammer broke the cap at (-194,62,244) without moving or collecting. Looking low toward (-193.5,61.1,244.5) from the same standing point let the empty bucket scoop lava. Standing still kept the cap beneath the player intact. Back at the factory, scripts/reactor_bucket.py lava transferred the bucket into the reactor. One Dust Block became netherrack at 15 EU/t in 20 ticks. The bench combined that with four Overworldian Matter. The reactor is free again. Bring storage to take several buckets on the next trip.
