# Tier Six engine core bay

Page 254 pays a quarter for the Tier Six ship and opens the egg expedition, Einsteinium and the first Omnium quest. The three cores need a machine the previous miner did not use. JEI lists one recipe for each core, one Enderium frame and eight redstone blocks at 8,000 EU/t for 2,000 ticks. The EV Signalum smelter accepts 2,048 V. An IV alloy smelter running one core at a time on the north feeder can handle all three. Each core takes 16 million EU across its run. Let the hardened capacitors recharge between orders and avoid overlapping the heavy press.

The surveyed bay at (21,86,−19) is empty, on top of the IV Assembly Line hatch and west of the double tungsten cable. The cable reports 8,192 V and four amps. The ME glass at (20,87,−19) can feed a west-side interface at (20,86,−19). Keep the existing roof glass in place. Prepare four quadruple tungstensteel windings from sixteen wire (eight ingots in the wiremill), two platinum cables, an IV hull and two circuits. Keep the circuits required by the first Crystal Processor Array until its four Energy Flow Circuits have been completed. Eight redstone blocks per core means 216 redstone for the three. The three Resonant Thrusters also require three basic Numismatic Dynamos. The fresh-build recipe is JEI crafting index one. Index zero upgrades a dynamo that already exists.
