# Three Diamond Furnaces for the Tier Six thrusters

Each Resonant Thruster takes four Enderium plates, three Red Alloy plates, a Redstone Reception Coil and a Numismatic Dynamo. The basic dynamo recipe takes a Diamond Furnace, two Vibrant Alloy plates, two Vibrant Bimetal Gears and two coils. The Resonant Conversion Kit recipe starts from a dynamo. The basic machine satisfies the thruster recipe. Leave both working generators in place.

Make the Obsidian Furnace for Genesis page 18 first. Once its reward has been claimed, JEI's shapeless recipe gives both Diamond Furnaces back for the one Obsidian Furnace. The three thrusters therefore need three diamond upgrades across both projects, costing 24 diamonds in total. At 130h55m the factory held two Silver Furnaces and a plain furnace for the third. Protect the 32-diamond reserve in each generator. Feed the upgrades only with surplus while the simulations are fueled. Other uses of an Obsidian Furnace can reuse the same reverse recipe until it goes into a machine.
