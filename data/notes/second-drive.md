# More fluid types at the pattern aisle

All four 1k fluid cells reached their five-type limit during the second fuel order. They used only 89, 105, 98 and 42 bytes respectively out of 1,024. The first drive's other six slots hold item cells. An ammonia transfer failed with 1,000 mB still safe in the portable tank. Feeding the entire dimethylamine and chloramine reserves to the MV reactor freed the space. The terminal then accepted the ammonia. The fuel line now takes hydrazine straight from the reactor to the mixer. It can send by-product water to the MV cutting saw, and finished fuel to the projector hatch.

The second drive is planned for (11,83,10), on the south face of the crafting interface. The survey found air there. It keeps the ten original cells in service and adds room for both kinds. One extra fluid cell admits five types. The storage chest in the drive's recipe needs eighteen dark steel ingots, three pure crystal varieties, a small storage crate, quartz glass and an illuminated panel. The original chest was consumed by the first drive. scripts/second_drive.py stages another from fresh materials and checks the new cell's slot after insertion. Until it is installed, scripts/drive_capacity.py reads the real fluid type counters; small water and ethylene reserves can go to their consumers to keep slots open.

The steel bay has 25 ingots on hand for this and other orders. Both the ordinary obsidian and the Void Crystal alternative should be checked for dark steel. The existing growth chamber handles the seeds. The old ME Chest and ME Drive quest rewards were already collected. See ae2-storage-costs.md for the original price comparison.
