# Automatic boiler fuel in the void

Build one Snad growing column above the boiler at (6,80,10). Use a downward hopper at y81, single rail and stationary hopper minecart at y82, Snad at y83, and a cane base at y84. Enclose the cut stalks and the cart; collect through the Snad. Leave space for water beside Snad, and a Dark Utilities timer pulsing the Snad. A piston at (7,85,10) faces west to break the second segment. An observer above it watches the third segment. A comparator reading the hopper should hold the piston extended when fuel backs up. Test the cutoff and check for loose cane before leaving it running.

The two hoppers need ten iron plates, two chests and a wrench. The four plates from commissioning leave six to compress. Rails cost six iron and a stick, minecart five iron, and the piston one iron. Timer uses a clock, four redstone torches and four stone. Observer needs nether quartz, two redstone and six cobble. Comparator takes three torches, quartz and three stone. Keep two spare torches for the cutoff. Use one Genesis Snad from the western shore and keep the second plot intact. Quest 228 eventually wants an autoclave and a vacuum chest, while this hopper collection route is immediately buildable. A future ender-IO line can collect from a larger field.

The farm is built and running. The cart sits at (6.5,82.06,10.51) underneath the Snad. Water is west of the crop. The timer north of it runs at 10 ticks. The observer and piston both face west. The upright redstone torch stack and the wire on top occupy x8, z10. The crop chamber is enclosed, with a roof at (6,87,10). Approach from the south stairs at z13. The eastern walkway at x8 leads to the observer wire. The construction helpers are scripts/farm_shell.py, scripts/farm_controls.py, scripts/farm_crop.py and scripts/farm_harvest.py. scripts/void_cobble.py collects building stacks from the drawer.

At the final check the hopper cart was the only nearby entity. The energy input comes in pulses; inspect the CEF charge over time to measure sustained production.
