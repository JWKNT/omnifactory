# Sugar cane on the western shore

Four cane plants grow at x=-209, y=63, z=194 through 197. The two middle plants sit on the Genesis Snad. Cut above the bottom segment to keep the plants growing. The lake beside them supplies water for later buckets. The bank immediately east is a block higher and is a good place to stand while placing or harvesting.

The plant and Snad at z=195 were moved for the void boiler farm. The second Snad at z=196 and the two ordinary plots remain at the lake.
