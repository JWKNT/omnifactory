In this machine each fish gives 40 mB in 80 ticks at 16 EU/t. Show the first bucket to page 416, then empty it into the new drum. scripts/fish_oil_reserve.py dispatches another 25 or 50 at a time while other machines work, moves each bucket to the drum and restores upward output after six buckets. The tender counts fish already committed to oil toward the 150-fish order.

Three successive transfers brought the drum to 3,000 mB. The next charge uses 50 fish for two buckets. Filling the first bucket while the second processes leaves the extractor running.

The fourth bucket reached the drum while the eighteen-ingot stainless charge was running. The extractor then showed zero stored EU and repeatedly lost recipe progress with 240 mB in its output. Its west cable at (3,80,8) is also the cable to the second blast energy hatch. The blast uses 120 EU/t and the extractor needs another 16, exceeding the four-amp LV supply. Leave the two blast hatches connected. Confirm extraction under blast load after installation.

The extractor finished the fifty-fish charge once the blast furnace stopped. The fifth bucket reached the drum. The reserve now reconciles the drum, extractor and fresh storage survey together. An older ME count had included fish that had already entered the extractor.
