# Tungsten after the repeat flight

Eight reactor casings take eight Basic Platings and one robust casing. Seven crafts cover the 54-casing quest, before the controller and ship port. Basic Plating yields two per craft and takes four tungsten plates, four Tough Alloy and one heavy steel plate. The casing plating alone therefore uses 112 tungsten plates. Neither plating nor casing has an alternate recipe or a direct quest reward.

The original sixteen-bar stock target was lower than the twenty-four-bar holdback on the plate press. Lift the working target to 32 and reserve eight loose bars while making plates. The tungstensteel order still retains four loose bars. Take tungstensteel in pairs from cooled tungsten and steel. The two IV pistons are already on a crafting CPU, and the first twelve new tungstensteel bars reached storage. Keep returning the clean tungstate order's lithium to the tough-alloy line.

One EV electrolyzer needs 896 ticks for one tungsten from tungstate. Producing the 112 bars for these plates alone occupies it for about 84 minutes before interruptions. Scheelite takes 1,120 ticks and remains a fallback. Each charge uses seven buckets of hydrogen. The existing HV water cell and its level emitter have delivered hydrogen to ME, with another 64 buckets fitting in the EV input tank. Check that supply under sustained load. Do not size a new gas plant from the tank's starting level.

Give pearl and polymer production a second EV electrolyzer at (20,83,−10). Its recipe needs an EV hull, four single platinum wires, glass, one aluminium cable and two Extreme circuits. Eleven of the four-output Quantumprocessors were available and qualify. An IV electrolyzer instead needs an IV hull, osmium wires and two Elite circuits, all shared with the crystal engraver. The new socket is beside the platinum spur powered by the existing four-amp EV converter. Its north interface can connect to the ME cable at (18,83,−11) through three roof segments. The first milestone is a pearl charge running here while the original machine finishes tungsten. Observe the converter under both loads before putting a second hydrogen export on this branch.
