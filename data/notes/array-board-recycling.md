# Recycling the board reagents

The HV reactor at (10,82,1) took 2,000 mB each of allyl chloride and hypochlorous acid. Five sodium hydroxide dust were available. Waiting for a sixth had sent the workshop back into the same supply request. Three dust are enough to start one charge. It produced 1,000 mB epichlorohydrin and 1,000 mB salt water. The remaining pair of liquid inputs can stay in the reactor.

JEI gives three sodium hydroxide dust, 1,000 mB hydrogen and 1,000 mB chlorine for each 1,000 mB salt water in an LV electrolyzer. The first salt water has left the HV reactor. Collect the hydroxide from (4,81,6) and use it for the second charge. The plant can retain the salt-water output as working stock for the next batch. scripts/board_chlorination.py handles the split charge. The coating reactor at (5,81,-5) reached 6,000 mB sulfuric acid, enough for 48 boards before the plastic-board order.

The MV assembler now has an ME pattern for two Small Coils from one steel bolt and one fine copper wire. The reusable bolt shape in the MV extruder made 32 bolts from four steel ingots on its first run. The bolts and finished boards are returned to ME so all three crafting CPUs can see them.

The recycle worked. Three recovered dust started the second charge, and 2,000 mB epichlorohydrin reached ME. With the old 424 mB reserve, the HV reactor took 2,424 mB. The refinery delivered its fifth 12,000 mB naphtha batch. Of the 60,000 mB produced, the first 16,000 have gone to the HV reactor. The MV chamber took 15,000 mB nitrogen and 16,000 mB oxygen and produced nitrogen dioxide. ME also confirmed 80 Small Coils and 70 SMD Transistors. Sixteen PVC sheets and sixteen copper foils entered the coating reactor for the plastic circuits used by the next machines.

The extra epichlorohydrin still needed for forty boards comes from the tower's much larger light-fuel fraction. The naphtha reactor rejected sulfuric light fuel while it held sulfuric naphtha. The portable tank safely returned all 12,000 mB to ME. A second MV reactor is being staged under the refinery mixer at (11,81,-11), beside the existing fourfold copper cable. Desulfurize the reserved light fuel there, return excess hydrogen, then use steam from the 16,000 mB boiler drum. The program-seven still already exists. One bucket of propene takes four buckets of cracked light fuel. The alternative seed-oil route needs 600 ordinary seeds for a single glycerol bucket. The light-fuel route uses a fraction already accumulating from the board order.

The JEI naphtha resin recipe itself only needs 30 EU/t. Titanium tetrachloride needs 480 EU/t. For sustained board production, a dedicated MV resin reactor near the caster would free the HV reactor for titanium and leave room to automate all three resin fluids. Page 730 recommends stocked fluid interfaces with pumps or regulators when scaling fluid delivery to many machines. The installed chemistry interface and native oxygen conduit already show how to share a connection between producers.
