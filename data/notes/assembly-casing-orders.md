# Four more casing orders after the first delivery

At 87h19m I found three Assembler Machine Casings in ME and ten Assembly Line Casings. The fabricator has delivered its first batch using seven upgraded Quantum Processors. Eleven casings go into the structure and four into the controller. Four more three-casing crafts will cover both. The two Quantum Processor Arrays in storage belong to the first mainframe.

Together these draw 49 multilayer boards, 77 QBits, 21 Nano CPUs, 210 fine platinum wire, 154 SMD transistors, 84 capacitors, 112 small coils and 112 diodes. The four remaining IV arms for rails and controller add to that bill. The alternative Nano Processor Array uses two tungstensteel plates and four Nano Processor Assemblies per elite circuit. With both Quantum Processor patterns working and 54 platinum ingots in stock, keep the IV recipes and increase batch sizes.

The new targets are twelve finished boards, thirty cut QBits and twenty-seven washed ore pairs. The LV macerator can grind galena while the HV mill makes tungsten. At 87h25m a crafting CPU was waiting for one copper cable. The wrapper had its wire and program 24, but only 12 mB of silicone rubber. The silicone reactor had 64 buckets of methanol, one of hydrochloric acid and ten of diluted hydrochloric acid in its outlet. Another six diluted buckets were in storage. The recovery still held two buckets of diluted sulfuric acid on program zero, one short of a complete charge. In this situation bank the partial charge, distill the diluted hydrochloric acid on program one and let the existing export bus feed the silicone reactor. Check the wrapper and CPU after recovery.

The still returned to work after its two-bucket partial charge went to storage. Ten buckets of diluted hydrochloric acid came out of the silicone reactor, and nine fresh polymer dust went back in with sulfur. The wrapper now has 1,140 mB of silicone, both cable orders finished, and ME holds two IV motors. The grinder and washer supplied 27 purified galena while the grinder switched to six sphalerite ore. The expanded MV wiremill has both platinum patterns. With 54 platinum ingots available it can order 64 fine wires without first waiting for loose single wires.
