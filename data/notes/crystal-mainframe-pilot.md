# First tier-eight circuit

Page 535 pays an OmniNickel for one Crystal Processor Mainframe and opens the ZPM Assembling Machine on page 533. The ZPM assembler takes two tier-eight circuits. Its example Wetware Supercomputers share the grade with this mainframe. The quest book advises moving to the cheaper wetware line once the assembler is commissioned.

The ten inputs, in pack script order, are 48 SMD Resistors, one HPIC, 36 SMD Transistors, 32 SMD Capacitors, 24 SMD Diodes, four Prescient Crystals, eight Crystaltine Components, two Crystal Processor Arrays, four HSS-E Frames and eight single Enderium Wires. The solder hatch takes 1,440 mB. The controller needs 15 million EU at 30,000 EU/t for 500 ticks. Keep its other high-draw machines idle through the pilot. Stage all ten stacks in storage before tying up the line's individual buses. The two eight-ingredient arrays can use its existing blocking ME interface. The ten-ingredient mainframe uses the physical loader until the extended processing pattern terminal is available.

The four HSS-E Frames need five rods and three plates. The bench makes four with a reusable wrench. The assembler makes the same four with a selector. Eight HSS-E rods and three cold ingots were present at the 123h16m survey. The Soul Binder is already commissioned. Four Prescient Crystals take four Vibrant Crystals and four Shulker vials. Sixteen of the 162 stocked Shulker Shells can fill four vials, and the binder returns each empty vial. Two Crystaltine ingots were already made on the elite table. Each finished component also takes Luminessence, a Black Steel Plate and an Osmiridium Plate.

Four cold Osmiridium Ingots are already available. A further four need three Iridium Ingots, one Osmium Ingot and one bucket of helium at 1,920 EU/t. The east furnace and common freezer handled the first lot. ME held 23 buckets of helium. Fit the cold-output pattern while a sample is still in stock. Continue running the first HSS-E motor fittings alongside the frame bill.

The workshop made the full set of four HSS-E Frames. The first filled Shulker vial and Vibrant Crystal reached the Soul Binder. Its XP input is the remaining requirement for that pair. Overworldian Matter grants ten XP per use according to its in-game tooltip.

At 124h33m the binder had returned two Prescient Crystals. It also returned the empty vial, and 154 Shulker Shells remain. Two more bindings finish the first mainframe bill. The second array has two Flow Circuits and all six Crystal Circuits for its last two. Their QBit order has reached the EV wafer reactor. Its ingredients are already in the machine, so a dry radon inlet takes priority even when ME reports no loose phosphide.

At 124h42m the north Assembly Line returned the second Crystal Processor Array. Both are in storage for page 535. After the array consumed its inputs the mainframe lacked eight SMD Capacitors, four SMD Diodes, eight Crystaltine Components, two Enderium Wires, two Prescient Crystals and one HPIC. The EV reactor has the two phosphide dust needed for a High-Power IC Wafer in storage alongside a Power IC Wafer. The apatite reactor has its full 10,000 mB sulfuric charge for the Luminessence line.

At 124h46m all four kinds of SMD and all eight wires were ready. One gallium dust split into four small piles. Four fine platinum wires, one small pile and 288 mB polyethylene made 32 diodes. Thin PVC sheets and aluminium foil from the networked cluster mill supplied 16 capacitors. The workshop now holds 52 diodes and 40 capacitors. The remaining bill is eight Crystaltine Components, two Prescient Crystals and one HPIC.

At 124h54m the starter reactor held 6,000 mB phosphoric acid after two apatite runs, and the workshop collected 80 gypsum. The finishing reactor needs 2,000 mB acid, one Energetic Blend and one Aluminium Dust per four Luminessence. Four blend and one dust are ready. JEI also shows one spare aluminium plate mills to one dust for the second batch. The extractor returned the full 288 mB molten red alloy for the HPIC wafer to ME. The additional four cold Osmiridium Ingots are in stock for plates. Reserve eight diamond blocks for power when unpacking blocks for Crystaltine.

At 125h00m the workshop had three finished Crystaltine Components. The first four Luminessence came from one blend and one aluminium dust. One of the nine diamond blocks supplied the third Crystaltine Ingot, leaving eight blocks for power. The five remaining components require 20 star nuggets, with six loose and no spare star after crafting the Wither model. Two more crafted stars yield eighteen nuggets. One block of nine Luminessence funds a four-core batch, in addition to the five pieces then needed by the components. The saw has 41 Exquisite Diamonds in its supply, of which sixteen are reserved for lattice. Two were cut into four Flawless Diamonds. The EV reactor returned another High-Power IC Wafer after the portable tank banked its 950 mB residual radon. The Vibrant Alloy furnace order has begun returning ingots.

At 125h15m all four Prescient Crystals and two HPICs were in stock. The only remaining first-mainframe ingredient is four Crystaltine Components. Four components have already been crafted. ME returned two each of the west, east and south Nether Star tips for the remaining metals. The north tips need Aerotheum Dust. One block of Luminessence suffices for both cores. The MV chemistry cell has a configuration-two circuit and oxygen for the parallel phosphorus route. JEI gives 2,000 mB phosphoric acid from two phosphorus dust, 3,000 mB water and 5,000 mB oxygen. The apatite route in the other cell remains useful whenever sulfate is available.

At 125h19m the first new Nether Star was in storage alongside three unused cores. JEI and the delivered surplus both confirm that the core recipe yields four. This corrects the earlier eighteen-Luminessence estimate. Each of the four tip recipes yields one. The second star already has its east, west and south tips. There were also 163 loose diamonds, enough for the next four Crystaltine Ingots.

At 125h27m both new Nether Stars had arrived. Two elite-table crafts used 16 diamonds, 20 lapis, eight star nuggets, eight iron and four gold. The result went directly into the fifth and sixth Crystaltine Components. One more batch of Luminessence and sixteen more lapis cover the last pair. The north solder hatch reached 1,224 mB. The charged north line reports the expected 30,000 EU/t for 500 ticks.

At 125h38m the north Assembly Line completed the first Crystal Processor Mainframe. The eighth component used the final elite-table ingot and one of the four new Luminessence. All ten item buses were loaded in recipe order. The controller consumed 1,440 mB of solder and ran for 500 ticks at 30,000 EU/t. The IV converter began with 40,262,144 EU, still held 28,787,064 at tick 428, and held 26,628,472 after completion. The work samples showed no power shortfall. One mainframe appeared in ME storage. Keep it for the ZPM assembler, which takes two. The second can use the same loader while the [extended pattern terminal](extended-processing-terminal.md) is being funded.

At 125h41m page 535 detected the mainframe and paid one OmniNickel. The UV Circuits reference page also completed and the ZPM Assembling Machine page opened. Astra now has 424 of 644 chapter quests complete.

At 126h05m the second mainframe had three Crystaltine Ingots. A new Nether Star supplied the nuggets for two elite-table crafts. The first of its two arrays already has the Draconium plates, Enderium wires and high-power integrated circuit. The roof assembler has 560 mB of solder. Four Crystal Circuits are ready, but the next Energy Flow Circuit needs six fine Niobium-Titanium Wires and the workshop has two. Use the six niobium dust in storage and the existing titanium line. Keep making four-output Crystal Circuit batches while the alloy cools. The read-only crystal status now reports the remaining star parts and table ingredients during the second mainframe as well.

At 126h43m the elite table had the gold, iron, lapis and star nuggets for two more Crystaltine Ingots. Fourteen Flawless Diamonds could yield 56 diamonds in the EV cutter. Previous cutter deliveries had reached the east dynamo before the table could take its sixteen. When the east queue has at least 48 diamonds, withdraw up to sixteen directly into the player's bag for fully funded table recipes. Leave at least 32 diamonds in that dynamo and keep all eight emergency diamond blocks. This also leaves the wafer cutter free for the last QBit CPU.

At 126h55m the elite table returned two more Crystaltine Ingots after a 16-diamond withdrawal from the east dynamo. It still held 33 immediately after the transfer. Five of eight ingots are ready. Two Prescient Crystals and all four Energy Flow Circuits for the next array have reached storage.

At 127h37m the two arrays and the HPIC for the second mainframe were ready. Two bucket transfers brought the Assembly Line to 2,056 mB solder. Seven Crystaltine Ingots are finished. The eighth needs four star nuggets and the workshop holds three. The Wither model cannot supply a star in this pack. Keep the elemental dusts for the four star tips ahead of the next Primal Mana batch while both furnace hatches have mana.

At 127h52m the elite table had returned the eighth Crystaltine Ingot. Nine apatite dust produced three buckets of phosphoric acid and 40 gypsum dust. The light reactor used that acid and its existing bucket to make all eight Luminessence from two Energetic Blend and two aluminium dust. The compressor returned the other four Osmiridium Plates. Use the resonator for Red Coal while the mills supply steel and electrum dust.
