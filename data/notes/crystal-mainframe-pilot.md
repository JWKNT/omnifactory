# First tier-eight circuit

Page 535 pays an OmniNickel for one Crystal Processor Mainframe and opens the ZPM Assembling Machine on page 533. The ZPM assembler takes two tier-eight circuits. Its example Wetware Supercomputers share the grade with this mainframe. The quest book advises moving to the cheaper wetware line once the assembler is commissioned.

The ten inputs, in pack script order, are 48 SMD Resistors, one HPIC, 36 SMD Transistors, 32 SMD Capacitors, 24 SMD Diodes, four Prescient Crystals, eight Crystaltine Components, two Crystal Processor Arrays, four HSS-E Frames and eight single Enderium Wires. The solder hatch takes 1,440 mB. The controller needs 15 million EU at 30,000 EU/t for 500 ticks. Keep its other high-draw machines idle through the pilot. Stage all ten stacks in storage before tying up the line's individual buses. The two eight-ingredient arrays can use its existing blocking ME interface. The ten-ingredient mainframe uses the physical loader until the extended processing pattern terminal is available.

The four HSS-E Frames need five rods and three plates. The bench makes four with a reusable wrench. The assembler makes the same four with a selector. Eight HSS-E rods and three cold ingots were present at the 123h16m survey. The Soul Binder is already commissioned. Four Prescient Crystals take four Vibrant Crystals and four Shulker vials. Sixteen of the 162 stocked Shulker Shells can fill four vials, and the binder returns each empty vial. Two Crystaltine ingots were already made on the elite table. Each finished component also takes Luminessence, a Black Steel Plate and an Osmiridium Plate.

Four cold Osmiridium Ingots are already available. A further four need three Iridium Ingots, one Osmium Ingot and one bucket of helium at 1,920 EU/t. The east furnace and common freezer handled the first lot. ME held 23 buckets of helium. Fit the cold-output pattern while a sample is still in stock. Continue running the first HSS-E motor fittings alongside the frame bill.

The workshop made the full set of four HSS-E Frames. The first filled Shulker vial and Vibrant Crystal reached the Soul Binder. Its XP input is the remaining requirement for that pair. Overworldian Matter grants ten XP per use according to its in-game tooltip.
