# Phenol and the first tier-three circuits

The first cut boule supplies enough integrated circuits for four Electronic Processor Arrays: each takes two of the chips. Plan the first three arrays for the MV Assembling Machine (two advanced circuits) and eight AE2 blank patterns (one advanced circuit). Both crafting recipes accept an Electronic Processor Array in the circuitAdvanced slot. The Patterns page also requires the ME Interface, Molecular Assembler, Crafting Unit and plastic sheets.

The older LV electronics route needs phenol. JEI gives one Phenolic Circuit Board from a Coated Circuit Board and 100 mB phenol. Each LV-assembled Basic Electronic Circuit takes a board, four resistors, four fine copper wire, two capacitors and 144 mB molten tin. Three Basic Electronic Circuits, four resistors, four fine copper wires, a transistor and another 144 mB tin give an Electronic Processor. Four of those, two resistors, two capacitors, sixteen fine electrum wires, two integrated circuit chips, two aluminium plates and another 144 mB tin give an Electronic Processor Array. For three arrays budget 36 boards and 51 tin ingots for solder across these three stages. Soldering Alloy is an alternate fluid at half the volume.

Use the Pyrolyse Oven for the first phenol. Sixteen coal or coal dust, 4,000 mB steam and circuit setting 1 give 1,000 mB phenol plus twenty coke or coke dust in 600 ticks at 30 EU/t. Four batches cover the 36 boards with 400 mB left. Wood tar through a Distillery costs 64 EU/t and yields 75 mB per bucket. The pyrolyse oven avoids that additional MV load. The book checks the controller, sixteen Cupronickel Coil Blocks, twelve ULV Machine Casings and the five different LV hatches and buses together (quest 22, one quarter). Build a separate set of coils to keep the blast furnace available. The sixteen coils cost 128 cupronickel ingots after wiremilling.

The live multiblock preview is three wide, three high and four deep. A controller facing north has the item input and steam hatch on its west and east. The rear face carries the item output, energy hatch and fluid output. Keep the two middle cells open. The northern deck has room east of the planned plastics row. A second LV Fluid Extractor can go at (4,80,8) below the existing LV assembler, adjacent to the zero-loss LV cable at (3,80,8). Put its fluid output upward for molten tin. The fluid extractor on the MV reactor can keep producing molten ender. These installations are still ahead.
