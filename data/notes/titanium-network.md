# Connecting the titanium reactor and blast hatch

The last hour's transfer record had eight titanium loads at the compressor and seven paired loads of rutile and carbon at the HV reactor. The new MV press has its own interface. For fluids, the HV reactor at (10,82,1) already touches an ME glass cable directly above it. A Fluid Import Bus on that cable's lower face can collect both titanium tetrachloride and carbon monoxide. The latter is a feed for methanol as well as a source of carbon and oxygen. Verify the new fluids in ME before relying on this return.

The shortest clear path I surveyed from the network to the east face of the north blast hatch starts at the furnace interface (10,83,-5). Four glass cables run east to (14,83,-5), two descend to (14,81,-5), one goes south and one descends to (14,80,-4). This leaves the cobblestone stairs intact. Fit a Fluid Export Bus facing west and set its filter with a titanium tetrachloride bucket. Sample its hatch before and after a fresh reactor charge and keep oxygen on its separate line. scripts/titanium_fluid_return.py and scripts/titanium_hatch_export.py stage these steps. At the time of this note neither bus had passed a delivery test.

The fluid buses each need two lapis plates, one MV piston and one AE2 core. One lapis ore smelts to six dye, each of which presses to one plate. For the reactor's solid reagents, the polymer kilns already proved an LV robot arm can keep specified amounts of two different inputs. A second stocked interface alongside the reactor would allow the same design. A Limited Item Filter instead takes an Advanced Item Filter built with a Z-Logic Controller. Its Slice'N'Splice quest comes after tungstensteel. Prioritize the fluid connection and retain the paired solid loads until the extra interface is funded.

The downward Fluid Import Bus is installed and active. Holding sneak for five ticks before aiming at the visible top edge of the reactor let it attach around the cable. After a three-reaction charge, the fluid terminal network held 1,000 mB titanium tetrachloride and 6,000 mB carbon monoxide. The reactor output tanks emptied. This is the first verified direct fluid return. Eight magnesium dust went into the north blast input for four titanium ingots. The hatch export still needs its cable, bus and filter.

The first two segments of the blast export branch at (11,83,-5) and (12,83,-5) are installed using spare Ender IO ME conduits. The last segment must be AE glass cable to hold the filtered part. The ME cable pattern can make the remaining glass cables. A future processing pattern can use three rutile and two carbon dust for one titanium ingot once tetrachloride delivery succeeds. A dedicated interface at (9,82,1) touches the HV reactor on its west face and the existing ME cable above. This would let a single CPU order span chlorination, smelting and cooling. Leave the current solder and SMD assembler interface on its existing recipe directions. The LV electrolyzer can now use carbon monoxide from storage whenever the gas bank has at least 24 buckets and carbon stock is below 32; the eight-bucket remainder is reserved for methanol.

The full eight-block branch is installed. Three Ender IO segments and five fluix cables connect the north blast hatch to storage. ME made six cables from the wiremill's four quartz fibers and three fluix dust. The final cable at (14,80,-4) attached to the cobblestone floor. Two lapis plates, one MV piston and a Formation Core made the Fluid Export Bus. I mounted it on the hatch's east face, took a one-bucket filter sample from the fluid terminal and set the filter in the bus GUI. Before returning the sample, ME dropped from 3,000 mB to zero and the hatch rose from empty to 2,000 mB while the furnace ran. The verified bus takes over all subsequent tetrachloride deliveries. The remaining routine player inputs are rutile and carbon at the HV reactor. The next expansion is an ME processing interface there.

The proposed item interface has another ME Interface to its west and a CEF to its south. Use an east-facing flat ME Interface on fluix cable at (9,82,1), pointing into the reactor. scripts/titanium_order_interface.py stages it with the next interface order. After fitting it, encode three rutile dust and two carbon dust for one cold titanium ingot. Verify that a CPU order returns the finished ingot and the magnesium cycle closes before retiring the hand loader. Fluid stock surveys now read the installed chemistry terminal's live storage list. Withdrawals and deposits still use its GUI.
