# Element quests from ore-processing by-products

At 82h29m the factory held 327 manganese dust, 191 calcium dust and 109 cobalt dust. Each has a one-dust-to-one-ingot furnace recipe in this instance. Pages 637, 632 and 639 each ask to see 64 ingots and pay one OmniQuarter. Use the Multi Smelter while the IV circuit and motor jobs run. Its return already delivers galena and other smelted products to ME. Preserve at least 32 dust of each metal for upcoming recipes, particularly manganese for stainless steel. Stage one element at a time, show the finished stack in the quest book, and keep the ingots. The three rewards can help fund more ore without taking materials from the Assembly Line.

By 82h44m all three pages were claimed in the book. The Multi Smelter processed each stack as two batches of 32 at 64 EU/t for 128 ticks apiece. Each page awarded one OmniQuarter and left the ingots in inventory. That brings the book to 347 of 644 chapter quests.

Leave zinc for a later pass, since its 48 dust do not yet cover a stack.
