# Feeding the first MV assembler build

The two Electronic Processor Arrays for the Advanced Assembling Machine are reserved in the network. The harder part of its bill is ten MV motors spread across the robot arms, pistons and conveyors. The bench and GregTech assembler motor recipes both use four double copper wires, two insulated copper cables, two aluminium rods and one magnetic steel rod per motor. Use the bench while the solder assembler makes vacuum tubes and resistors. The first motor and MV machine casing reached storage.

The lower macerator doubled 29 copper ores and the diamond hammer converted the crushed ore to impure copper dust. Once 38 dust were ready together, the furnace tender split them into 16, 16 and 6 so the wiremill could start without waiting for an entire stack to smelt. The cutter finished two nine-plate aluminium charges. The blast furnace finished its first 21-ingot charge and the last three dust were collected for the next charge. The lathe made all 28 aluminium rods. The compressor took a batch of 59 rubber for sheets.

The AE2 CPU delivered eight glass tubes and then another six for this build. It also delivered a Basic Circuit. scripts/workshop_status.py shows the current MV motor, arm, piston, conveyor and hull stocks while the order is active. See ae2-pattern-road.md for the five installed patterns and blast-feeder.md for the independent LV supply.

The last three aluminium ingots came out of the blast furnace. Both MV pistons, one conveyor module and the MV hull have been crafted. The AE2 crafting CPU has now delivered a Primitive Processor on demand as well. The assembler order is still active.
