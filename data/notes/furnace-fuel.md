# Keeping the four workshop furnaces burning

The first 32 uraninite ore finished firing, but the second firing stopped with 32 Uraninite Dust and no coal, charcoal or cane available. Coal is also an ingredient in steel and model mesh. When it runs out, scripts/coal_reserve.py can use five pennies for 32 coal ore and turn two grove logs into eight planks. In the recovery batch the workshop used its three existing planks as well. Sixteen coal ore went into the east furnace with wood. The first coal out started the uraninite's second firing in the west and north furnaces.

scripts/smelt_stock.py can collect its own input from another furnace and split a batch across two free furnaces. It handles coal, charcoal, reeds and jungle planks. The factory order runner collects coal during the firing if the next order needs fuel. It can buy more ore when the reserve runs low. scripts/ore_purchase.py now breaks a quarter into five nickels, or a nickel into five pennies, as required by the actual ore recipe.

The iron route in scripts/lv_order.py checks stored ore and crushed stock. One normal ore makes two crushed ore in the macerator. Hammer each into impure dust, then fire it into an iron ingot. Use this for iron shortages while a bigger order is running.

The engraver order bought 32 more iron ore while the macerator doubled copper. The planner can now use an available ordinary furnace for up to eight spare raw iron, copper or tin ore when the mill is occupied with different material. It keeps at least eight raw ore and the entire planned doubling allocation, and gives preference to dust already ready for firing. In this instance each of these raw ores smelts directly into one ingot.

After the engraver is installed, the next factory order also builds one Basic Electric Furnace. The planned socket is under the 4x Conductive Iron Cable at (5,80,6), on the existing cobblestone floor. This adds a fuel-free lane alongside the four ordinary furnaces. The installed mod defines a base 128-tick, 4 EU/t smelting recipe before overclocking. The workshop planner can load it, count its queued output and collect it for another operation. Physical installation and the first smelt are still pending.

The Basic Electric Furnace is installed below the live LV cable at (5,80,6). It reached 1,024 EU. The cobalt brass charge for the first cutter had already finished in the east coal furnace, producing six ingots. The renewable dust line can now load up to eight Resonant Clathrate into the electric furnace when it is available. The regular smelting order uses the same lane for metals.

For the first processor batch, one nickel paid for 32 coal ore. Eight jungle planks fired twelve ore and yielded twelve coal. The remaining twenty ore went into metal overflow. The new electric furnace handled 24 sand while two ordinary furnaces fired five redstone ore and nine impure iron dust. During ore or terracotta work, the crafting mortar can convert cobblestone to gravel and gravel to sand. Each machine conversion takes only sixteen ticks when the macerator is free. A Thermal Clastic Deposition augment costs four mana-infused ingots and four plates, so it can wait.

Small redstone orders were firing a single ore for six dust and leaving most of a coal burn unused. The furnace planner now allows up to eight ore per redstone batch when the ore reserve permits. Keep eight raw ore when making extra dust. The electric furnace reports its newly loaded quantity to the four-furnace dispatch in the same round, so the other furnaces can use their slots on different materials. Inputs specifically held for a quest or final assembly stay available.

The first order after installing the Drive fired eight redstone ore and put 48 redstone dust into storage. Thirteen raw redstone ore remained. That is a full coal-sized batch with the intended raw reserve.

The void workshop now has eight vanilla furnaces. Four new furnaces sit directly above the original row at x10 through x13, y81, z1. The cobblestone drawer supplied the 32 blocks for all four. The furnace survey verified both rows, and the first upper furnace accepted 32 slimeballs with four coal. Another 32 slimeballs and eight impure tin dust went into lower furnaces at the same time. The furnace picker, collectors and oven-order planner all use the same list of installed sockets. Large orders can split across up to four free furnaces.

The oven order currently calls for over a hundred iron plates as well as over a hundred wrought iron plates. The MV cutter has 815 mB water left from wafer work. Nine iron or wrought ingots make a block at the bench; the cutter takes 42 mB plain water per block and returns nine plates. The planner can split large ingot stocks between the cutter and compressor. Small batches still go straight to the compressor. The MV cutter has now taken its first block for the oven order. Its output slot held all nine iron plates. The replacement hammer also turned a stack of 25 crushed iron ore into 25 impure iron dust in the ME Crafting Terminal.

Sixteen lignite ore were still on the shelves in three host rocks. The live furnace registry gives the smelted Lignite Coal 1,200 burn ticks, enough for six ordinary smelts. When coal is low and at least three ordinary furnaces are free, the planner can fire this stock in the electric furnace. Ordinary furnaces can use it for metal dust and wrought iron while the regular coal stays available for coke.

The Pyrolyse Oven's coal recipe yields twenty coke per bucket of phenol. The furnace registry reports 3,200 burn ticks per coke, enough to smelt sixteen items. The furnace bank can now use coke from the oven on larger charges and leave raw coal for the next phenol run. It keeps four coke in stock because the mortar and centrifuge can convert each into one carbon dust. No quest asks to hold the coke itself.
