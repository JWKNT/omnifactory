# What the first diamond dynamo costs

Quest 781 checks the Numismatic Dynamo and its Lapidary Calibration augment together. The augment needs eight diamond plates and one emerald. The LV compressor makes each plate from one diamond. The dynamo needs a Diamond Furnace; the upgrade from a Gold Furnace takes a further eight diamonds. Reserve at least seventeen diamonds across the Shulker model, furnace and augment, plus the first diamonds to burn. A Shulker pristine yields six diamonds. The existing steam plant can continue powering the workshop while the new system starts.

The dynamo also needs two vibrant plates and two Vibrant Bimetal Gears. Each gear takes four vibrant ingots and an Energized Bimetal Gear, which itself takes four energetic ingots and an Infinity Bimetal Gear. Total alloy budget is ten vibrant ingots and eight energetic ingots. Two vibrant ingots are banked. Four of the six banked energetic ingots are reserved for the Loot Fabricator. With that reservation, make fourteen more Energetic Alloy, then convert eight of those to vibrant using eight pearls. This is in addition to pearls for data-model progression. The Excitation Coil needs five red alloy plates and two Redstone Conductance Coils. Use the JEI output with the furnace and gears when crafting the basic dynamo, since several tier conversion recipes also show up for the same item ID.

For later obsidian, quest 352 suggests a Fluid Solidifier. Its block mold turns 1000 mB of lava into one obsidian in 1024 ticks at 16 EU/t. The LV Chemical Reactor makes 1000 mB lava from a magma block in 120 ticks at 30 EU/t. Pristine Blaze Matter can make 32 magma blocks in the Loot Fabricator. Before that model is available, the camp pool is still the earliest source for the second dark steel hull.
