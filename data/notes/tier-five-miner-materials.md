# Tier Five and the breeder share a tungsten budget

Page 252 needs the Iridium Heavy Plating quest. The plate quest also requires the LuV Compressor. Its recipe consumes two ordinary iridium plates at 15,000 EU/t. Keep at least 24 iridium ingots' worth from the first medium shipment for the twelve heavy plates. That shipment also pays for the smaller Osmiridium alloy sample and osmium rods needed by the IV emitter and sensor.

JEI lists six more Signalum Heavy Plating, three Vibrant Thrusters, two Advanced Micro Miner Guidance Systems, two EV Field Generators, two Signalum engine cores, one Supercharged Laser Array, one Basic Quantum Chest, one Fission Controller and one Fission Reactor Port in the 7×7 Tier Five ship. The new EV alloy smelter will therefore make at least four Signalum cores across the two ships. Keep its processing pattern installed. Keep the IV plate press and the separate four-amp EV chemistry branch in operation when the LuV press goes in.

Page 323, Naquadah Ingots, also depends on page 350, Fission Reactor. A controller alone only covers one component of that page. The full breeder bill includes 116 tungsten plates and at least 148 Tough Alloy. Scheelite and tungstate have no OmniCoin recipe. Once the Tier Three gemstone flight has used its fuel and its cargo is home, reserve another Tier Two ore flight for the breeder and LuV machines unless an accessible vein or a later mission has supplied the shortfall. Its proven shipment is 192 ore of each tungsten mineral. The HV mill and centrifuge give better ore utilization than immediate smelting. See the breeder alloy and first cargo notes for the individual recipes.

The LuV Compressor itself takes a LuV hull, two LuV pistons, two insulated niobium-titanium cables and two Master circuits. JEI accepts Quantum Processor Arrays and Nanoprocessor Mainframes in those slots. The Assembly Line needs 64 HSS-G rounds, twelve plates, eight rings, eight rods, four small gears, two full gears, two LuV motors, eight YBCO cables, 288 mB solder and 500 mB lubricant for the two pistons. Reuse the existing motor order and piston bus sequence. At 106h53m the workshop had five small gears, four rings and two plates. This is a reason to restart HSS-G and YBCO batches after the first crystal cargo, before the twelve iridium heavy plates are due.

The east furnace can run all three first metals. The live recipes require 2,719 K for iridium, 3,306 K for osmium, and 2,900 K for Osmiridium. The alloy heat consumes three iridium ingots, one osmium ingot and one bucket of helium at 1,920 EU/t for 500 ticks. It yields four hot alloy ingots. The east MV fluid hatch was empty at 106h55m. Deliver one helium bucket once the metals are ready. Hold five more buckets for the 5,000 K engraved chip heat. The common freezer returns all three cold metals.

The LuV press quest is already open and pays a dollar. Its piston fittings and pilot now have a separate order in scripts/iridium_press.py. It reuses the prior Assembly Line's bus layout and program, and can work on the HSS-G rounds and rings while the first ore samples run. The first cargo is home. The segment from its two initial iridium ore through four crushed ore to four impure piles has been observed in ME. The full stack pages 654 and 655 each pay a quarter. Route surplus iridium through those pages before making later hulls and plates.

With the gemstone cargo home, the next tungsten ship has first call on two Reinforced Mining Lasers. After it departs, build a third laser for the Supercharged Laser Array. Its EV reactor charge uses one bucket of radon at 2,000 EU/t for 200 ticks. The workshop waits for the indium reactor to clear and preserves radon for QBit wafers. The two advanced guidance systems together need four EV sensors, six carbide heavy plates and eight Extreme circuits. The pair of EV field generators adds two nether stars, eight Extreme circuits and eight single End Steel wires by the grid recipe. Each Vibrant Thruster adds four Vibrant Alloy plates, one Vibrant Energy Conduit, a Prescient Crystal and two Red Alloy plates around an Energetic Thruster. Keep four Good circuits, one MV field generator, three aluminium plates and an MV hull for the Basic Quantum Chest. Budget 172 Tough Alloy for the breeder including its twelve coolers.
