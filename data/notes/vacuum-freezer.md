# Reserve the west bay for the Vacuum Freezer

The three-by-three floor at x−5 through −3, z7 through 9 is cobblestone at y79. All 27 spaces above it at y80 through 82 were air. The blast furnace output bus at (0,80,8) faces west. The two spaces at (−1,80,8) and (−2,80,8) are clear. Put the freezer input on its east wall at (−3,80,8), then connect the two buses with item conduit filtered for hot ingots. Stainless steel and the other cold blast products should stay in the output bus. Leave a path around the connection. No freezer parts are installed.

Build with fluid hatches from the beginning. The quest book and the three-by-three JEI layout call for twenty frost-proof casings with both item buses, both fluid hatches, a power hatch and the freezer controller. The controller consumes another casing. Seven casing crafts give all 21. They use 42 aluminium plates and seven aluminium frames. Two frame crafts give eight frames for six more plates and ten rods. Reserve 53 aluminium ingots for those 48 plates and ten lathed rods, apart from the hatches and pumps. The MV input hatch takes an MV hull and a single copper cable; 128 V can cover the 120 EU/t Kanthal cooling recipe. Check shared power under load before commissioning.

The controller also consumes three HV electric pumps, two insulated gold cables and three EV circuits. This is three Refined Processor Mainframes in addition to the two planned for the HV assembler. The first mainframe unlocks quest 150; the completed freezer controller pays one quarter. Keep a stack of cooled Kanthal together for quest 602 before turning it into wire.
