# A first batch of plastic for AE2 patterns

Keep twenty surplus rubber saplings for the brewery. In this instance each sapling with 100 mB water gives 100 mB Biomass. The single-block distillery with circuit 0 converts each 1,000 mB Biomass to 700 mB Ethanol at 30 EU/t for 240 ticks. Two distillations supply one full bucket of Ethanol and leave 400 mB toward the next batch. Ordinary biomass gives much more ethanol per bucket here than fermented biomass; use the tower later for the latter's other products.

An LV Chemical Reactor combines sulfur dust and 4,000 mB water to supply 1,000 mB Sulfuric Acid and 6,000 mB Hydrogen. Keep the hydrogen output clear. One bucket each of acid and ethanol gives 1,000 mB Ethylene plus 1,000 mB Diluted Sulfuric Acid at 120 EU/t for 1,200 ticks. Give the plastics line its own MV reactor once the quartz and pearl line is busy.

The LV polymerization recipe uses circuit 0. Each 144 mB Ethylene and 1,000 mB Oxygen yield 216 mB Polyethylene. Water electrolysis can supply 1,000 mB Oxygen per bucket at 30 EU/t for 1,500 ticks, also giving 2,000 mB Hydrogen. Air is another option at a lower polymer yield. Six oxygen batches turn 864 mB Ethylene into 1,296 mB Polyethylene. A plate mold in the Fluid Solidifier makes nine sheets from that quantity. For the first dedicated factory, lay out separate fluid inputs, product tanks and byproduct drains; leave the water supply for paper and clay in operation.

The LV reactor made its first bucket of Sulfuric Acid from one stored sulfur dust and 4,000 mB from the workshop water pipe. It took 1,150 ticks. The bucket completed quest 94 and paid a nickel. The other output tank holds 6,000 mB hydrogen. The acid bucket is reserved for the MV ethylene reaction. scripts/first_acid.py locates the output by the fluid displayed in the open GUI.

For the Brewery (504) and single-block Distillery (546), budget one blaze rod each. In Peaceful the workshop can make both rods from two bones and four Hellish Matter. Make the matter from four netherrack and sixteen Overworldian Matter. Two mined tricalcium phosphate ores give six dust in an ordinary furnace. Five dust in the electrolyzer give three calcium dust and two phosphate dust. Compress two calcium into bone meal, then use one additional Overworldian Matter to craft 22 bones. The Slime model has started a measured sixteen-polymer run to add to the eight Overworldian Matter already stocked. This leaves the Shulker model available after the batch and avoids spending the first rods on another data model.

The existing LV electrolyzer at (4,81,6) accepted six buckets of water through its GUI. Its first three cycles delivered 3,000 mB oxygen, with hydrogen in a second tank. Leave the oxygen in the machine for the polyethylene line. scripts/reactor_bucket.py now handles the electrolyzer by name. The other workshop machines kept producing parts during electrolysis.
