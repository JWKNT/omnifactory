# Hydrogen from the clay workshop to Microversium

Quest 400 accepts one bucket of deuterium. It pays a nickel and opens the Microversium branch. A Basic Centrifuge takes 500 mB hydrogen to 10 mB deuterium in 800 ticks at 30 EU/t. The first bucket therefore needs 50,000 mB hydrogen and 66 min 40 s of processing. Ten Moon Dust would also work, but each requires moon turf and a 400 EU/t macerator; the Lunar Mining Station is still ahead. No quest reward supplies either feed. The LV route can run during the long Pyrolyse Oven and electronics construction jobs.

Use the empty socket directly above the workshop electrolyzer at (4,82,6). Extend the existing LV cable up one block at (5,82,6). The centrifuge recipe takes four Basic Circuits, two LV motors, two tin cables and an LV hull. Quest 55 pays a nickel for the machine before placement. Plan the purchase after the brewery starts supplying ethanol and before building the sixteen oven coils. Use the existing electrolyzer for water in short batches between solid orders. The 10,000 mB oxygen reserve also produces 20,000 mB hydrogen; only fifteen more buckets of water are needed for the first deuterium if all that hydrogen is recovered.

Point the electrolyzer's fluid output up and keep its item output manual. JEI lists hydrogen as a centrifuge input and lists no centrifuge recipe using oxygen. The GT machine checks its recipe map when filling a tank. Verify that hydrogen arrives, that oxygen stays below and that the centrifuge actually starts. The shared electrolyzer remains accessible from the west. Keep six buckets of oxygen for polyethylene and four for annealing. Once the first deuterium bucket is collected, use the quest page before consuming it.
