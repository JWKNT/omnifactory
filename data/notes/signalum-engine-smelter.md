# Signalum engine cores at EV

JEI gives the Signalum Micro Miner Engine Core one recipe. A Signalum frame and four redstone blocks go into an alloy smelter at 2,000 EU/t. The two tier-four engines need two such frames and eight blocks. The installed HV core smelter caps recipes at 512 EU/t. Save it for earlier engine cores and Energetic Alloy.

Build the EV smelter with two Extreme circuits, four quadruple Nichrome wires, two aluminium cables and an EV hull. The wiremill turns eight Nichrome ingots into the sixteen single wires. At 106h04m two ingots and eight qualifying circuits were in stock. The IV smelter instead calls for Elite circuits, quadruple tungstensteel wire and an IV hull. The EV machine meets the core recipe and also runs lower-tier alloy patterns.

The chemistry roof has a four-amp EV cable at (19,83,-9). Branch one insulated aluminium lead east to (20,83,-9) and replace the cobblestone above that lead with the smelter. Its top stays level with the catwalk. Fit the ME interface on its south face at (20,84,-8), connected from the glass cable above the EV assembler. The new branch supplies one machine. Keep the separate HV iron reactor's gold cable tile at (20,83,-10) free for that reactor.

The next engine grade, Enderium, needs 8,000 EU/t. Plan an IV alloy smelter on the north IV branch when that engine is due. Keep the EV station on Signalum frames and lower-voltage alloys once both are running.
