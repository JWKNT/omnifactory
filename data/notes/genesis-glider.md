# Glider and the last Genesis pages

At 125h42m page 353 paid an OmniNickel. The Atomic Reconstructor converted eleven Rotten Flesh into eleven Leather on its temporary shelf, completing the twelve leather for the glider. The successful throw used the relay reconstruction routine: from the step, face north with a slight upward pitch, drop the flesh, then walk back. The magnet recovered the converted stack and the pick recovered the shelf. Two wings used twelve leather and four sticks. The central part used six iron rods from the lathe.

Genesis now has 49 of 51 pages complete. The remaining pages are the Obsidian Furnace and Leadstone Jetpack. The furnace takes two Diamond Furnaces and seven obsidian. Each jetpack thruster takes a Steam Dynamo, a Redstone Reception Coil, four lead plates and three red alloy plates. The final pack adds four more lead plates, one basic flux capacitor and one leather strap. A spare strap is already in stock.
