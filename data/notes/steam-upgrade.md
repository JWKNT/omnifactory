# Scaling power after the first wiremill

One basic Steam Dynamo produces 40 RF/t according to quest 752. Running the wiremill continuously at its default 32 EU/t depleted the buffer. With overclocking disabled the machine verified 8 EU/t, a nearly full buffer, and a steady output of fine copper wire. Stagger machine batches while the camp has only this generator.

The alloy smelter unlocks both steel and the Boilers and Turbines quest. Prepare for two extra steam dynamos. The book specifies one dynamo with a boiler conversion feeding two dynamos with turbine conversions at equal upgrade levels. It rates the trio at 320 RF/t. Look up the augment ingredients, hardened upgrade kits and their dependencies before moving the power station. Give the permanent plant room for steam pipes, water and accessible fuel storage.
