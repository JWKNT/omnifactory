# A filtered feed from the blast furnace to the freezer

Quest 156 recommends sending hot metal directly between the two buses. The west-facing blast output is at (0,80,8). The planned freezer input faces east at (-3,80,8). Only two blocks separate them, and both spaces were air. The installed GregTech item buses push from their front or pull into their front through any cover on that face. That allows both machines to use the same double chest. It needs no power, keeps up with the ten-second blast cycle, and buffers production during a freezer outage.

The iron Chest Transporter is crafted. The nine recovered hot Kanthal are still in their original chest at (4,81,2). After the freezer is formed and powered, scripts/freezer_kanthal.py checks that the chest holds only those nine ingots. It moves the chest intact to (-2,80,8). The freezer input should draw directly from it. Look for nine cooled Kanthal at the west output bus, then hold them for quest 156. The installed recipe takes 112 ticks per ingot at 120 EU/t.

For subsequent hot ingots, scripts/hot_ingot_filter.py prepares a GregTech Ore Dictionary Filter from eight zinc foil and an emerald plate. The alternative uses olivine. Two foil already exist, and the remaining six can come from two zinc plates in the cluster mill. Set the blast bus's west cover to whitelist ingotHot* for extraction. The hot Kanthal tooltip has the ore name ingotHotKanthal. Once the nine cold ingots and filter have both been verified, a second chest at (-1,80,8) can join the first and bridge the gap. Cold aluminium and stainless should remain in the EBF output bus for other lines. Verify the first fresh blast reaches the freezer on its own before committing the longer coil run. The filter, joined chest and cooling trials are still pending.

A south service opening will preserve access to cold output after the double chest covers the EBF's west face. Open only the single cobblestone block immediately south at (0,80,9), beneath the existing stairs. Verify the bus GUI opens from (0,80,11) before joining the chest. The installed GregTech code makes the export bus push through its front cover's item handler every five ticks. The three-ingot delivery is the in-world test.
