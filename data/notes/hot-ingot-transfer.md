Its west side has an installed GregTech Ore Dictionary Filter. The screwdriver GUI set it to extraction, whitelist, ingotHot*. All three settings were read back from the installed cover. Its east-facing freezer input should pull all nine. Each takes 112 ticks at 120 EU/t. Count nine cold ingots at the west output, then claim 156 on its page.
