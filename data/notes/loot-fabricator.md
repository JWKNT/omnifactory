# Slime matter and the second RF socket

Quest 685 awards a quarter for holding the finished Loot Fabricator. Collect it from factory parts with scripts/picklist.py before opening the quest. The installation helper scripts/loot_socket.py extends the charger's conduit north to (7,81,8) and places the fabricator above it beside the charger. Check the printed RF buffer after installation and leave the top accessible for pristine matter. The socket was surveyed before the first fabricator order.

JEI lists six outputs for a single Pristine Slime Matter. The choices are 32 slimeballs, eight copper coins, six silver coins, four nickel coins, two gold coins or one platinum coin. These are Thermal Foundation coins. Take slimeballs from the first pristine if the camp's last slimeball is still the only reserve. That provides spare models and material for sticky pistons without draining the coin supply. Ordinary Overworldian Matter is a separate output and stays reserved for model progression.
