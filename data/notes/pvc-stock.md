# Keeping PVC on hand for capacitors

Four thin PVC sheets and one foil make sixteen SMD capacitors in the polyethylene-fed assembler. Empty capacitor and PVC bins held up the good-tier circuit for the freezer converter, even with boards, fine wire and transistors already on hand.

The campaign now checks sheet equivalents at each stock survey. Below four sheets with fewer than 32 capacitors, scripts/pvc_stock.py opens a replenishment charge. Four rock salt ore yielded twelve rock salt in the electric furnace. Eight salt supplied four buckets of chlorine at the electrolyzer. The east dehydration reactor supplied one bucket of ethylene. Chlorination, the oxygen-fed polymer reactor and caster delivered ten sheets into ME. The cluster mill took two of those and the terminal counted eight thin PVC sheets. The capacitor batch is the next delivery to verify. Use recovered sulfuric acid for the next dehydration charge when available.
