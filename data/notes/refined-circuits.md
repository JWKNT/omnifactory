# The first Refined Processor Array

One array takes eight MV solder operations. Make twelve Refined Circuits in three batches of four, assemble them into four Refined Processors, then combine the processors into one array. Across all eight operations the live recipes require eight plastic boards, 22 SMD resistors, 20 capacitors, 22 transistors, 22 fine tin alloy wires, three Central Processing Unit chips, four RAM chips, four single vibrant alloy wires, two stainless steel plates and 576 mB of solder. Four solder ingots cover the fluid exactly. Three tin alloy ingots in the wiremill yield 24 fine wires. Sixteen plastic boards cover the first two arrays.

Use the glass lens and one of the twelve ordinary silicon wafers in the installed MV engraver. The resulting CPU wafer gives eight chips in the MV cutter for 90 mB of ordinary water. The higher-voltage wafer routes can wait. The order runs through scripts/first_cpu_chips.py while other hardware is being built. Check for chips before loading another wafer.

Quest 133 follows both the SMD and plastic substrate pages. Quest 135 follows the refined circuits, then quest 203 covers the array. Spend the first spare array on another eight blank crafting patterns. Reserve four further arrays for two EV mainframes and the HV assembler.
