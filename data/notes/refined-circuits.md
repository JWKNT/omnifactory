Across all eight operations the live recipes require eight plastic boards, 22 SMD resistors, 20 capacitors, 22 transistors, 22 fine tin alloy wires, three Central Processing Unit chips, four RAM chips, four single vibrant alloy wires, two stainless steel plates and 576 mB of solder.
