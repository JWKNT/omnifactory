# Automating the extended crafting tables

Page 408 pays an OmniQuarter and opens pages 406 and 813. A package recipe holder stores up to twenty recipes. The packager and unpackager use matching encoded holders. Build the first pair for repeat miner hulls once the titanium and circuit lines can cover another ship without delaying the first iridium and gemstone cargoes.

The installed recipes make each ME Packaging Component from an ordinary Packaging Component, four titanium plates, two quartz glass, an annihilation core and a formation core. The ordinary component itself needs another four titanium plates, four Vibrant Alloy Plates and an Eye of Ender. The first packager and unpackager each add four titanium plates, two Nano Processor Assemblies and a Mechanical Crafter. One takes an import bus, the other an export bus.

The Package Recipe Encoder consumes two holders, a separate pattern encoder, a Nano Processor Assembly, four titanium plates and an ME Packaging Component. Keep two more holders for the machines. The current JEI grid yields three holders per Crystaltine block, three blank patterns, two quartz glass, two titanium plates and one ME Packaging Component. Two crafts supply six holders. The encoder takes two, the first machine pair takes two, and two remain for the next line. The pair, encoder and both holder crafts require five ME Packaging Components. Budget 56 titanium plates and 20 Vibrant Alloy Plates before counting the mechanical crafters, buses and pattern encoder. Page 813 also opens the 16-input Extended Processing Pattern Terminal for the Assembly Line.

The two Crystaltine blocks are the expensive part of the holder order. The Elite table makes one ingot from ten lapis, eight diamonds, four star nuggets, four iron and two gold. Eighteen crafts take 180 lapis, 144 diamonds, 72 star nuggets, 72 iron and 36 gold. One star makes nine nuggets, so reserve eight stars. No book reward supplies Crystaltine or those nuggets. Eight crafted stars from scratch take 168 quartz, sixteen of each of the four elemental powders, two of each elemental rod for the cores and two blocks of Luminessence. Each core recipe yields four. Let the Signalum engine orders finish using those same powders first. The Wither simulation will supply realm data for later flights. Its fabricator menu has no star output.

Page 406 asks for three packagers, three unpackagers, six holders and the advanced, elite and ultimate automatic crafters. The elite crafter consumes an Elite Extended Crafting Table, an Automation Interface, an ME Packaging Component, four Elite Components and two Black Steel Plates. Reuse the existing elite table after the first manual flights and only once the replacement can start running. Keep a clear network aisle for the three table sizes and their package machines.
