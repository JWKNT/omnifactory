# Solder for the EV assembler

At 72h20m there were 112 mB of soldering alloy in the EV assembler and 376 mB in the HV assembler. One electronics craft uses 72 mB. The EV tank must also have solder when the network delivers array and mainframe ingredients.

ME held 15 tin ingots, 37 antimony ingots and 744 tiny piles of tin dust. Nine tiny piles combine into one dust. JEI's alloy smelter accepts nine tin dust and one antimony ingot for ten soldering alloy ingots, just as it accepts nine tin ingots. Compact 27 dust from 243 tiny piles for a 30-ingot solder batch. This yields 4,320 mB and preserves the ordinary tin ingots. Use the existing solder export and MV extractor at (10,82,4) to fill the HV assembler, then take a bucket to the EV assembler while leaving 500 mB in the HV tank. The two machines can work at the same time.
