# Propene for epoxy and the allyl chloride side quest

Page 806 points to propene and chlorine as another route to epichlorohydrin. The other proven route uses six buckets of fish oil per bucket of glycerol. That required 150 fish and several extractor loads. The twelve clean naphtha buckets already banked will make eight epoxy sheets if supplied with 576 mB epichlorohydrin and four buckets of nitrogen dioxide. Keep the naphtha for that reaction.

Use twelve of the eighteen remaining buckets of sulfuric light fuel for the pilot. Desulfurization costs two buckets of hydrogen and also returns another bucket of hydrogen sulfide. The workshop boiler can supply the 24 buckets of steam for cracking the clean light fuel. In a single-block distillery on circuit seven, each bucket of cracked light fuel yields 250 mB propene. A twelve-bucket run yields three buckets. For allyl chloride, each propene bucket takes two chlorine and yields one hydrochloric acid plus one allyl chloride. The parts shelf held a bucket of hypochlorous acid. With the allyl chloride and sodium hydroxide it can supply the first epichlorohydrin charge.

A full second tower would also capture ethylene, benzene and eight other products from the same cracked fuel. The current four outlets are assigned to crude oil. The single-block MV still can use the spare MV hull from the air collector batch, two good circuits, two copper cables, two glass, one blaze rod and one MV pump. A bone and two of the 99 stored Hellish Matter make the rod. Build two pumps together and reserve the second for the fluid interface described on page 730. Reserve the existing LV still for diluted acid. Revisit an eleven-outlet tower once there is steady oil production and demand for the other streams.

The second light-fuel charge is finished. The HV reactor returned one bucket of hydrogen sulfide. I cleared the remaining hydrogen before putting the clean fuel back in, since hydrogen also cracks light fuel and gives a different product. The first eight buckets consumed the full 16-bucket steam drum. Sixteen coal restarted the water-fed boiler at 160 mB/t and supplied the remaining eight buckets of steam. All twelve buckets of steam-cracked light fuel reached the ME terminal. The naphtha reserve remains twelve buckets. The next job is commissioning the program-seven distillery.
