# Feeding the shared plastic board stock

At 63 hours the basic circuit line had solder, CPU chips and SMDs, but no refined circuits and only one plastic board. The caster held 44 polyethylene sheets and the coater had copper foil and acid. scripts/epoxy_coater_feed.py now feeds surplus polyethylene if PVC is unavailable. Keep sixteen polyethylene sheets for pattern blanks. PVC makes two plastic circuit boards per sheet, polyethylene makes one. The resulting boards reach ME through the existing coater output. I saw stock increase from seven to ten while the player was elsewhere. Each polyethylene board took 600 ticks at 10 EU/t in this machine.
