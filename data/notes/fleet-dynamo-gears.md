Three Basic Reactant Dynamos go inside the tier-three ship's Reinforced Thrusters. Two more go directly in the tier-four hull. Together they need ten Energized Bimetal Gears. Each gear gets four Energetic Alloy ingots in the alloy smelter after two Grains of Infinity and an iron gear make its Infinity Bimetal Gear. All three alloy patterns are already installed. Order affordable subassemblies while the gold is processing. Four iron gears and six grains were loose at 103h27m. The existing gear die uses four iron ingots per additional gear.
