# The five Reactant Dynamos and their gears

Three Basic Reactant Dynamos go inside the tier-three ship's Reinforced Thrusters. Two more go directly in the tier-four hull. Together they need ten Energized Bimetal Gears. Each gear gets four Energetic Alloy ingots in the alloy smelter after two Grains of Infinity and an iron gear make its Infinity Bimetal Gear. All three alloy patterns are already installed. Order affordable subassemblies while the gold is processing. Four iron gears and six grains were loose at 103h27m. The existing gear die uses four iron ingots per additional gear. A hand-crafted gear takes four plates and four rods. Each dynamo also uses two Energetic Alloy plates, so allocate fifty Energetic Alloy ingots to the five dynamos before counting the ship thrusters' own alloy.

The first ore mission with the Wither data pays for the osmium line. The alternative medium mission for Infinity Dust Blocks requires a Composition Sensor and a tier-four ship, so it cannot supply these first gears. Use the two dry standing places in the existing bedrock gallery for an interim reserve of at least 24 grains. The player can go when the dynamo queues are healthy and the first inexpensive gears have consumed the loose grains.

For the permanent source, the instance enables Fire Water on bedrock in the overworld. Page 759 specifies 2% per random tick per covered block, a Vacuum Chest, two Ender Chests, and a bucket of Fire Water. It pays a quarter. Page 67, the Octadic Capacitor, opens the Vat (511); both pay a nickel. The Vat uses an industrial chassis, an obsidian furnace, two MV pumps, two tanks, a cauldron and two dark steel plates. Hootch comes first in the Vat from water, sugar and a food such as potato. Blaze powder and redstone turn the Hootch into Fire Water. Keep a dry gallery aisle, place the chest within collection range, chunkload the gallery and verify that grains reach the workshop chest before retiring the lighter. Building the Vat also opens Dew of the Void.

The native request for four Energized Bimetal Gears came back two Grains of Infinity short, after accounting for stored intermediate gears. At 103h55m both lapidaries held over sixty diamonds. For the permanent gallery, live JEI shows one bucket of Hootch with blaze powder and redstone in the Vat makes one bucket of Fire Water. The Vat itself uses two MV pumps, two Fluid Tanks, two Dark Steel Plates, an Obsidian Furnace, cauldron and Industrial Machine Chassis. Finish the remaining laser banks before spending their Basic RF Capacitors on the Octadic Capacitor prerequisite.

The Basic RF Capacitor pattern is on the main molecular shelf beside the capacitor bank. The pattern uses an SMD Capacitor, three Electrical Steel Nuggets and two single tin wires to make three RF capacitors. The LV wiremill has delivered its first 24 tin wires from twelve tin ingots. Its former Fine Annealed Copper Wire card had the same input and output as the faster MV wiremill card, which remains installed. One ingot cut into nine electrical steel nuggets covers the last two laser banks.
