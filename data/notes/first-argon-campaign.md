# Use one tower for the first argon

Build one cryogenic tower for the first glowstone-doped boule. The initial plan allowed space for a second tower. The pack’s actual cycle times make it worth switching feeds on the first tower before funding another set of 84 steel casings and 52 frost-proof casings.

One liquid-air run takes 125 ticks at 500 EU/t and yields one bucket of noble gases. Fifty-three runs take 331 seconds of tower time and consume 2,809 buckets of liquid air. The noble-gas run takes another 50 seconds at 750 EU/t and makes 35 buckets of argon. At the west freezer’s measured 143 ticks per four buckets, the freezer needs about 84 minutes for that charge. The first tower has time for both jobs.

Claim page 171 with its required HV energy hatch. For the noble-gas run, fit an EV energy input hatch and power it from an EV source. The ordinary hatch recipe uses an EV hull and an aluminium cable. The structure definition accepts fluid hatches and energy hatches in frost-proof casing positions. Keep the liquid-air drum isolated while charging the noble gases, or fit a second input. Drain the first stage’s output hatches before switching gases.

The first campaign also produces 2,120 buckets of nitrogen, 583 of oxygen and 53 of carbon dioxide. An iron drum holds 256 buckets. Give surplus nitrogen and oxygen a genuine overflow path so the first few runs do not stop the plant. A filtered low-priority fluid conduit to a fluid trash can beyond the useful gas reservoir is one option. Keep the noble gas on a dedicated path. The output storage, EV feed and tower remain to be built.

The cycle definitions are in `instance/scripts/Multiblocks.zs`. Check the formed controller, gas routing and actual output volumes at commissioning. Revisit a second tower when the freezer fleet can keep the first busy.

The claim map confirms both tower chunks, (−2,0) and (−1,0), belong to the player and are loaded in the void. The second command pair reported that the chunk could not be modified because that chunk was already claimed and loaded. Fifteen frost-proof and nine solid steel casings are in ME at 74h22m. The fourth furnace now advertises aluminium ingots as an ME process. Its south outlet already separates cold ingots from the hot freezer return.

At 74h29m the fourth furnace advertised native orders for aluminium, stainless steel and chrome. The northern furnace can still take overflow powder. The LV steel smelter had stalled with wrought iron inside and no carbon. Eight graphite dust went through the existing electrolyzer recipe, which returns four carbon per dust. The first casing inventory reached 15 frost-proof and nine solid steel. The tower controller also consumes an Extra Utilities screen. Use the existing resonator for Stoneburnt and the alloy smelter for its resonating crystal.


The live JEI preview gives a 6 × 12 × 6 tower, with the controller facing west at local (0,0,2). Put the base at x−17 through −12, z10 through 15, y80. The input is (−17,80,13). On the east edge, x−12, the energy hatch is at z13 and the five outputs occupy z10, 11, 12, 14 and 15. The preview uses three LV outlets, one MV and one HV. The pack matcher accepts export hatches there and the quest requests five HV outlets, so use the five quest hatches. The upper steel ring rises to y90 and its four-block cap sits at y91. The JEI material count is exactly 84 solid steel and 52 frost-proof casings with the seven hatches installed. Preserve both long aisles for plumbing and the energy hatch change.

The current iron drum holds 256 buckets. It would fill less than a tenth of the way through the 2,809-bucket air campaign. Live JEI lists the Basic Quantum Tank at 64,000 buckets. It costs an MV hull, three aluminium plates, four good circuits and an MV field generator. The generator takes an Eye of Ender, four more good circuits and four Energetic Alloy wires already in storage. A Reinforced Large Drum instead needs six vibrant alloy plates, two stainless steel drums and an iron drum. Give the collector its MV hull and pumps first. Then put the quantum tank at (−6,81,10) and a single fluid conduit at (−5,81,10) between it and the existing iron drum. All three positions are above the cobblestone freezer deck. This keeps the first 64 buckets in service and puts the bulk supply on the tower side of the freezer. Verify the tanks’ actual balance before counting the extra capacity.
