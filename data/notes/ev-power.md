# Power for the EV assembler

The current hardened lapidary dynamo showed 3,646 RF/t under load against a maximum of 3,750. A full 2,048 EU/t EV load costs 8,192 RF/t at the workshop's conversion rate. The Shulker line has banked over 480 diamonds. The operating limit here is how quickly we can turn those diamonds into RF while the furnaces and simulation chambers are running.

Quest 609's Reinforced Upgrade Kit takes four electrum gears, four electrum plates and a block of aluminium. The existing MV extruder takes four electrum ingots per gear. The mold recipe takes eight. The compressor takes one ingot per plate, so the project calls for twenty electrum. Keep four gold aside while ordering electrum through the ME alloy smelter. scripts/reinforced_dynamo.py stages the kit, opens the quest book for its nickel and checks the dynamo's actual capacity after installation. The kit is still in preparation.

The direct four-slot EV CEF recipe costs another EV hull, an Extreme circuit, four End Steel Energy Conduits and eight single End Steel wires. Its tooltip confirms a soft hammer reverses it to take four 512 V amps and output one 2,048 V amp. Reserve a full four-amp supply specifically for it. The existing north feeder already serves two HV machines. A transformer buys the voltage tier, while additional generation and RF distribution determine sustained throughput. Measure the kit first, then size the second dynamo or transmission augment against the load.
