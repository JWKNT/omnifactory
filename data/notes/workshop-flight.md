# Flight for the west deck

Placing the last two overhead ME cables took separate sight lines while sneaking. Before building the longer west-deck power run, compare personal flight with the time spent on accesses. Page 45 pays an OmniQuarter for the Conductive Iron Jetpack. Page 552 pays a nickel for the Leadstone Jetpack. Both are open. Each has one construction recipe in this instance, alongside recipes that charge, recolor or dismantle an existing pack.

The Conductive Iron version needs twelve conductive iron plates across the pack and its two thrusters, six red alloy plates, two ordinary Conductive Iron Energy Conduits, two Resonating Redstone Crystals, a Basic RF Capacitor and a leather strap. The Leadstone version uses twelve lead plates, six red alloy plates, two Redstone Reception Coils, two Steam Dynamos, a Basic Flux Capacitor and the same strap. One steel plate and two leather make the strap. The Atomic Reconstructor converts one rotten flesh to one leather; the stock chest has eleven flesh. The compressor, alloy smelter and charging station are already available. Save the installed dynamos for power. Reprice conductive iron after the furnace's cable and power bill. A working trial should include a controlled takeoff and landing over the deck, followed by an overhead placement.
