# Construction flight

The Conductive Iron Jetpack is equipped and page 45, Ironman, is claimed. The two thrusters, four conductive iron plates, leather strap and one basic capacitor made the pack. Three basic capacitors came from one SMD capacitor, three electrical steel nuggets and two tin wires. There was no need to spend the double-layer capacitor.

The 10M RF basic Flux Capacitor from the wafer row charged the jetpack to 43,200 RF. The capacitor went back into the MV converter, where the readout confirmed it was reinstalled. A held jump binding then lifted the player from y80.00 to y83.69 over the platform at (9,14) and used 448 RF. The harness move command applies vanilla movement input and gave an ordinary jump without using RF. For powered flight use the bound jump key as in scripts/workshop_flight.py and always release it. Plan roof and drum servicing from a clear column with a solid landing underneath. During ground travel the pack can stay equipped. The leather tunic is in ME storage.
