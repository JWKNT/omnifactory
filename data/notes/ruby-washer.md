# Wash the ruby for chrome

The first coil batch used a single LV mill for both passes. At 12 EU/t, 24 raw ruby took eight minutes to crush and 48 crushed ruby took another eight to grind. The quest on ore by-products led to a better option for the remaining chrome. Each crushed ruby in a water-fed ore washer gives three tiny chrome dust; nine tiny dust make one full dust. The purified ore still gives one ruby dust after grinding and centrifuging. Six ruby dust electrolyze to one chrome and two aluminium. This makes eighteen raw ore worth eighteen guaranteed chrome and twelve aluminium, with no random macerator drops assumed.

I cancelled the two unstarted 24-ore lots. The current 24-ore lot continues. The next eighteen raw ore are already on hand. A second installed LV macerator above the clay reactor can crush them at the same time, with its downward export switched off for ore. Sand processing will re-enable that export. The existing sand and gravel machines stay on their dedicated lines.

I chose the MV washer. All three voltage tiers yield the same guaranteed chrome. On the 400-tick, 16 EU/t water recipe, the expected time for 36 crushed ore is twelve minutes at LV, six at MV, three at HV. The MV build needs an MV motor, two bronze rotors, an MV hull, two copper cables, two good-grade circuits and glass. The HV washer needs a stainless hull, two advanced-grade circuits, an HV motor and gold cables. Neither machine pays a direct quest reward. The MV transformer already has spare outputs beside the extruder at (11,82,3). The washer is ordered at (12,82,3) with an Infinite Water Source above. Verify water, energy and a measured batch. Compare upgrading the mill as a separate question. In the installed mod LV and MV macerators have one product slot; the HV Universal Macerator has three and can capture bonus chrome on the first crush. The east HV cable at (11,82,2) leaves room for it.

At 32h39m the upper mill accepted eighteen ruby ore. Its first cycle showed 12 EU/t and 400 ticks, with seventeen ore still in the input. The centrifuge was cleaning the earlier batch at the same time. This starts the new order from the twenty ore already on the shelf.
