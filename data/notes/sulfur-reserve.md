# Sulfur for the next acid batches

The workshop has ten sulfur dust. Quest 628 pays one OmniQuarter for showing 64 and leaves the stack intact. Two nickel purchases of 32 sulfur ore each cost four nickels together. Fire 54 ore in four spare coke furnaces, leaving ten ore. The quarter is worth five nickels. This gives the workshop acid feed and one net nickel while the electric machines make reactor components.

The live furnace gives one dust per ore. The macerator gives two crushed ore, but the resulting impure sulfur also needs cleaning. The centrifuge is occupied with deuterium. The alternatives include electrolysis of sulfides and a chance byproduct from silver processing. The stored galena and sphalerite are also sources of lead and zinc. No quest pays sulfur dust as an item reward. Use the direct furnace route for this batch.
