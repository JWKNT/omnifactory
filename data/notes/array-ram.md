# RAM for five more arrays

Five more Microprocessor Arrays need 120 RAM between the arrays and their twenty Processor Assemblies. The new blank-pattern batch also needs an Advanced circuit. With the dozen ordinary silicon wafers already on hand, the installed MV engraver can make one RAM Wafer at 120 EU/t using its diamond lens. The HV recipe makes four from a glowstone-doped wafer at 480 EU/t, and the EV recipe makes eight from a naquadah-doped wafer. Reprice the HV engraver when the ordinary stock runs down.

Five wafers entered the engraver together. Each takes 900 ticks at MV. The cutting machine has 3,816 mB water and takes 90 mB per wafer to produce 32 RAM. scripts/array_ram.py collects engraved wafers for cutting while the laser works through the remainder. The total order is 160 RAM. Keep the spare work available for mainframes and patterns.

All five wafers were cut. The collection log totals 160 RAM. The freed engraver then took five silicon wafers with the glass lens for 40 CPU chips. Those five CPU wafers are now sawing. The upcoming 15 batches of Microcircuits need 30 CPU chips before counting other circuits.
