In this instance, GregTech's RecipeLogicEnergy checks the machine's rated input voltage. EnergyContainerHandler accepts smaller packets. Two IV packets per tick can cover the first crystal circuit in a LuV assembler. Test this with a charge before committing larger lots.

A new LuV CEF would take four Signalum Energy Conduits, eight single Signalum wires in two bundles, another LuV hull and a Master circuit. The north workshop already has a four-amp IV CEF and two hardened capacitors. Once the first LuV assembler has been crafted, extend that CEF to both the line hatch and the crystal workshop.
