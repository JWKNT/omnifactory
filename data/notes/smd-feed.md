# A liquid polyethylene reserve for the MV assembler

The first nine polyethylene sheets cover the pattern batch. The next batch needs a liquid split for SMD components. At the start of the Crafting Unit order the ethanol still held 2,500 mB, the water-fed reactor held 3,000 mB of sulfuric acid and the electrolyzer had 35,000 mB of oxygen. Use a bucket each of acid and ethanol. The MV ethylene reactor should then hold a second bucket of diluted acid toward the three-bucket recycling charge.

Combine the new 1,000 mB of ethylene with the 136 mB left in the polymer reactor. Seven reactions use 1,008 mB ethylene and seven oxygen buckets, producing 1,512 mB polyethylene and leaving 128 mB ethylene. Temporarily switch off automatic fluid output. Reserve one full bucket for the MV assembler, then reopen output to the plate mold. The remaining 512 mB cast three sheets and leave 80 mB in the solidifier. Remelting two sheets for the diode's 288 mB is another option if the ethanol line is unavailable.

The first MV recipes consume 288 mB for 32 diodes, 288 mB for 32 transistors, 144 mB for 24 resistors and 36 mB for sixteen capacitors. All four batches fit in the reserved bucket. The capacitors also need four thin PVC sheets and an aluminium foil. Keep the solder-fed LV assembler available for the circuit-board recipes. The nickel by-product route for the diode wire is in first-platinum.md.

With two mainframes planned for the HV assembler, run the resistor recipe twice. After the 32 diodes, make 32 transistors and 48 resistors. All three orders together consume 864 mB of the reserved litre. The remaining 136 mB covers the 36 mB capacitor batch once PVC is available. One annealed copper ingot supplies eight fine wire through two wiremill passes, and four of the eight small gallium piles make the full dust to fire and compress for the transistor plate. The two resistor batches use the banked electrum ingot the same way. Two coal dust in the centrifuge make two guaranteed carbon dust at 18 ticks each. Dark ashes or charcoal dust already on the shelf also work. scripts/smd_passives.py is queued after the diode quest. The component recipes and costs are verified in JEI; the batches still need a live check.
