# Coal dust for the steel smelter

At 110 hours, the steel smelter had wrought iron in its input, and ME had coal dust but no carbon. The HV centrifuge was processing iron ore. JEI gives one Carbon Dust per Coal Dust at 30 EU/t for 18 ticks. Charcoal Dust and Coke Dust also work. Electrolyzing stored carbon dioxide is another source, but needs output tank space for the oxygen. Coal on the original LV centrifuge can supply more than one carbon per second without taking time from the HV ore line.

The LV centrifuge at (4,82,6) has 32 V power and an open roof socket. The south and north conduit bundles serve fluids. Keep those connections for the old deuterium line. Fit the item interface on top, connect it to the west fluix cable through (3,83,6), and point it down. The carbon tender can load the first charge by hand. Count the first carbon returned to ME and the next steel from the smelter before calling the line commissioned.

The first 19 Coal Dust ran at 30 EU/t and 18 ticks each in the LV machine. Its output held all 19 Carbon Dust. After banking those, the upper arm fed the steel smelter and it started another 200-tick steel cycle. Stock subsequently reached 16 steel. That verifies the recipe and the steel feed. The LV centrifuge still needs its automatic item return.
