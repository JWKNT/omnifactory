# The pulsating iron branch

The full stack of 64 nickel ingots paid a 25-value coin in quest 640. Three nickels from it bought 32 uraninite ore. Six ore went into the void west furnace, then four dust were smelted again in the camp east furnace. Pulsating Dust (28) was claimed. The remaining raw ore is in void metals. The follow-up (29) needs one pulsating iron ingot and an ender pearl. The alloy smelter makes a pearl from one diamond and one pulsating dust, or an ingot from one iron and one pulsating dust. The pearl is complete and two ingots were ordered. One diamond remains from the mining trip.

The next quest is Item Conduits (39). For the first four, use six binder and three single pulsating iron wires. Camp parts had eight binder. Mill the two new ingots for four wires after the furnace coils finish. The assembler makes eight conduits from the ingredients, but the LV assembler quest needs tier-two circuits, a robot arm and a conveyor; four conduits now can start moving ore and machine products while that tier is being built.

Pulsating Dust (28) and Pulsating Iron and Ender Pearls (29) are now both claimed. The pearl used one of the two remaining diamonds, and two actual pulsating iron ingots came from the alternate iron recipe. The spare diamond and pearl are in void metals.

The wiremill processed both ingots. Six binder from camp and three wires made four item conduits. Quest 39 is complete and claimed. The conduits are available for the first transport line.
