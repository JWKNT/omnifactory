# Native mixing for the miner alloys

Black steel for the elite table and Signalum for the arrays share the original LV mixer. It also makes fuel for the projector. The five dry recipes can all run on its current 32 V supply. Black bronze takes 500 ticks at 8 EU/t, black steel 200 at 15, red steel 800 at 8, Ardite 200 at 30, and Signalum Blend 400 at 30. The MV mixer halves those processing times, but the ore mill, lathe and power buffer account for more of the current delay. First connect the working machine. Leave the easterly aisle and the fuel tanks accessible.

The top of the mixer at (6,81,3) was clear. Point its item outlet upward, allow inputs on that face and place an ME Interface above it. Four glass cable tiles reach the existing line from (8,83,3), across the roof at y84 and down to (6,83,3). All four were air at 96h59m. The foil mill gets the first spare interface. The crafting grid also makes eight red steel and four Signalum Blend at the same yield. Give those two recipes to a molecular assembler. Black bronze can use separate silver and gold dust in the grid or combined electrum dust in the mixer. Encode black steel and Ardite on the mixer first. Its material return should go straight into ME. One verified nine-dust black steel delivery from the machine is the commissioning test. An independent higher-voltage mixer is worth revisiting once the projector and the alloys are both taking sustained orders.
