# Enderium budget for the sixth miner

The regular Tier Six ship needs nineteen Enderium Heavy Plating, three Enderium engine cores and three Resonant Thrusters. Its other fittings are four Supercharged Laser Arrays, three Fission Reactor Ports, two advanced guidance systems, two Basic Quantum Chests, two IV Field Generators and one Fission Controller. JEI confirms the installed 7×7 elite table takes the 49-slot recipe.

The LuV press consumes two plates per heavy plate at 30,000 EU/t. The three engine frames take twelve more plates and twelve rods, then eight redstone blocks per frame in an 8,000 EU/t alloy smelter. The thrusters consume another twelve Enderium plates and one basic Numismatic Dynamo apiece. The lathe yields two rods per ingot. The 62 ordinary plates and twelve rods therefore require 68 ingots, or seventeen four-Blend batches and seventeen buckets of Primal Mana. Reserve the first heavy plate long enough to earn the nickel on page 316. It opens the ship page, which pays a quarter.

The ordinary smelting recipe accepts Enderium Dust. JEI obtains that dust from existing Enderium parts or smaller piles. It cannot smelt fresh Blend. The productive recipe starts with four lead dust, two platinum dust, one blue steel dust, one osmium dust and a Resonant Clathrate for four Blend. The mixer uses four ingots’ worth of liquid lead for the same yield. Use the grid for the stockpiled lead dust. The sixth furnace needs 250 mB Primal Mana per ingot at 6,400 K. The product is already cold. Page 750 pays a nickel and opens the plate and Resonant upgrade pages.

At the recipe’s 20,800 base ticks, a single HV heat could take about eight minutes and forty seconds per ingot before heat bonuses. That is enough to prove the first heat. It is too slow for the ship. Budget a pair of EV hatches and a separate EV converter for the sustained run once the fuel and grid can carry them. At three ordinary overclocks, 120 EU/t becomes 7,680 EU/t for about 130 seconds, roughly two and a half hours for 68 ingots before heat bonuses. Keep preparing Blend and Primal Mana during the coil build. Fund an actual sustained generation test before committing the lot. The fifth furnace already demonstrates that a full capacitor bank can cover one short run while the long-term generation rate remains below its draw.

The conversion ratio and conduit limit change the first production build. One Vibrant connection supplies at most 8,192 RF/t, or 2,048 EU/t in the EV or HV converter. A single HV hatch only accepts two 512 V amps. Two HV hatches on the same four-amp HV converter can accept 2,048 EU/t together. That covers a 1,920 EU/t second overclock and roughly 260 seconds per Enderium ingot. It also fits the existing RF connection. Build this pair before the first heat. Stage the more expensive dual EV replacement when the line can deliver roughly 30,720 RF/t continuously. A single Vibrant inlet would otherwise leave the EV capacitor waiting for most of its charge. End Steel Energy Conduit is rated for 32,768 RF/t here; there are three segments in storage.

At 119h43m the ore line had accumulated 111 tiny Osmium Dust as a by-product of iridium. The first 36 were packed into four full dust. This preserves the twelve raw osmium ore for later. The ordinary MV compressor has three pattern slots and the LuV press has eight. After page 750, run a second ingot and order one heavy plate through both presses. The heavy step alone uses 6,000,000 EU over 200 ticks. Check the actual heat and power supply before ordering all nineteen.

The LuV press itself can store 1,048,576 EU. The north IV converter currently has two fully charged Hardened Flux Capacitors and two empty slots. Their combined discharge rating is 80,000 RF/t. The heavy Enderium plate needs 120,000 RF/t for ten seconds. Install a third hardened capacitor before dispatch. Reserve at least 8.5 million RF in each of three capacitors for the pilot. Making one more hardened unit uses the existing lead, copper, sulfur, redstone, Invar and tin routes. The next reinforced model needs hardened glass and electrum as well. Keep the other machines’ capacitors where they are.

The first blend lot has four brass dust, five rose gold dust and four osmium dust. The HV mill supplied five steel dust. The old Water Mill at (22,80,−3) and Resonator at (17,80,1) produced four frequency-tagged Red Coal from ordinary coal. The existing Black Steel mixer pattern was encoded with a sample of this grid’s Red Coal.

The new gold lead ran at its rated four amps on the common section and two amps on the last leg. One bucket of Primal Mana came from the fourth furnace. The first ingot used 250 mB and left 750 mB for the remaining three blends. Collecting the ingot completed page 750. Reserve two ingots for the LuV press before drawing wire for the array.
