# A separate reactor for liquid polyethylene

The two planned mainframes need 112 SMD resistors, 88 capacitors and 92 transistors including the circuits and arrays below them. The first array order should leave 42 resistors, four capacitors and two transistors. At the current JEI yields the difference takes three resistor batches, six capacitor batches and three transistor batches. They use 432, 216 and 864 mB of polyethylene respectively, or 1,512 mB in total. Recount the parts before assembly if another order uses them.

The 128 mB of ethylene saved when switching to PVC is enough to turn one fresh 1,000 mB bucket into seven polymerizations. They consume 1,008 mB ethylene and 7,000 mB oxygen and give exactly 1,512 mB polyethylene. The MV ethylene reactor still needs its water by-product drained before another ethanol charge. The MV cutter takes that water. Send the diluted sulfuric acid to the recovery still.

Keep the existing reactor and plate mold on PVC. The next polyethylene reactor belongs at (6,81,-6), one block north of the acid recovery still, under a single 4x Conductive Iron Cable. The survey found both spaces empty. The only JEI recipe for the Basic Chemical Reactor uses a tin rotor, LV motor, hull, two glass, two tin cables and two basic circuits; there is no quest reward for that machine. Its reusable circuit stays at zero. Leave fluid auto-output off so the 1,512 mB can move as one charge to the MV assembler. The marked order in scripts/pe_reserve.py follows the motor patterns. Installation and the fluid transfers still need an in-game test.
