# Material and power budget for the first silicon boule

The EBF recipe is 32 silicon dust and one tiny gallium dust for a single boule. It draws 120 EU/t for 6,000 ticks, or 720,000 EU. Quest 80 pays a nickel and unlocks Silicon Wafers. The clay electrolyzer also makes aluminium, sodium and lithium. Reserve four of the ten stored silicon for electrical steel in the first ME storage housing. Collecting 26 additional silicon and 26 aluminium together takes thirteen batches of seven clay dust. We had four dust in stores, so 22 terracotta blocks at four dust per block will cover it. The Lost Cities library floor near (-166,65,249) is one known source.

Use the LV macerator for terracotta, then feed the clay dust to the electrolyzer in batches of 63 and 28. Collect all four products between recipes. For the EBF run, leave the shared 128 EU/t feeder available and check the CEF's charge first. The full CEF holds just over 2.5 million EU. Put the five-minute run alongside an overworld ore trip or work at the bench. Silicon dioxide electrolysis is another LV silicon recipe but does not yield the aluminium needed for MV hulls.

The supply trip yielded 35 gray terracotta in five hammer strikes at the library. The upper floor around (-168,65,249) gave way into the floor below; the player reached y58 safely. The macerator received 22 blocks. Its 88 dust plus four already in stores went to the electrolyzer in four deliveries of 12, 20, 48 and 12. This is enough for all thirteen batches with one clay dust left over. The other thirteen terracotta are spare. Run scripts/wiremill.py --machine electrolyzer to collect finished products, including all the aluminium. The first machine controller was assembled from two aluminium plates and one redstone during this run, and quest 790 paid a nickel.

The EBF produced the first boule from 32 dust and one tiny gallium. The two cave walks and bedrock excavation occupied the run; the controller was idle with the boule in its output bus on return. Quest 80 paid a nickel. The boule is on the workshop's first digital storage cell. The optional Element 014 bounty asks for 64 silicon ingots and remains open.
