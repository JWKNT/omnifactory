# Black Quartz for the first micro miners

The Advanced Extended Crafting Table and Titanium Heavy Plating are both prerequisites for the Tier Two Micro Miner. The table needs an Advanced Catalyst, whose components need Black Steel and Luminessence. The recipe for nine Black Steel Dust is three Steel Dust, two Black Bronze Dust, two Void Crystals and two Red Coal in the LV mixer. The two unfamiliar ingredients come from the Atomic Reconstructor and the Extra Utilities Resonator.

The workshop already has one Black Quartz on the parts shelf. Page 74 checks one loose crystal and an Aluminium Casing. Page 75 checks a Machine Block. The casing takes one quartz and eight aluminium plates. The Machine Block takes one quartz and eight steel plates. The survey counted only two steel ingots during the mainframe order. Show a loose crystal to page 74 before consuming it. Find a second crystal from Black Quartz Ore in the world or crystallize Crushed Black Quartz in the existing autoclave with 1,000 mB water. The quest book describes the quartzite electrolysis route. Save aluminium for the wiremill interface before committing the eight plates.

Budget five lead plates, two MV emitters, one Endereye Lens and the Aluminium Casing for the Reconstructor. The Resonator takes the Machine Block, four Red Alloy Plates and four Resonating Redstone Crystals. The Manual Mill needs another such crystal, a Redstone Gear and two Polished Stone. The mill opens the Extra Utilities checkbox page. Four Stoneburnt then satisfy the first grid-power quest. This is a later workshop branch, with no materials taken from the HV orders.

The additional aluminium charge is in the blast furnace. For black quartz, the LV macerator gives four crushed quartzite per ore. Two ore are enough for eight impure dust, then eight washed Quartzite Dust. The MV electrolyzer takes four dust for one Crushed Black Quartz, and the water-fed autoclave takes 1,000 mB and 150 ticks per crystal. Two OmniNickels buy 32 Quartzite Ore if none is available; the Tier One Micro Miner can supply it later. The two-crystal work order is queued beside the interface build. The original loose Black Quartz is still on the parts shelf.

Pages 74 and 75 each pay one OmniNickel. Together they repay the two-nickel ore order. The workshop order can craft and claim either casing when its eight plates are free. It keeps one crystal loose for page 74 and releases the aluminium only after the wiremill interface is in place.

Two nickels bought 32 Quartzite Ore. Two entered the macerator. Together they yielded eight crushed ore, which the bench hammer turned into eight impure quartzite dust. The remaining 30 raw ore are on the metal shelf.

The electrolyzer and autoclave finished the eight dust. The parts chest held three Black Quartz. An order for six aluminium plates joins the two already in ME to supply the first casing. For the Machine Block, a sixteen-ingot ordinary steel charge will share the furnaces with the HV components. It can use coal dust, charcoal dust or carbon from banked coke with wrought iron in the LV alloy smelter. Reserve four steel plates for the polyethylene sheet mold as well.

The processing compressor delivered the six aluminium plates. The workshop crafted the Aluminium Casing, then presented it with a loose Black Quartz on page 74 and received one OmniNickel. Keep the casing for the Atomic Reconstructor. The next block on this branch requires eight ordinary steel plates and a second crystal.

The sixteen-steel charge finished. Eight ingots became eight plates in the LV compressor. The bench combined those plates and one Black Quartz into the Machine Block, and page 75 paid one OmniNickel. The Manual Mill and Dark Steel Enchanter pages are open. Reserve the block for the Resonator.
