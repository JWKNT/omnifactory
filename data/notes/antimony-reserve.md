# A stack of antimony for solder

Quest 651 opened when the book registered the solder bucket. It pays one OmniQuarter for carrying 64 Antimony Ingots and leaves the metal with the player. A second coin purchase of 32 Stibnite Ore costs four nickels. There are 29 ore left from the first purchase. To turn those 61 ore into 64 ingots, process three through the LV macerator and fire the other 58 directly.

Live JEI gives six crushed ore per raw stibnite. Hammer eighteen crushed ore to eighteen impure dust, then fire those for 54 nuggets and pack six ingots. The electrolyzer also separates clean stibnite into antimony and sulfur, but it takes 1,200 ticks per five dust. The small furnace batch uses existing coke, leaves the electrolyzer available, and needs no secondary macerator drops. Verify the yield before buying the last 32 ore. Start the reserve during the circuit order, with the assembler already running.
