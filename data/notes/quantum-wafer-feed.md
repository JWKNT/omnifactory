The IV assembler also needs Nano CPUs and QBit CPUs for further runs. Reserve one Nano CPU wafer for QBits when their stock falls below eight. The MV cutter yields five QBit CPUs per wafer. Each wafer then takes one phosphide, one Nano CPU wafer and 50 mB radon. The alternative needs two Improved Ender Eyes and 288 mB molten gallium arsenide per wafer.
