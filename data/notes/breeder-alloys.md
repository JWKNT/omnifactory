# The first breeder alloys

The fourth blast furnace and freezer make Yttrium Barium Cuprate for Advanced Plating. Their long Lumium run leaves the MV alloy smelter free to start the other branch. Two boron dust and two steel ingots can make four Ferroboron Alloy. Each ferroboron then pairs with one lithium dust for two Tough Alloy. The factory has 234 lithium dust, so these first charges need no ore purchase. Pages 610 and 611 each pay an OmniQuarter. The tender checks the actual output and handles each reward on its page before advancing.

Hard Carbon Alloy is a separate HV smelter recipe. Two graphite ingots and one diamond make two alloy. Four hard carbon, four cuprate plates and one Basic Plating make two Advanced Plating and open page 347 for two OmniNickels. Page 350 pays an OmniDollar for the starter breeder components. The later Omnium chain on page 680 calls for multiple reactors, so reserve space for parallel builds. Sixteen lunar uraninite ore are being held for that line.

Four Ferroboron Alloy finished in the MV machine. I carried one to page 610 and collected its OmniQuarter. The same smelter then took all four alloy and four lithium dust. Eight Tough Alloy followed, and page 611 paid its OmniQuarter. The next 32 graphite ore cost five OmniPennies. The electric furnace accepted the full stack. The HV smelter is available for the diamond and graphite recipe once the ingots return.

The full page 350 bill is much larger than its first samples. JEI gives eight reactor casings per Robust Tungstensteel Machine Casing and eight Basic Plating. Fifty-four casing blocks therefore need seven crafts, plus one more robust casing for the controller. Its four Advanced Plating need two more Basic Plating. That makes 58 Basic Plating in 29 crafts, costing 116 tungsten plates, 116 Tough Alloy and 29 Steel Heavy Plating. The eight cells add another 32 Tough Alloy and 32 Reinforced Glass. The controller's four circuitExtreme slots accept Nano Processor Assemblies, Quantumprocessors, Refined Processor Mainframes or Microprocessor Arrays. Price those against current stocks once the first IV assembler is funded. Keep these bulk tungsten and circuit orders behind the IV motor and mainframe parts. The first pair of Advanced Plating alone needs two tungsten ingots' worth per Basic Plating and four cold cuprate plates for the final craft.

Four graphite ingots and two diamonds made four Hard Carbon Alloy at 500 EU/t. I collected its OmniQuarter on page 732. The fourth blast furnace then took four Yttrium Barium Cuprate dust. Its controller reported 586 ticks per hot ingot at 1,920 EU/t. The output bus can hold that first charge while the filtered freezer branch is built. Page 199 requires the cooled ingot.

All four hot cuprate ingots reached the fourth furnace output bus. The filtered route needs two ore-dictionary covers, one to send hot ingots to the freezer and one to return cold furnace products to ME. The cluster mill raised zinc foil to 18 against a bill of 16. Five emerald ores are available for the two filter plates. Keep the charge in the bus to test the complete route.

Page 199 paid one OmniNickel at 71h30m. Red extraction from above the fourth furnace now delivers cuprate through the freezer. The bus was then wrenched to face east, freeing its south side for the second cover. That cover excludes ingotHot* and extracts on lime. Both red and lime outlets are enabled. The first grid craft produced two Basic Plating from four tungsten plates, four Tough Alloy and one Steel Heavy Plating. Four cooled cuprate went into the compressor and all four plates finished. One Basic Plating, those four plates and the four Hard Carbon Alloy fund the first Advanced Plating craft.

Page 347 is complete. The second grid craft took one Basic Plating, four cuprate plates and four Hard Carbon Alloy, and returned two Advanced Plating. I carried them to the book and collected two OmniNickels. Page 350 is open. Its [cells and coolers](breeder-cooling-and-cells.md) take another 56 Tough Alloy on top of the 116 needed by the platings.

The gemstone flight leaves the stock of 451 lithium dust available for Tough Alloy. The first restock target is 32 alloy for the eight cells. Each steel ingot and boron dust makes two ferroboron; each ferroboron and lithium dust makes two tough alloy. Keep one full boron dust for board fibers. The workshop has 25 lepidolite ore, 30 crushed and ten impure piles. The HV mill and centrifuge give three tiny boron piles per impure lepidolite; nine tiny piles make one dust. Borax electrolysis needs 23 borax for four boron, and the direct medium mission needs a Tier Five miner and stabilized uranium. Start with the lepidolite already here. The breeder tender can install both alloy patterns and work on the next lepidolite batch while tungsten comes back from the repeat miner.
