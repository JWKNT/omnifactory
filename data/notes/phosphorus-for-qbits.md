# Phosphorus for repeat QBit batches

At 87h47m nine tiny indium piles, one gallium dust and eighteen more washed ore pairs were ready. Phosphorus was the missing element. The LV electrolyzer was idle and the MV electrolyzer was already separating bauxite. The LV cell has five open pattern positions, a working product return and room for the oxygen by-product.

Use five Tricalcium Phospate Dust to make two Phosphate Dust alongside three calcium, then five Phosphate Dust to make one phosphorus and four buckets of oxygen. Encode the phosphate as the primary result of the first pattern so AE can order it. This pack spells the first material “Phospate.” Seven ore in storage can smelt into 21 dust in the Multi Smelter. Ninety-six tiny piles already in ME can make another ten whole dust. JEI also lists two monazite for one phosphate, and nine apatite dust for three phosphate plus calcium and chlorine. The phosphoric acid route requires an existing bucket and ME held none. If a restock is needed, five pennies buy 32 Tricalcium Phospate Ore, enough for 96 dust before electrolysis.

The orders in scripts/phosphorus_orders.py target eight phosphorus dust, enough for 120 QBits once the other materials arrive. Check both encoded patterns and a delivery before relying on them.
