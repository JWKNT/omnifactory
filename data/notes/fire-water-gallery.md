# Fire Water in the bedrock gallery

At 136h29m I checked the exposed bedrock on the gallery floor. At y1 the strip runs from x−147 to −141 along z139 and from x−148 to −141 along both z140 and z141. The air or fire directly above each of these tiles was visible to the player. Leave z141 dry for the walk between the two standing places. A short wall along that side of the first two rows will keep the Fire Water out of the passage. Inspect the ends before pouring. The gallery crosses a chunk boundary at x−144, and both halves are in the same z chunk.

The installed Ender IO configuration allows infinity powder production in the Overworld. Its Fire Water drop chance is 0.025 per eligible random tick. The Vacuum Chest range is six blocks. Place it near the middle, clear of the walking strip. Page 759 asks for two Ender Chests, a Vacuum Chest and a bucket of Fire Water, and instructs the player to chunkload the site. Give the gallery its own matching chest colours and an item return at the factory. Check the workshop grain count before leaving and after time spent in the Void. That measures delivery with the player away.

Page 511 pays for the Vat, which the gallery needs before it can make Fire Water. The alloy smelter can upgrade the existing Simple Machine Chassis for two grains. The Vat also uses two MV pumps, two Fluid Tanks, two Dark Steel Plates, a cauldron and an Obsidian Furnace. Reserve one Diamond Furnace for each remaining ship dynamo before combining two for the Vat. JEI shows 1,000 mB water with a crop and a sugar ingredient making Hootch. It shows 70 mB for the displayed ingredient pair. Check the actual yield with the farm's crops. For Fire Water, one bucket of Hootch, blaze powder and redstone make one bucket. Reserve that first bucket for the quest and then the gallery.

The gallery trip returned 25 grains at 136h34m. All four wheat plants by the factory pool were mature and ME held 366 sugar cane and two blaze powder. The installed Vat recipe multiplies wheat (3) by sugar (1). Its water conversion is 0.25. Two wheat and two sugar should therefore turn six buckets of water into 1,500 mB Hootch, enough for the Fire Water batch. Check the machine's actual output on the first charge. The existing pool wheat routine harvests and replants the row.

For exactly one bucket of Hootch, use two wheat seeds with two sugar and four buckets of water. The mature crop row supplies seeds as well as wheat.
