# Prescient Crystals and the Soul Binder

Each of the three top-tier thrusters takes a Prescient Crystal and an Energetic Thruster. That lower thruster takes a separate Vibrant Crystal. Budget six Vibrant Crystals and three Pulsating Crystals for propulsion, plus sixteen Pulsating Crystals for the mission's flux. Make the six vibrant gems in the existing MV autoclave in runs between Pulsating Iron charges. A single tank can accept the next alloy after its previous charge empties.

JEI gives one Prescient Crystal and returns an empty vial when a Vibrant Crystal and a Shulker Soul Vial run in the Soul Binder. The installed Ender IO recipe specifies 200,000 RF and eight experience levels per cycle. A shop-made empty vial uses three fused quartz and one Soularium. Four Shulker Shells around that vial fill it, so twelve shells and one reusable vial cover the flight. The Shulker model can select shells at the Loot Fabricator. Keep one separate diamond bay running.

Page 542 pays a quarter for the binder. Use the installed 5×5 table and the open socket south of the vibrant power line at (13,82,8). The machine needs sixteen Soularium ingots, four tungstensteel plates, four different heads and a Soul Machine Chassis. Make eight further Soularium into Soul Powder for the chassis. Gold and soul sand go in an alloy smelter. One hellish matter and one sand give four soul sand. At commissioning the workshop had 182 gold, seventeen hellish matter and two other consumers for the first new tungstensteel plates. The third simulation bay can supply the creeper, zombie and enderman heads from the stored models when the diamond reserve is healthy. Four bones in the compressor make the skeleton skull. Check the first soul-binding cycle's actual experience draw before budgeting the other two.

At 114h34m I recovered the Zombie Data Model, rotten flesh and four bones from the old chests. The LV compressor turned the four bones into a Skeleton Skull. Six rods from the LV lathe went into the eight-bar wrench recipe for the Simple Machine Chassis. With the diamond stock covering power, the third simulation bay switched to Zombie and delivered two pristine matter to its fabricator. Select Zombie Head there before switching the bay back.

The third fabricator returned twelve Zombie Heads. Four iron bars, four iron plates and one Grains of Infinity item made the Simple Machine Chassis. The collected Skeleton Skull turned up in the east half of the parts chest at (10,80,4). The binder tender now retrieves that skull, and future workshop sorting sends skulls to ME.
