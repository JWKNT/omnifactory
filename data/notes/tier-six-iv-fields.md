# The Tier Six miner's two IV field generators

Each generator takes four Elite circuits and an Improved Nether Star. The table uses four single Lumium wires per generator. The IV assembler can substitute molten Lumium for the wires. The workshop has two wires and two Crystal Circuits, and will need eight Elite circuits in total.

The LuV assembler gives four Crystal Circuits per batch. Two batches need two multilayer boards, two Crystal CPUs, two Nano CPUs, four fine niobium-titanium wires, sixteen SMD resistors, eight SMD transistors and 144 mB solder. At 133h01m all the item costs except transistors were in stock; the workshop had two transistors. The eight-output alternative needs a ZPM assembler and a Crystal SoC. Quantum Processors, Nanoprocessor Arrays and Microprocessor Mainframes also qualify, but each recipe returns one and uses more parts. Use the existing four-output LuV pattern.

One MV assembler run produces 32 transistors for six Fine Annealed Copper Wires, one Gallium Plate and 288 mB polyethylene. The wiremill turns the one stored annealed copper wire into four fine wires, in addition to the two already in ME. There are 112 tiny piles of gallium. Nine tiny piles make one dust; smelt the dust and compress the ingot. This starts the next circuit lot without having to wash more ore.

At 133h16m the electric furnace held four finished Gallium Ingots. The basic wiremill held four Fine Annealed Copper Wires and two more were in ME. These cover the first 32-transistor batch once the compressor returns a Gallium Plate and the MV assembler receives its polyethylene.

The four fine wires were collected. The MV assembler holds 108 mB polyethylene, 180 mB short of a transistor batch. Its source reactor contains 64 buckets of oxygen and 120 mB ethylene. Another 200 mB ethylene are in fluid storage. Use the existing hull refill and assembler pipe for the transistor lot as soon as the six wires are available. Give the assembler the next polyethylene before drawing another sheet batch.

At 133h49m the workshop compressor held a finished Gallium Plate. Six Fine Annealed Copper Wires were available, and the MV assembler held 540 mB polyethylene. The LuV assembler had 400 mB solder. The workshop has two multilayer boards, three Crystal CPUs, five Nano CPUs, ten fine niobium-titanium wires and 40 SMD resistors. Once the transistor batch returns, these cover two four-output Crystal Circuit runs. The roof assembler now has a measured player-loading route in case all crafting processors are occupied.

At 134h03m the MV assembler had produced 32 SMD transistors. The LuV assembler used eight of them in two 200-tick runs. Its solder fell from 400 to 256 mB and the circuit stock rose from two to ten. The eight Elite circuits for both fields are now ready. Nine of ten Nether Star Nuggets made one Nether Star; the HV chemical bath takes 2,000 mB radon to improve it. ME had 30,000 mB radon. For the second star, three tips and a core are available; the East tip is missing. The grid needs six more single Lumium wires for the two fields. Three more ingots through the MV wiremill will cover them. ME has no molten Lumium for the alternative assembler recipe.

At 134h08m the first Improved Nether Star was visible in the HV bath output. The star made from nuggets went through a 1,920-tick cycle at 384 EU/t. The bath retained 500 mB radon. Collect the star and make the Lumium wires for the first generator.

The second Improved Nether Star finished in the HV bath by 134h29m. The first was already in workshop stock. Four Tin Alloy Dust, one Energized Clathrate and nine Luminessence are available for the next Lumium Blend. The outstanding blend ingredient is two Sterling Silver Dust. One batch makes five from four silver dust and one copper dust. The LV mill received eight silver ingots.

Both Improved Nether Stars have reached stock. The next blend received its Sterling Silver Dust, and the network accepted all six outstanding Lumium wires. At tick 9,702,000 the fourth furnace was partway through the batch with two blend in the bus and 750 mB mana at the hatch.

At 134h47m the fourth furnace paused 521 ticks into the first 1,837-tick heat. It draws 1,920 EU/t, so a full heat costs 3,527,040 EU. The converter has just one basic cell and holds at most 2,565,536 EU. Fit a second basic capacitor to bring it above five million EU. Its grid recipe takes two lead, one copper, three redstone and one sulfur. ME held one lead ingot and 264 lead dust, so the workshop can make the missing lead without borrowing an Assembly Line battery.
