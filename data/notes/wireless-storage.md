# When the workshop can go wireless

A Wireless Crafting Terminal would let the player request and collect materials at the north machines. Live JEI shows a Fluix Pearl, a Wireless Terminal and an ME Crafting Terminal. Both the handheld Wireless Terminal and the Wireless Access Point consume a Wireless Receiver. Each receiver requires an EV Emitter and three titanium plates. Titanium is still waiting on the Vacuum Freezer. The handheld's Dense Energy Cell also takes eight ordinary Energy Cells and a Calculation Processor. The ME Security Terminal used to link the handheld needs a 16k storage component. Revisit after the first titanium batch.

Page 108 offers an earlier improvement for programming interfaces. Its ME Interface Terminal costs one Bright Illuminated Panel, one Engineering Processor and one whole ME Interface. It can manage pattern slots across the base. The next interface still goes to the electric furnace, where its patterns can save a trip on every order. Once the inscriber and interface hardware have batch recipes, reassess a central interface terminal for the expanding machine rows.

Titanium and the first Nano Processor Assemblies are available. Reserve one assembly for the east furnace converter. The two receivers together need two EV emitters. The dense cell alone needs 32 Certus crystals. Two nickels bought 32 Certus ore. The new pattern on the bulk furnace returned 32 GregTech Certus gems to ME. The water-fed autoclave at (4,81,4) can convert those into AE2 crystals. Its overhead conduit connection still needs verification. A second fixed terminal at the EV deck costs only a Bright Illuminated Panel and a Basic circuit and can save trips while the wireless parts are in production.

At 115h the north terminal and pattern shelves span three floors. The fourth guidance sensor and the breeder require materials on different floors, and the HV mill has filled all eighteen of its current pattern slots. Page 108 would let the player file patterns from one terminal. JEI accepts any of the three illuminated panels. Page 814 uses a reversible one-item conversion once unlocked. Page 108 pays a nickel. The separate extended-pattern terminal on page 813 needs a PackagedAuto Encoder and is a later build.

The growth chamber can combine two sets of charged certus, nether quartz and redstone into four Fluix Crystals. Compress three for the panel. The alloy smelter returns two Quartz Glass per glass and Certus Quartz Dust. The MV cutter makes nine diamond plates from one stored block for the Engineering Processor and later prints. The next single interface goes to the thermal centrifuge. Build the terminal with spare hardware and verify its screen before routing any pattern through it.

At 122h49m the ME Interface Terminal is installed at (14,81,−5), facing north. A newly encoded Enderium card remained in the player's inventory when the walk to its eastern interface failed. The player recovered it at the correct furnace; the blend-to-ingot pattern is now installed. Use the existing interface terminal for the next distant pattern. Its GUI associates each displayed machine with the same position its highlight button marks. Match that position and dimension, scroll to the actual row, put the carried card into an empty slot on that row, then check the destination interface for the exact encoded pattern. Keep the local interface accessible for machines the terminal does not show.
