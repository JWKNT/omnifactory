# Keep the gas bounty stacks for Omnium

The LV Fluid Solidifier opens elemental quests 613, 619 and 620. Each pays a quarter for 64 solidified hydrogen, nitrogen or oxygen. Each uses the reusable ball mold and 1,000 mB of its gas per item. JEI lists 500 ticks per cast, so an LV solidifier needs 26 minutes 40 seconds of continuous work to cast a full stack. Dense Hydrogen also unpacks into Solidified Hydrogen.

The 9×9 Mote of Omnium recipe uses one solidified piece each of hydrogen, helium, nitrogen, oxygen, fluorine, neon, chlorine, argon, krypton, xenon, mercury and radon. Bank the bounty stacks for that recipe. Keep the first plate mold and solidifier on polyethylene. For bulk elemental casting, add a second solidifier with a ball mold and feed whole batches from tanks. Preserve oxygen for plastics and hydrogen for deuterium before drawing surplus into that line.
