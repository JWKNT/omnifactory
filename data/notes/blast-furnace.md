The top and bottom use eleven heatproof casings, one controller, two LV energy hatches, item input and output buses, and fluid input and output hatches. The controller goes in the middle of an outside bottom edge. Plan access to both energy hatches and enough floor space for a second furnace.

Live JEI gives two single wires per cupronickel ingot in the wiremill, two singles per double wire, and eight doubles per coil. Sixteen coils therefore take 128 cupronickel ingots.
