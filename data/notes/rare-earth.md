# Rare Earth from the fluorine supply

The MV electrolyzer used bastnasite for fluorine. Send the next three of the remaining 26 ore through the LV macerator, hammer the twelve crushed ore, and centrifuge all twelve impure dust. Each dust gives three tiny piles of Rare Earth and one clean Bastnasite Dust. Save the clean dust for the next fluorine charge. Impure redstone gives only one tiny pile, and the alternatives using purified ore or dust would add a cleaning step.

Page 139 needs nine tiny piles together and pays an OmniNickel. Show the 36 tiny piles before combining them into four Rare Earth. The next page needs a full Yttrium Dust. Each separation gives at most one small pile of yttrium, and the quest book recommends an HV centrifuge to guarantee the chanced outputs. Keep all four Rare Earth for that machine.
