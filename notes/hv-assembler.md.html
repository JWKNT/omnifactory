Reserve two mainframes for the HV assembler

The first EV circuit opens the Vacuum Freezer, PackagedAuto and HV Assembling Machine pages. Quest 132 requires the SMD Diodes and Second Tier Three Circuits pages, and pays two OmniQuarters. The HV assembler recipe has two circuitExtreme slots. JEI accepts a Refined Processor Mainframe in each. A first batch of two mainframes can therefore pay the quest and supply both slots. No quest in this book awards the mainframe itself.

For the pair, reserve 32 SMD resistors, 16 capacitors, 12 transistors, eight diodes, eight RAM, eight stainless steel frames, sixteen Vibrant Alloy wires and four Refined Processor Arrays. Each mainframe takes 500 ticks at 110 EU/t. The two JEI recipes differ only in their fluid. The pair takes 144 mB of soldering alloy or 288 mB of tin. Keep the polyethylene in the MV assembler until the SMD batches finish. The first 24-resistor batch falls eight short of the pair even before counting the circuits used to make the arrays.

The HV machine itself also needs two HV robot arms, two HV conveyors, the HV hull and two insulated gold cables. Keep the MV machine available after the upgrade so it can continue making SMDs.

After using the first array for patterns, the two mainframes still need four more arrays. If all eight spare Refined Circuits survive, forty more circuits take ten MV cycles. Sixteen processor cycles, four array cycles and two mainframe cycles bring the additional solder bill to 2,304 mB, or sixteen ingots. Across these four stages reserve 30 Plastic Circuit Boards, 10 CPU chips, 24 RAM, 84 Fine Tin Alloy Wires, 32 single Vibrant Alloy wires, 112 SMD resistors, 88 capacitors, 92 transistors and eight diodes, plus eight stainless plates and eight stainless frames. The current RAM stock is 32 before the first array takes four. Keep one more plain silicon wafer ready to engrave if three CPU chips remain from the first lot; one wafer cuts into eight chips. Recount the spare circuits before making substrates. Every four consumed elsewhere add one circuit cycle.

The workshop had eight CPU chips and eleven plain silicon wafers when the models reached six of eight. The laser and cutter can prepare one more CPU wafer alongside the model order. The target of sixteen chips covers the five for the first array batch and ten for the two-mainframe batch, provided the eight spare Refined Circuits remain available. The models and acid still use the older Basic Circuits.

The second CPU wafer finished engraving. The MV cutter took the wafer and the ME inventory now shows sixteen Central Processing Unit chips. The two-mainframe chip reserve is ready.

The post-board order in scripts/mainframe_run.py takes the 1,512 mB PE charge, assembles the three SMD reserves, then loads sixteen solder ingots' worth of fluid. It requests 48 total refined circuits, sixteen processors, 32 Vibrant Alloy wires, four arrays, and finally the two mainframes. run/first-mainframes checkpoints the delivered stages. These stages are queued. For the eight frames, the bench makes four per three plates and five rods with a wrench. The lathe gives two rods per ingot. With the eight plates in the arrays, the combined stainless bill is nineteen ingots. Fifteen were in storage at the last survey, so a second eighteen-ingot blast batch can also supply HV hardware.
