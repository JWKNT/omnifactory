Choosing a thermal centrifuge for the first breeder

Page 672 unlocks after the reactor page and pays one OmniDollar for eight NuclearCraft Uranium-238 items and one Uranium-235. The regular HV centrifuge already in the factory handles depleted fuel. The Thermal Centrifuge is a separate GregTech machine. Its dust and ingot recipes each take 3,200 base ticks at 48 EU/t per clump. Nine conversions take 24 minutes at MV, about twelve at HV, or about six at EV. The Tier Five flight will need substantially more than that first sample.

At 108h48m the MV version could use two of four available motors and one of two hulls, but its two quadruple Cupronickel wires were missing. HV needs two HV motors, an HV hull, two quadruple Kanthal wires and two gold cables. EV needs two EV motors, an EV hull, two quadruple Nichrome wires and two aluminium cables. The tungsten expedition and EV furnace are already using the available MV motors and EV hulls. Stage an independent powered bay after the miner parts are secure. Prefer HV or EV for repeated fuel orders and fit an ME interface for the two clump recipes. Preserve the existing HV centrifuge's ore patterns.

For feed, two OmniQuarters buy 32 Uranium 238 Ore. Its purified ore gives three tiny Uranium 235 dust in the thermal centrifuge. The existing HV centrifuge can also get three tiny Uranium 235 dust from an impure pile of Uraninite Dust; three OmniNickels buy 32 Uraninite Ore. Check actual returns before buying a second lot. A cheaper lot of Pitchblende costs five pennies; compare the in-game by-products before assigning it to the same job.

The mission consumes 32 Stabilized Plutonium. JEI shows the same two-item ball mold output for 144 mB of each of three different fluids. NuclearCraft's tiny Plutonium-239 clumps give 16 mB each in a GregTech fluid extractor. The LEU-235 route returns eight of those clumps per depleted item plus other inventory. Keep the Plutonium-241 clumps separate. Their NuclearCraft item IDs are different from the GregTech 241 nugget accepted by the extractor. Account for the actual recipe and item ID before crediting them to a mission.

The roof survey found a powered 512 V, four-amp gold cable at (12,84,1) with air immediately above. Use that outlet for the first HV Thermal Centrifuge once the Tier Two ship leaves. A downward-facing interface at (12,86,1) can join the existing fluix network with one cable at (13,86,1). The MV comparison would save two Kanthal windings but double the first sample's machine time. The EV chemistry cable is already sharing the wafer cutter, electrolyzer and engine smelter. Two Kanthal ingots and one dust were in ME at the survey. Make the remaining Kanthal and two HV motors while the cargo is in processing. The machine and its two patterns still need construction and a delivery test.

The first eight common clumps and one rare clump can come from the same four Uranium 238 Ore. The mill gives eight crushed ore, the MV washer purifies them, and the HV mill makes eight purified dust piles. The ordinary centrifuge lists one common dust and three tiny rare dust per purified pile. The mill also lists a rare dust by-product, which the budget can do without. Nine tiny rare dust fill the crafting grid for one full dust. No quest awards these two items directly. Buy one 32-ore lot once the thermal machine is ready, test four first, and record the actual yield before committing the other 28.

The 111h12m survey found an Endervoir at (12,86,1), occupying the planned interface socket. Keep the water supply in service. The revised site is (10,85,2), south of the iron reactor’s new gold cable at (10,85,1). Its top interface at (10,86,2) can join the live network through (11,86,2) and (12,86,2). Both machines can share the same gold cable and network branch.

JEI also accepts the full NuclearCraft Plutonium-241 item (metadata 8) in the fluid extractor. The nine-tiny-clump grid recipe accepts metadata 10 and returns one of those full items. This halves the game fuel bill for the first Naquadah flight. Nine spent LEU-235 items give 72 tiny clumps of each useful type. Pack each set into eight full items. Extract and cast each fluid separately with the ball mold for sixteen stabilized items per set. Reserve the other in-game products for their own quest lines.

Start with nine U-235 clumps and 72 U-238 clumps. The HV thermal centrifuge can begin before the casing shell is complete. From six stored U-238 dust, the two-quarter lot of 32 uranium ore brings at least 64 more via washing and centrifuging, with guaranteed tiny 235 from the purified piles. The by-product ore route on uraninite gives only a 14% base chance for common uranium and is less suitable for the bulk order. Keep the first eight-plus-one clumps until page 672 pays.
