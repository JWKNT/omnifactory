Feeding the first aluminium batch

Quest 69 requires a GregTech aluminium ingot after the blast furnace quest. This instance's JEI shows a reliable LV source of aluminium dust in the Basic Electrolyzer. Seven clay dust take 400 ticks at 30 EU/t and yield two sodium, two silicon, one lithium and two aluminium dust. One aluminium dust in the EBF takes 400 ticks at 120 EU/t (48,000 EU per ingot). The installed CEF accepts a large charge before a run: 1.49 million EU was measured while the workshop was active. Verify the two LV energy hatches are being fed and see the first ingot through before building a larger power plant.

The aluminium ore in the camp and void stores is libvulpes:ore0@9. JEI lists furnace smelting into libvulpes:productingot@9 and no uses for that ingot. Reserve the ore until a real use is verified. Ordinary bauxite dust electrolysis also makes aluminium, but that particular recipe needs 60 EU/t and an MV electrolyzer. The clay route works in LV and supplies silicon for the circuit line. Check the live clay, electrolyzer and furnace recipes when ordering the machines.

Clay is exposed under the lake just west of the initial sugar cane plot. Shallow blocks around (-214,61,203) lead to a larger bed. Twenty-four clay balls came back; 21 were ground into clay dust with the iron mortar and recipe index 1. That makes three electrolyzer batches. An LV electrolyzer needs a hull, two basic circuits, four gold wires, one tin cable and one glass. The planner supports gregtech:machine@240. Quest 260 detects the machine and all the clay products, so collect every output from its separate slots.

The basic electrolyzer now runs at (4,81,6), west of the workshop power cable. The first three clay batches yielded six aluminium dust, six sodium, six silicon and three lithium. The output slots were collected, quest 260 paid a nickel, and the EBF's first two aluminium ingots completed quest 69. There is a second clay source already at the factory. Eight gray terracotta from Lost Cities floors gave 32 clay dust in the macerator. Twenty-eight went into the electrolyzer for another eight aluminium, bringing the combined furnace order to fourteen ingots. Reserve nine plates for the first MV hull. The casing costs eight; the hull recipe also needs one plate, two wrought plates and two single insulated copper cables.

The first MV hull has been crafted and quest 259 paid an omniquarter. The furnace finished all fourteen ingots. Nine went into plates for the hull. The other five were visible in the EBF output bus at the end of the run. The CEF held 1.37 million EU at that point. An isolated MV cable and CEF will be needed when the hull becomes a powered MV machine; the existing four-amp LV line still runs the single-block workshop.
