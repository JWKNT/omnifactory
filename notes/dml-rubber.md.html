Rubber for the north feeder

The seven fourfold cables alone take 28 insulated wires. A workshop survey found 31 slimeballs and nine Pristine Slime Matter left from earlier simulations. This instance's JEI shows that smelting one slimeball yields one Rubber. The LV compressor turns each Rubber into one Rubber Sheet. The hammer consumes two Rubber per sheet. One pristine can supply 32 slimeballs, enough insulation for all seven cable blocks with four sheets left over.

scripts/lv_order.py uses the electric or ordinary furnaces for slimeballs while raw rubber is short, leaving one ball for crafting. When the stock drops below sixteen balls, scripts/shulker_round.py can feed another stored pristine to the idle fabricator. Diamond, pearl and metal jobs retain their place in that machine. The grove still supplies wood and saplings. Centrifuging rubber logs gives rubber only half the time and would compete with the new centrifuge's deuterium order.
