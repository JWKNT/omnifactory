Silicon for the inscriber

A steel level emitter exposed a shared shortage. The calculation processor needed printed silicon, and the logic processors for new ME interfaces need the same print. The workshop had 30 silicon dioxide dust from stone processing and two silicon dust already separated.

The MV electrolyzer can split 30 silicon dioxide dust into ten silicon dust and oxygen. This puts the oxygen separator's material stream to use and leaves carbon and blast-furnace time free. JEI confirms ordinary smelting turns each dust into one ingot. The compressor already advertises silicon plates. The north furnace interface had one remaining slot, so scripts/silicon_stock.py assigns it to silicon ingots. The workshop requests eight plates and holds up to sixteen combined plates and ingots. Once the north interface feeds the Multi Smelter, its silicon pattern moves with its glass and wrought iron recipes. Check for an actual delivery and the steel cutoff before treating either buffer as commissioned.

The silicon smelting pattern is installed. The first AE job for two ingots shared the three CPUs with a rubber sheet job and a copper wire job. The north furnace finished processing its slimeballs, and the two silicon ingots appeared in ME. Verified the smelting stage. The plate and steel-control commissioning checks follow.

Four thin PVC sheets occupied the MV electrolyzer input. JEI lists them for assembling, macerating and fluid extraction. They were misplaced and prevented the commissioned bauxite feed from entering. Silicon dioxide electrolysis draws 30 EU/t in this instance, so the idle LV electrolyzer at (4,81,6) could take the silica while the bauxite separator was occupied. All 30 dust were consumed, ten silicon dust appeared in the output, and oxygen rose while it ran. The first two silicon plates also became Printed Silicon. One went into the Calculation Processor for the steel emitter.

The electrical steel line needs raw silicon dust as well. With 298 stone dust in ME, the centrifuge can take 45 and return 30 silicon dioxide. Electrolysis turns that into ten silicon dust and 20 buckets of oxygen. The refill now runs when raw silicon is short even with oxygen in reserve, and keeps sixteen dust for alloys before smelting more silicon for AE. The next check is a delivery of electrical steel while the processor line is also drawing silicon.
