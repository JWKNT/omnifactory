How much deuterium the first Microversium stack takes

Quest 594 wants 64 Microversium Ingots together and pays one quarter. The first bucket of deuterium covers twenty ingots. Keep it in workshop storage until the blast furnace is ready. A further 2,200 mB covers the other forty-four ingots. Quest 216 also needs the tier-three circuit and Elite Catalyst quests before the first projector can register.

The installed instance lists two fresh-material blast recipes. Each takes one steel ingot or one steel dust, one Energetic Blend and 50 mB deuterium for 600 ticks at 120 EU/t. The recipe requires 1,700 K and the assembled furnace reported 1,800 K. The other JEI routes recover previously manufactured Microversium from parts, nuggets, dust, fluid or Modular Machinery buses. The book offers no direct ingot or blend reward. The installed LV mixer gives two Energetic Blend per redstone and glowstone dust. Budget 32 of each for a stack, along with 64 steel and 32 minutes of blast time.

The centrifuge may run while the workshop builds the oven and circuits. Its additional 2,200 mB requires 110,000 mB hydrogen, equivalent to 55 water buckets. scripts/deuterium_line.py can refill in four-bucket batches when hydrogen falls below 4,000 mB, leaving the shared electrolyzer available between charges. It counts both machines’ hydrogen, product and pending water before loading more. At full rate the additional deuterium takes 2 h 26 min 40 s. The oxygen also serves plastics and annealing. Keep the blast hatches available for the short electronics jobs while the deuterium accumulates.

A dedicated water electrolyzer is queued after the oven quest to remove the recurring bucket trips. The socket at (4,82,7) is directly above an existing LV cable and immediately south of the centrifuge. The new water source belongs at (4,82,8), above the assembler. Its neighbour gets water automatically; the electrolyzer’s north fluid output can then supply the centrifuge. The current centrifuge output faces east, so its south input is available. scripts/hydrogen_supply.py checks the two new blocks, oxygen production and a working or supplied centrifuge. The original electrolyzer remains available for rock salt, clay and other item batches. Both new sockets were air on survey.

The centrifuge had finished 160 mB of the additional 2,200 mB when the soldered circuits started. I took the empty Ender IO Fluid Tank to the water-fed autoclave, filled it in the tank widget and emptied it into the original LV electrolyzer. The transfer delivered exactly 16,000 mB in one trip. The electrolyzer accepted its first bucket immediately and displayed 15,000 mB remaining. This charge supplies 32,000 mB hydrogen, enough for 640 mB deuterium. scripts/electrolyzer_water.py repeats that transfer between circuit batches when the hydrogen reserve runs low. The dedicated second electrolyzer remains on order.

Use (5,82,7), directly east of the dedicated electrolyzer, for the new water source. I surveyed it as air above a cable. The earlier location would have put an infinite water source directly on top of the solder assembler, which has only one input tank. The revised blast feeder goes overhead at y83 to leave both new machine sockets free.

The first stack earns the quest, and the complete projector needs another twenty Microversium Ingots for casings built into its controller and vent. The centrifuge reserve is now 3,200 mB in addition to the first stored bucket. See small-projector.md for the full 84-ingot count and the 21-casing order.

With 2,240 mB deuterium in the centrifuge, the original electrolyzer stopped despite having 24 buckets of water. Its oxygen output was full at 64,000 mB. From the tank page I filled the portable Ender IO tank with 16,000 mB of oxygen, then emptied it through the blast furnace input hatch at (1,80,6). The hatch verified all 16,000 mB. The electrolyzer resumed at 30 EU/t, with 48,000 mB oxygen and 23,000 mB water showing during its first new cycle. The centrifuge needs 960 mB more deuterium for the projector reserve. Use the hatch oxygen for annealing, or return it to the empty portable tank before charging deuterium into that hatch.
