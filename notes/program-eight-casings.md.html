A permanent selector for machine casings

The new miner fuel reactor and elemental solidifier need MV hulls. The north Assembly Line converter and transformer need EV hulls. The ME hull recipes already exist, but making each casing at the terminal still takes a wrench and a player visit.

The live recipe book shows two routes for each casing from LV through IV. Both use eight plates. The assembler keeps one integrated circuit set to eight and runs without a wrench. I picked the LV assembler because the casing recipe fits LV and the workshop already has a powered socket at (5,83,6). A full interface above it can touch the glass cable at (6,84,6). This occupies an optional roof foothold. The west aisle and other approaches to the centrifuge stay open. The first patterns will be for aluminium and titanium. The inscriber gets the first spare interface once its output filter is fitted.

This bay is planned. Verify a casing returned to ME before counting it as working.
