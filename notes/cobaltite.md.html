Cobaltite and the arsenic quest

Thirty-one cobaltite ore remained from the first cobalt-brass purchase. The LV macerator gives six crushed cobaltite from one ore. Hammer those into impure dust and wash the stack in the workshop cauldron. The ordinary furnace gives a cobalt ingot directly and misses the arsenic. After the fluorine and chrome charges, the MV electrolyzer can split the six dust into two cobalt, two arsenic and two sulfur dust. Quest 266 needs one arsenic and pays a nickel. Realgar also works at LV, and the MV recipes accept enargite and tennantite. None of those three ores were in the last survey. This charge is queued for the electrolyzer; check the output before counting it.

One ore has now gone through the macerator, hammer and cauldron. Six clean cobaltite dust are ready for electrolysis after the chrome charge.

The MV electrolyzer accepted all six dust just after the chrome page. It processed them three at a time, 600 ticks per operation. At the end the output slots showed two cobalt dust, two arsenic dust and two sulfur dust. The next collection takes the arsenic to page 266 while the first stainless steel batch runs.

The output was collected, and holding one arsenic dust paid the nickel on page 266. Both cobaltite operations are finished.
