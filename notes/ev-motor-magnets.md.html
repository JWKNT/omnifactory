Magnets for the first EV motors

Ten EV motors need ten magnetic neodymium rods. The lathe makes two rods per ingot and the polarizer magnetizes them. The assembler motor recipe itself needs 1,920 EU/t, so the first motors for the EV assembler will be made at the crafting terminal.

The first HV grinder will get bonus neodymium from bastnasite. Its diamond head is ready, but its gold insulation and stainless steel are still in production. Buying 32 neodymium ore cost six nickels. Four of those ores give eight crushed ore in the existing LV macerator. Grind those again, centrifuge the eight impure dust, blast the clean dust and turn the ingots into sixteen magnets. That leaves six spares and 28 ore for future jobs. Keep the 23 bastnasite ore for the HV grinder's bonus slots. scripts/ev_motor_magnets.py services the batch between workshop jobs.
