Keeping the polymer line supplied

The first lake trip with an iron excavator returned empty. Several exposed beds lie below normal walking reach. On the second trip, swimming down to a shallow bed at (-231,61,199) and pulsing the Fluxomagnet recovered four clay balls. The bench made one clay block. The polymer smelter paired it with one Pulsating Dust, and four polymer reached the Shulker chamber. The trip and the travel cooldown cost several minutes. The excavator stays available for surface work.

The long-term supply is the cobblestone generator. JEI has a single macerator recipe at each step from cobblestone to gravel, sand and Dust Block. The water-fed LV reactor turns one Dust Block and one bucket of water into a clay block. A mortar can do the crushing at the bench but wears down. The Igneous Extruder can make sand from 1,500 mB water and a lava charge with Clastic Deposition. That augment costs four mana-infused ingots and four plates, which makes it a later option. Library terracotta is a fast intermediate supply. Each block takes 120 ticks and gives four clay dust, enough for one clay block.

A second Basic Macerator is queued with the oven order. The first one is busy doubling copper, iron and nickel. The new one goes at (6,82,4) above the water-fed reactor, with one fourfold cable at (5,82,4). Both spaces were air. Quest 270 required the original macerator and no quest pays a spare. The order uses a hull, two circuits, three tin cables, a motor, a piston and a wrought iron buzzsaw blade. Once the machine is installed, the clay helper collects its outputs and reloads the next stage in batches of up to 32. The existing reactor and polymer smelter are already working. Check the new mill for charge and a completed batch before counting it as commissioned.

During reactor construction the dedicated socket was still empty and the clay run was grinding another batch of cobblestone by hand. The mill was on the list of optional parts, which the workshop rarely reaches during large machine orders. Give it its own construction turn immediately after the first plastic sheets and before the AE2 hardware. Including it in the combined bill added a shortfall of sixteen iron ore. Buy 32 for two nickels, keep the existing ore mill busy with the remaining copper, and use the arriving iron for the third LV hull and the piston.
