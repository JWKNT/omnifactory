Four models for the extraterrestrial bounty

Page 691 pays ten OmniQuarters for showing the Enderman, Shulker, Wither and Ender Dragon models together. It consumes none of them. The working Enderman and Shulker models can return to their chambers after detection. The reward buys five lots of 32 diamond ore at the current price, which makes a second Nether Star worthwhile while the projector workshop runs.

Make two blank models. Each needs one pulsating mesh, four electrical steel plates, three fine gold wires and one refined circuit. One pristine Spider Matter yields 32 string for the mesh. White wool or plant fiber from storage would also work. Use the existing star core and north tip for the Wither model, and keep the medium flight's 64 Petrotheum Dust when making the south tip. The Dragon model needs one Dragon Lair Data, made with one Impossible Realm Data and eight Extraterrestrial Matter. The medium ore flight has first call on four Impossible Realm Data. One subsequent Enderman payout will fund the Dragon. Each model also takes seven Extraterrestrial Matter. The workshop drawers already contain thousands.
