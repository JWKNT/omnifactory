119 h 00 min played · first sent at 116 h 07 min

Reserve one cold Naquadah ingot for the wafer line

The Tier Five flight returns 256 Naquadah Dust. The first cold ingot pays a quarter on page 323. Reserve one ingot before drawing the coil wire. A single boule then supplies 64 wafers. Page 796 pays another quarter and opens the System-on-Chip quest. The advanced version also requires Draconium Ingots (288).

JEI calls for nine silicon blocks, one cold Naquadah ingot and 16,000 mB argon at 8,000 EU/t for 1,440 ticks. The pack script sets 5,400 K. The fifth furnace already meets the heat requirement, but its controller currently reports only 4,096 input voltage. Its EV hatch and basic capacitor suffice for the Naquadah metal heat at 1,920 EU/t. Verify the controller reports at least 8,000 input voltage and can sustain 11,520,000 EU before charging the boule. The Assembly Line's separately powered hatches are a working reference.

The silicon blocks need 81 ingots. The Multi Smelter already has the dust-to-ingot pattern. Keep a separate dust allowance for silicone insulation and plates for printed silicon. The factory had 30 silicon dust and 199 buckets of argon at 116h07m. Avoid diverting silicon into storage blocks while the breeder and ship still depend on insulated cables.

Use 240 mB lubricant in the cutter for 400 base ticks at 384 EU/t. Water needs 960 mB and 1,600 ticks. Keep an uncut wafer for comparing later recipes. An Amethyst Lens yields one ASoC Wafer and a Nether Star Lens yields two SoC Wafers at 1,920 EU/t. Each engraving page pays a nickel. The two field generators are finished. The next reusable Nether Star Lens takes one star and 1,000 mB water in an autoclave. The Amethyst Lens requires an entire Draconium Block and 1,000 mB Primal Mana in an EV autoclave. Keep the IV engraver’s Emerald Lens available for Crystal CPU orders.

A second EV hatch can use the existing four-amp CEF. This instance's EnergyContainerList sums voltage times amperage across the hatches. The current hatch accepts two amps at 2,048 V and the controller reads 4,096. Two such hatches should give 8,192. The furnace requires at least ten heat-proof casings. It has twelve, including the proposed second hatch site at (39,83,−12). The planned hot-ingot conduit crosses the roof at (39,84,−10). Run the power down the west face instead. The three sockets at x38, y83, z−10 through −12 are all air. Fit three 4x Aluminium Cables there and turn the converter and both hatches west. The tooltip rates this cable at 2,048 V and four amps with one EU loss per block. Even across the full three blocks this leaves over 8,000 EU/t across the two hatches. Check both connections and the controller readout before loading the silicon.

One hardened capacitor stores ten million EU in a CEF and can discharge at 10,000 EU/t. Adding one to the basic capacitor already installed would bring the local capacity above 12.5 million EU, enough for one boule. This avoids a second converter. Budget one EV hull after the second ore electrolyzer, an aluminium cable for the new hatch, three rated cable segments and the hardened capacitor. Leave the furnace on its existing feed until the parts are together and the furnace is idle.

At 118h15m the fifth furnace had both EV energy hatches and its controller reported 8,192 V. The hardened and basic capacitors together held 12,565,536 EU. The second hatch still needs its west cable connected and a charge verified at the hatch itself before a boule run. The first sixteen coil ingots became 32 single wires, then sixteen double wires. The original cold ingot remains available for the boule.

Both hatches charged on the west branch. An ME order for sixteen cold Naquadah Ingots reached the furnace and finished via the west freezer. Each hot ingot drew 7,680 EU/t for 72 ticks. With the original lot already queued, the empty converter needed a recharge. The soft hammer held the furnace while the capacitor bank charged to 6.74 million EU, then the remaining twelve heats finished. Later orders are limited to the energy in the bank. By 118h40m the full sixteen were cold, the bank had recovered to 12,565,536 EU and seven silicon blocks were in ME. The last two blocks now have priority over another coil heat. Recover the remaining helium for engraved chips and put argon in the hatch.

The nine blocks and one reserved ingot went into the fifth furnace through the new ME pattern with 16,000 mB argon. The controller showed 8,000 EU/t for 1,440 ticks. The boule reached ME and page 796 paid an OmniQuarter. The saw has 20 mB lubricant, below its 240 mB cut. Two Oilsands Dust are available. Electrolyze them and use the free LV mixer tank for an oil and redstone charge, then deliver the lubricant directly to the saw.

The LV electrolyzer made 2,000 mB oil from the two dust. After recovering the oxygen from the mixer, two redstone and the oil supplied 1,500 mB lubricant. The ME interface at (17,83,−9) now has the lubricant cutting pattern. At 119h00m all 64 wafers from the boule were in storage.
