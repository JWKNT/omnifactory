Reserve one cold Naquadah ingot for the wafer line

The Tier Five flight returns 256 Naquadah Dust. The first cold ingot pays a quarter on page 323. Reserve one ingot before drawing the coil wire. A single boule then supplies 64 wafers. Page 796 pays another quarter and opens both System-on-Chip engravings.

JEI calls for nine silicon blocks, one cold Naquadah ingot and 16,000 mB argon at 8,000 EU/t for 1,440 ticks. The pack script sets 5,400 K. The fifth furnace already meets the heat requirement, but its controller currently reports only 4,096 input voltage. Its EV hatch and basic capacitor suffice for the Naquadah metal heat at 1,920 EU/t. Verify the controller reports at least 8,000 input voltage and can sustain 11,520,000 EU before charging the boule. The Assembly Line's separately powered hatches are a working reference.

The silicon blocks need 81 ingots. The Multi Smelter already has the dust-to-ingot pattern. Keep a separate dust allowance for silicone insulation and plates for printed silicon. The factory had 30 silicon dust and 199 buckets of argon at 116h07m. Avoid diverting silicon into storage blocks while the breeder and ship still depend on insulated cables.

Use 240 mB lubricant in the cutter for 400 base ticks at 384 EU/t. Water needs 960 mB and 1,600 ticks. Keep an uncut wafer for comparing later recipes. An Amethyst Lens yields one ASoC Wafer and a Nether Star Lens yields two SoC Wafers at 1,920 EU/t. Each engraving page pays a nickel. The ship's second field generator has first call on the next star.
