Six Basic RF Capacitors

Quest 272 pays a nickel and opens the Basic Capacitor Bank, Double Capacitor and Farming Station branches. The RF capacitor is an Ender IO item. The electronic capacitor for GregTech circuits is a separate part. JEI offers three bench recipes for the RF capacitor. Use one vacuum tube, two bare tin wires and three electrical steel nuggets. The alternative electronics are a GregTech capacitor or an SMD capacitor. This instance lists no RF capacitor quest reward.

The bench made six and quest 272 paid its nickel. Two electrical steel ingots divide into eighteen nuggets at the bench. Twelve tin wires and six vacuum tubes finish the batch. The factory order can share the wiremill and assembler with the polymer smelter and brewery. Four RF capacitors belong to a future basic capacitor bank, the other two can make one Double Capacitor with two energetic alloy ingots and coal dust. A bank also takes four dark steel plates and a Power Buffer. The buffer uses four iron plates, four red alloy plates and one redstone block. The bank is earmarked for the isolated LV blast furnace supply described in blast-feeder.md. The Farming Station also needs a Z-Logic Controller and a Soul Machine Chassis.

The bench used the two spare energetic alloy ingots, two Basic RF Capacitors and one coal dust to make the Double-Layer RF Capacitor. Quest 44 registered and paid its nickel. Four Basic RF Capacitors remain for the bank.
