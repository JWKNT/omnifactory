The motors and circuits inside the Assembly Line

The twelve assembly line casings cost six recipe runs, each with two IV robot arms. The controller takes two more arms and four assembler machine casings. Count those four in addition to the eleven casings the quest wants loose. Five batches of three assembler casings cover all fifteen. They consume five IV motors, five tungstensteel frames and 35 elite circuits. The controller itself needs two further elite circuits, an IV hull and the fourteen arms already counted. Each arm embeds two IV motors, one piston containing a third motor, and a further elite circuit. That brings this section to 47 IV motors and 51 elite circuits. Keep the two tier-six arrays for page 531 separate.

Forty-seven motors use 188 sixteenfold annealed copper windings, equivalent to 1,504 annealed copper ingots in the existing wiremill. The west furnace can anneal copper using its installed oxygen supply. The dedicated Spider loot recipe gives 12 copper ingots per pristine. Keep it on copper while the motor orders consume stock, subject to the polymer and power reserves. Start the west furnace in batches while the steel, hull and quest materials are processing. Do not commit all the copper on hand at once: the cable wrapper also needs it.

JEI accepts Crystal Circuits, upgraded Quantum Processors, Nanoprocessor Arrays and Microprocessor Mainframes as elite circuits. Crystal Circuits yield four per batch, but their 10,000 EU/t recipe exceeds the installed IV assembler's rating. The upgraded Quantum Processor route takes 4,000 EU/t and uses multilayer boards, QBits and fine platinum wire. The Nano Array route uses four Nano Assemblies, RAM and Lumium wire. Keep the existing ME patterns for all three feasible alternatives. Feed native orders in small batches so individual shortages can be diagnosed and don't tie up all the CPUs.

The 24 grates are eight grid crafts. Each uses one MV motor, one steel rotor, one steel frame and six iron bars. The factory's HV assembler can make the rotors from four steel plates, one ring and 32 mB of solder each. The grid uses six iron rods and the wrench for eight iron bars.
