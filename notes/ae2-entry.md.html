The first AE2 storage network

The quest book ties Charging Quartz (0) to Dark Steel and the LV Autoclave. Reserve the first pump for that autoclave. It converts the pack's GregTech Certus Quartz material to the AE2 crystals needed for the charger. The charger accepts RF through an energy conduit. The first fluix combines charged certus, nether quartz and redstone in a single water block.

The Energy Acceptor (282) leads to the terminal and Crystal Growth Chamber on separate branches. Both meet at the ME Chest (63), itself an ingredient for the ME Drive. This offers a way to replace repeated walks to three physical stores. The ME Controller follows the growth chamber, and the quest book says channels are disabled in this instance. Provide space for the power connection, drive and terminals close to the machine row.

JEI calls for five dark steel plates and two certus quartz plates in the charger. First make twelve dark steel ingots for quest 26, then compress five. Steel and one obsidian each work in the alloy smelter, and quest 23 pays a nickel for twelve obsidian once Molds and the LV Fluid Solidifier are done. Dark Steel itself only needs the Steel quest, so the charger can advance first. A 24-block harvest would leave twelve obsidian banked for the later detection. Certus ore can be bought in lots of 32 for two nickels. Each ore smelts into two GregTech gems. Macerate two gems to dust and compress those for the plates; save other gems for the autoclave, which uses 250 mB water per AE2 certus crystal. The charger quest requires both charged and ordinary AE2 crystals at the same time.

The void parts chest already held three dark steel ingots when the autoclave was built. Nine new ingots will reach quest 26. The camp parts chest also holds two Conductive Iron Energy Conduits. Those two conduits would let the east turbine share RF with a charger and the CEF. The proposed turbine position is (7,80,9), touching the existing steam conduit on its west. Face it up into a conduit at (7,81,9), linked west through a second at (6,81,9) to the CEF. Both new cells and the space above the turbine were surveyed clear. The lower turbine would keep its original connection throughout. This layout still needs testing before the network depends on it.

Quest 0 is complete. Twelve ordinary certus crystals came from the autoclave; the charger made five charged on its first run. The charger itself was collected from the boiler hopper's GUI after its quest check, then reinstalled. Four charged certus, four redstone and four nether quartz produced eight fluix in the new factory pool. scripts/fluix_pool.py SETS throws equal batches and collects the result; one set makes two fluix. Pool interior is x11..12/z11..12 at y80. The pool is also renewable bucket water.

Energy Acceptor (282) is complete and its nickel was claimed. The acceptor sits at (8,81,9), on the east face of the new RF branch. Leave the next block east, (9,81,9), for the Crystal Growth Chamber so it can touch the AE2 grid. The acceptor used four fluix plates, four electrical steel plates and one LV hull. The silicon from clay electrolysis supplied the electrical steel. The additional 32-ore iron order is being doubled and smelted for the two machine hulls.
