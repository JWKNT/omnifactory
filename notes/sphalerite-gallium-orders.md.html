Order small gallium dust from sphalerite

One stack of diodes and the wafer line both need gallium. The pilot fed twelve clean sphalerite dust into each of the LV and MV electrolyzers. Eight small gallium dust appeared in ME. Each three dust give one zinc dust, two small zinc dust, one sulfur dust and one small gallium dust. No fluid input is needed. The HV mill and ore centrifuge can prepare more from the mission stock of 121 granite sphalerite ore. Hold nine washed ore for the indium reactor separately.

The existing LV electrolyzer interface at (3,81,6) now contains a processing pattern with three sphalerite dust on the input side and one small gallium dust as the requested output. Using a real gallium sample in the first output slot lets a diode order request it directly. The machine also returns its zinc and sulfur to storage. The spare output slots in the pattern are clear. Check the available clean dust before ordering more, as the MV electrolyzer can also process it.
