20 h 54 min played

The first two titanium ingots

Six rutile dust from the bauxite electrolyzer cover exactly two titanium ingots. Hold the dust until the HV hull unlocks Early Rutile (page 151). Page 152 asks for a bucket of titanium tetrachloride and also needs the HV Chemical Reactor page (153). That reactor takes a hull, two single gold cables, an HV motor, a steel rotor, two glass and two Advanced circuits. Both the Electronic Processor Array and the Refined Processor Array qualify. Reserve the first mainframes for the HV assembler as planned in hv-assembler.md.

Use the reactor once with three rutile dust, two carbon dust and four buckets of chlorine. Live JEI gives one bucket of titanium tetrachloride and two of carbon monoxide in 500 ticks at 480 EU/t. Give both fluids somewhere to go. The first four buckets of chlorine can come from eight rock salt in the LV electrolyzer. The rock salt ore left after PVC is ample.

Budget the Kanthal coil upgrade, an EBF power feed for 480 EU/t and a Vacuum Freezer before repeating the reactor recipe. A bucket of titanium tetrachloride and two magnesium dust give one hot ingot and six magnesium chloride dust in 800 ticks at 480 EU/t. The freezer cools the ingot in 121 ticks at 120 EU/t. Feed the six dust to the LV electrolyzer in two charges of three. This returns the original two magnesium dust and four buckets of chlorine for the next ingot. The recurring inputs are rutile, carbon and power once that loop is running. Plan a short output route from the furnace to the freezer and a separate route for the salt. These are verified recipes; the titanium machines have not been built.
