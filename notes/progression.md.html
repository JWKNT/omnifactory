From the first mine to the Creative Vending Upgrade

Genesis pays four diamond mining hammers, two dimension cakes, two Snad and four sugar cane. Claim it before making mining equipment. Keep a return cake for the void. Start the workshop beside the first productive mine, plant the cane, and reserve the void for factory lines that will outgrow the mining camp.

The first powered bench needs two branches to meet. Iron Supply opens the hand tools and furnace upgrades. Tools lead to the metals, wires, rubber insulation and circuit components. In parallel, the steam dynamo needs water and fluid conduits. Bring the branches together at the LV CEF. Use the first machines to cut the handwork in the next batch of cables and circuits.

Build around repeat orders. Put bulk metals in drawers and leave room to connect them to AE2. Give simulation chambers and their inputs a permanent supply before relying on their outputs. Separate continuously running resource production from machines handling requested crafts.

The Microverse starts with a projector, rocket fuel and quantum flux. Its tenth miner needs neutronium plating, an ingot and a solar panel to reach the Heart of a Universe. Reserve expansion space for power, fusion and parallel processing. The book calls the Creative Vending Upgrade a win and lists Creative ME Cell after it. Check outstanding chapters at that point.
