Black quartz for the Atomic Reconstructor

Quest 74 unlocks after the MV Electrolyzer. It checks for a loose Black Quartz and an Actually Additions Aluminium Casing together. The casing itself uses eight aluminium plates and another Black Quartz. There is one quartz from the Lost Cities library. The nearby machine-block branch on page 75 uses another quartz and eight steel plates.

The MV electrolyzer can turn four quartzite dust into one Crushed Black Quartz in 400 ticks at 90 EU/t. The LV autoclave takes the crushed quartz and 1,000 mB water for 150 ticks at 16 EU/t. Two nickels buy 32 quartzite ore. Smelting one ore gives two quartzite gems for milling. For higher yield, macerate one ore into four crushed quartzite, hammer and wash for four clean dust. The city find covers the first casing, so make the loose gem before committing the eight plates. No quest supplies a spare Black Quartz.

Keep the casing for quest 107. The Atomic Reconstructor costs that casing, five lead plates, two MV emitters and an Endereye Lens. It gives access to the coal and iron crystal recipes on page 112. These remain planned builds.

JEI also lists one quartzite dust as a 14% secondary output when milling certus quartz ore. Twelve certus ore are in the chest. Save any quartzite that appears during AE2 production; four dust are enough for a gem. The dedicated quartzite purchase gives a predictable yield when the reconstructor is due.
