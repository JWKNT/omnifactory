Gold for electrum foil

At 83h10m the board line had eleven silver ingots, one electrum ingot and no gold ingots, dust or ore. Each multilayer board needs 16 electrum foil. The Guardian model can return eight gold per pristine, but none was in storage. The six-gold grid recipe takes iron, glowstone and Hellish Matter. Glowstone is also holding up the wafer line.

Spend two of the 41 OmniQuarters on 32 gold ore. Mill sixteen ore first. Each produces two crushed gold; milling the crushed ore produces impure gold dust, which can smelt directly into ingots. Keep the second half for the next electrum batch. This leaves the rotating chamber free to supply glowstone while the smelter and cluster mill produce foil.

The first sixteen ore produced 32 crushed gold, then 32 impure dust in the HV macerator. The network chose its existing centrifuge pattern before smelting. I observed gold dust and three tiny piles of nickel in one centrifuge cycle. Two impure dust went into the first foil order; the remaining thirty went into a gold ingot order. Sixteen ore remain available for a second batch.
