The first IV assembler and its step-up transformer need at least 41 tungstensteel and 18 plain tungsten before spare parts. The steel bill includes eight hull casing plates, six piston plates, six plates for eight frames, 38 rods and two small gears. The rod total covers motors, pistons, arms and frames. The lathe makes two per ingot. Thirty-five single tungsten cables consume another eighteen ingots. Reserve the two batches separately. A native compressor pattern for tungstensteel plates is visible in ME. The powered lathe now has the rod recipe as well. Page 197 paid its nickel and the first native order delivered two plates. The east furnace actually makes two hot tungstensteel per tungsten plus steel. The 41 steel ingots therefore require 21 of each raw ingot, producing one spare.

Ten IV motors also take 320 annealed copper ingots in sixteenfold wire and ten magnetized neodymium rods. Stock oxygen before annealing. The two master circuits are a separate EV assembly job. Check the circuit alternatives again before committing the frames, and compare the three CPUs’ actual queues.
