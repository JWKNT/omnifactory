Where two hours of factory work went

At 49h23m the book shows 269 of 644 chapter quests complete. Both EV conveyors are in storage. Four motors went into them and two more motors have been made for the arms. Cold titanium now returns through the freezer to ME. The copper mill produced 64 crushed copper from 32 ore, and its nickel byproduct contributes to the stainless run.

The player loaded 140 stacks. The HV reactor alone took 28. Chlorine cost 13 container trips for 108 buckets, titanium tetrachloride another 13 for 23 buckets, and carbon monoxide seven for 44 buckets. All three crafting CPUs were idle in 500 of 661 observations and saturated together just once. There were 42 free item types in the drive. Adding a CPU now would leave the biggest delays in place.

Make chlorine a stocked utility. Both furnace conduits for magnesium chloride are fitted. First prove that six dust arrive automatically after a north furnace charge. Then give that salt a dedicated electrolyzer, return magnesium to the blast supply and pipe chlorine to the HV chemistry line. Leave a clear service aisle and space for a second machine on the new deck. The old LV separator also processes copper byproducts and the MV separator processes bauxite. Sharing either one has already cost changeovers. Keep counting container trips after the connection is live.

The air separator is a different case. Ten buckets of air give the same 3.9 buckets of nitrogen and one of oxygen across the candidate tiers. MV frees the existing LV centrifuge and uses four refined-tier circuits. The HV option takes four advanced circuits and two HV motors. EV would compete directly for the motors reserved for the arms. Build the MV separator in the staged casing. Revisit HV for the autoclave, where the carbon-fiber recipe improves from 85% to certain output. For electronics use the four-per-board Microcircuit route whenever epoxy supply permits.

The side quests on fluid transfer, machine logistics and autocrafting guided the layout. A recurring hand-fed ingredient merits a permanent supply even while a quest machine is still under construction. Sulfur and water are already charged for polyethylene acid. Keep the dehydrator, sheet caster and board blanks supplied while motions, metal batches and plates progress independently.
