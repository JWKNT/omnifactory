Power for the first ZPM assembler

One ZPM arm is in ME. The second arm completes the expensive part of the assembler bill. The machine also needs its own supply for 36,000 EU/t ASoC circuit recipes. JEI shows two ZPM CEFs. Both consume a ZPM hull, four Enderium Energy Conduits and an Ultimate circuit. Their wire requirements are two quadruple or two sixteenfold Enderium wires. Save the existing ZPM hull, its two Naquadah leads and both Crystal Processor Mainframes for the assembler.

The ZPM Voltage Transformer (machine 606) is much cheaper. The grid recipe takes two Power ICs, four single Vanadium-Gallium Cables, one single Naquadah Cable and one LuV Machine Hull. Eight Power ICs were available. Make a third Naquadah lead while the wire wrapper is set up. Let the motors and second arm take their quad vanadium-gallium cables first. The 8,192 V and 32,768 V hatches together supported the first arm. More motion orders still need both hatches, so leave that Assembly Line power arrangement intact.

Build a second LuV CEF beside the RF conduit at (23,85,-20). Its exact bill is four Signalum Energy Conduits, two lengths of 4x Signalum Wire, one LuV hull, a chest and one Master circuit. The first four remaining Energy Flow Circuits belong to the second arm. Make a fifth before building this converter. Candidate positions over the grates are (24,85,-20) for the CEF, (25,85,-20) for the transformer and (26,85,-20) for the assembler. All three were empty. Plan a service walk immediately north at y84. Keep the transformer output directly against the ZPM assembler and verify its single 131,072 V output face before enabling the feed.

The separate CEF can also run the 25,000 EU/t stem-cell reactor at (24,85,-21). Point its LuV output north for an egg run or east for the transformer. Check that the other machine is idle first. This revises the earlier idea of borrowing the working north Assembly Line converter. No power hardware for this extension has been installed yet. The first reinforced glass for the reactor went into the MV alloy smelter while the first arm was running.
