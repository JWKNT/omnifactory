Black steel for the Quantum Compressor

The first projector needs its own Quantum Compressor. Page 216 still depends on Elite Catalyst, and that branch starts at the open Red Coal quest on page 115. Luminessence is already claimed. The Quantum Compressor recipe takes two Elite Catalysts, a ninth Elite Component, a Black Iron Frame, four black steel ingots and one black steel plate. Each catalyst uses four components and a plate. The frame uses four ingots, a plate and four glass.

That totals eight loose black steel ingots and thirteen plates, plus nine each of aluminium plates, enderpearl plates and Luminessence. The mixer produces nine black steel dust per charge from three steel dust, two black bronze dust, two Void Crystals and two Red Coal. Queue three charges for 27 dust. Show the dust on page 116 before blasting it. The west furnace takes 200 ticks per ingot at 120 EU/t. Two five-dust black bronze charges in the mixer take six copper dust and four electrum dust.

The Resonator converts ordinary coal to Red Coal. Page 115 warns that the coal carries its creator's tag. Use a real output sample when encoding its pattern. The installed mod increases the Resonator's GP draw as it progresses toward 1,600 energy. The existing 15 GP Manual Mill is short at the end of the craft. The four Stoneburnt already held can go into a passive Water Mill. JEI charges two redstone gears, two MV pumps and an MV hull. Give the mill four flowing sides and measure at least 16 GP before relying on it for six Red Coal. Water power also removes the cranking from future Stoneburnt orders.

The Water Mill and epoxy bath share one batch of pump hardware. scripts/workshop_water_mill.py uses the existing gear die for two conductive iron gears and the reconstruction bay for Restonia. The eight Restonia plus two gears make two Redstone Gears. The alternative is a Thermal compactor gear recipe using eight Resonating Redstone Crystals. The die and alloy smelter are already installed. Check for the crafted mill, then enclose the water and verify its GP in game before loading the Red Coal order.
