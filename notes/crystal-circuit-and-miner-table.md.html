The 7×7 table and the first Crystal Circuits

The LuV assembler opens page 155 alongside the Crystal CPU and multilayer board quests. Its first recipe makes four elite-grade Crystal Circuits from one Crystal CPU, a Nano CPU, one multilayer board, eight SMD resistors, four SMD transistors, two fine niobium-titanium wires and 72 mB solder. It pays one OmniDollar. The eight-circuit SoC route needs ZPM. One engraved chip and a reusable lime lens in an IV engraver make one Crystal CPU at 7,600 EU/t for 100 ticks. An Emerald Lens works as well as Olivine. Ten emerald plates, one exquisite emerald and 5,000 mB helium make ten engraved chips in the EBF. Page 313 calls for HSS-G coils. Keep the first HSS-G ingots for the LuV motor parts while expanding alloy production.

The exquisite-gem bounty needs the gemstone sensor and tier-three miner quests. The sensor's osmium and iridium branch also passes through the tier-four miner. Both miners use a 7×7 Elite Extended Crafting Table. Start this independent branch in parallel. Page 386 is open and pays one quarter. The table requires four aluminium blocks, eight loose black steel plates, eight Elite Components, four Elite Catalysts and a spare 5×5 table. Including the catalysts, budget 24 components and 36 black steel plates. The components also consume 24 aluminium plates, 24 Enderpearl Plates and 24 Luminessence. Keep four additional Luminessence available for Lumium and use the dedicated acid-fed cell. Cut three compressed pearl blocks for 27 plates. The pack scripts explicitly make one 5×5 table into two at the crafting grid, so the installed table can supply the spare and go back to its working position.
