Batch smelting after the first ore mission

Page 398 describes the Multi Smelter. Basic coils already process 32 items per operation. Rubber and redstone both had large orders on the single network furnace while other crafting CPUs were free. A second CPU cannot speed up that furnace. Keep the single furnace available for small orders and route bulk smelting through a dedicated input and output bus.

The quest asks for fourteen Heat Proof Casings and a controller and pays two quarters. The structure also needs eight coils, energy and item hatches. The controller accepts three advanced circuits, so check available Nanoprocessors before paying for older circuits. Three spare casings and two Invar frames were on hand at the first survey. Compare coil options against the planned third blast furnace before committing nickel. The first miner mission can return nickel alongside the iron and rutile already needed.

On arrival of the nickel mission I priced the shell against today's stock. Three heat-proof casings and two Invar frames are available. Four more casing operations give twelve casings. Combined, fifteen will cover the controller and the fourteen blocks on page 398. The existing recipe gives three casings per six Invar plates and one frame.
