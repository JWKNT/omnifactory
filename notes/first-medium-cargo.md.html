First iridium and osmium flight

The elite table finished the Tier Four miner and page 251 paid an OmniQuarter. The medium projector accepted the ship, 64 Petrotheum Dust, eight Quantum Flux and four Wither Realm Data. Its controller ran at 3,750 EU/t for 800 ticks. The west export bus at (20,80,-28) held exactly 64 + 64 Iridium Ore and 16 Osmium Ore. Use the west aisle at (19,80,-28) to unload.

Each raw ore yields two crushed ore in the HV mill. Grinding crushed iridium can also recover platinum dust; crushed osmium can return iridium dust. Finish the impure piles in the HV centrifuge and freeze the hot ingots after the blast furnace. Start with two ore of each kind. Keep one cold metal of each species for page 253, three more iridium and one more osmium for its alloy, and the remaining osmium for rods. Reserve 24 iridium ingots' worth for the first twelve Tier Five heavy plates. The native mill and centrifuge interfaces both have all their current slots occupied. Fit expansion cards for the repeat cargo recipes; the pilot uses those same machines.

Both stacks of iridium ore and all sixteen osmium ore reached ME. Two iridium ore yielded four crushed ore, four impure piles, four clean iridium dust and twelve tiny osmium piles. Nine tiny piles supplied the first osmium dust. The east furnace ran iridium at 1,920 EU/t for 1,332 ticks per bar. The fourth ran osmium at 1,920 EU/t for 52 ticks per bar while iridium continued. Its interface now has the cold osmium recipe, sharing the freezer. Neither furnace needed a new energy hatch.

The east MV fluid hatch received 1,000 mB of helium. Three cold iridium and one cold osmium then yielded four hot Osmiridium Ingots in 500 ticks. Four cold alloy ingots reached storage and page 253 paid a quarter. The sensor, Conflux Catalyst and Ultimate Extended Crafting Table branches are now reachable through their other prerequisites. Four loose osmium ingots remain; the gem sensor and IV emitter need five rods between them.

The LV lathe turned three osmium ingots into six rods. The IV assembler used one rod, one tungstensteel plate, one Quantum Processor and one Eye of Ender to make the first IV sensor. The shared titanium reactor had received both a dense iron block and three rutile dust. Removing the single dense ore opened a slot for the titanium job. The cold metal and plate reached their waiting order, and the dense ore went back in for nitric acid. The EV assembler then used the plate, one platinum rod, one ender pearl and a circuit for its sensor at 1,920 EU/t. The east furnace now has the combined iridium heat and freezer pattern. The optical assembly still needs three Pulsating Iron Ingots.
