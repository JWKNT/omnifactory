Tier Two Micro Miner and its 5×5 table

Quest 10 requires page 245 for Titanium Heavy Plating and page 209 for the Advanced Extended Crafting Table. The ship needs six heavy plates, two Basic Mining Lasers, two LV Field Generators, one Basic Micro Miner Guidance System, one Stainless Steel Chest, one Micro Miner Engine Core, one Advanced Diesel Generator and three Electrical Steel Thrusters. Its reward is one OmniNickel. The book recommends stocking components in AE2 and filling the extended table with JEI until the later extended-crafting automation unlocks.

Black Steel and Luminessence are already complete. Pages 206 and 207 each pay an OmniNickel. Page 209 pays two OmniQuarters. Start the table alongside the HV compressor. The table assembly needs eight Advanced Components in total (four become one Advanced Catalyst), eight Basic Components (four per Basic Catalyst), three further black steel plates across the three catalysts, and two Compressed Crafting Tables. That is 19 black steel plates and 16 Luminessence from an empty bench. The advanced set adds eight electrum plates and eight glowstone plates. Two glowstone blocks in the cutter yield all eight glowstone plates. The basic set adds eight nether quartz plates and eight iron plates. Use the second table recipe in JEI; the first just changes the existing table's appearance.

scripts/advanced_crafting_table.py now stages the table ingredients during projector rounds. The first two glowstone blocks were crafted from eight of the 103 dust in ME. The MV cutter can turn both into eight plates. It shares time with industrial plate cutting. The alloy, Luminessence and compressor work can run alongside it.

The two HV pistons were assembled together from two HV motors, four gold cables, six stainless plates, four stainless rods and two small gears. The workshop also produced an HV machine casing from eight more stainless plates. The next gold cables are split between the hull assembler and the compressor itself. Two Advanced Components were made for the 5×5 table. Page 206 paid one OmniNickel in the quest book. Eight glowstone plates from the two blocks passed through the MV cutter.

The alloy-smelter recipe for the Micro Miner Engine Core needs 480 EU/t. The existing MV alloy smelter cannot run that recipe. The proposed HV bay at (12,83,0) can share the 512 V line overhead. Allow four 4x Kanthal wires, two gold cables, two Nanoprocessors and an HV hull. The engine frame uses four electrum rods and four electrum plates. The LV sensor assembler recipe consumes one steel plate where the crafting table consumes four; it also takes a brass rod, a circuitBasic and quartzite. The three Electrical Steel Thrusters each incorporate a lower-tier Conductive Iron Thruster. Starting the table alone will not supply the ship.

The ship bench has produced its first LV field generator. The titanium line has recovered enough for ten more plates in ME. Each projector round now checks the HV alloy-smelter order in scripts/engine_core_bay.py. The washer had 48 purified ruby left from earlier chrome production. The order can grind 18 of those, centrifuge the dust and electrolyze it for three chrome alongside the table work. The HV smelter and engine core still need physical delivery and a working test. The ship planner also checks active CPU outputs before assigning the same part to the bench.
