Tier Two Micro Miner and its 5×5 table

Quest 10 requires page 245 for Titanium Heavy Plating and page 209 for the Advanced Extended Crafting Table. The ship needs six heavy plates, two Basic Mining Lasers, two LV Field Generators, one Basic Micro Miner Guidance System, one Stainless Steel Chest, one Micro Miner Engine Core, one Advanced Diesel Generator and three Electrical Steel Thrusters. Its reward is one OmniNickel. The book recommends stocking components in AE2 and filling the extended table with JEI until the later extended-crafting automation unlocks.

Black Steel and Luminessence are already complete. Pages 206 and 207 each pay an OmniNickel. Page 209 pays two OmniQuarters. Start the table alongside the HV compressor. The table assembly needs eight Advanced Components in total (four become one Advanced Catalyst), eight Basic Components (four per Basic Catalyst), three further black steel plates across the three catalysts, and two Compressed Crafting Tables. That is 19 black steel plates and 16 Luminessence from an empty bench. The advanced set adds eight electrum plates and eight glowstone plates.
