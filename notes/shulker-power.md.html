Steam supply for the first Shulker model

The first boiler and its two basic turbines have a combined nameplate of 320 RF/t. Only the east turbine currently points into the RF conduit. The other one points directly into the CEF. The CEF's stored EU has been useful for metalworking; count RF at the simulation chamber separately. Its internal buffer holds 2,000,000 RF, so a charged chamber can run a batch above the conduit’s sustained input rate.

Three Hardened Upgrade Kits would raise the entire trio to 150% of base, or 480 RF/t before workshop loads. Each costs four Invar Gears, four Invar Plates and a block of Electrical Steel. Quest 528 gives the output multiplier. Even all three upgrades together fall short of continuous Shulker simulations at 512 RF/t. Reinforced kits raise the trio to 250%, or 800 RF/t, and each needs four electrum gears, four electrum plates and one aluminium block. Quest 609 opens after the first hardened kit; aluminium is already claimed. Before spending on six kits, compare a second basic steam trio and fuel supply, and try a fully charged chamber with intermittent Shulker runs. Measure RF reaching the chamber rather than assuming that upgrading both turbines feeds the same branch.

The installed conductive iron conduit reports 512 RF/t. That matches one Shulker simulation, while the fabricator asks for 1,000 RF/t. Reserve one surplus Energetic Alloy ingot from the sixteen-ingot dynamo batch to upgrade the branch. The wiremill turns one ingot into the two wires required. The LV assembler combines those wires, six Conduit Binder and one existing Conductive Iron Energy Conduit into four Energetic Alloy Energy Conduits at 16 EU/t for 80 ticks. The bench uses the same materials for three. Quest 41 is open. The workshop planner now knows the assembler route. Inspect the conduit after crafting and measure delivered RF under load.

The installed Numismatic fuel registry lists 1,200,000 base RF per diamond. In this version Lapidary Calibration adds 25 percentage points to fuel energy and four times the dynamo base rate to maximum generation. With the plain basic dynamo this predicts 1,500,000 RF per diamond and 2,500 RF/t at full load. The Shulker simulation takes about 300 ticks at 512 RF/t, or about 153,600 RF. The fabricator spends 50 powered ticks at 1,000 RF/t to turn one pristine into six diamonds. At the starting 5% pristine chance, twenty simulations average six diamonds for roughly 3.12 million RF including one extraction. Actual drops vary; retain fuel until the model levels up. The chamber needs more than one full simulation's energy in its buffer before it starts.

Four Energetic Alloy Energy Conduits were made in one LV assembler batch and quest 41 paid its nickel. The three existing conductive iron conduits are at (6,81,9), (7,81,9) and (7,81,8).
