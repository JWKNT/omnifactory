Recovering carbon from titanium production

The HV reaction that makes titanium tetrachloride left two buckets of carbon monoxide. The LV electrolyzer processed both buckets. Its oxygen output rose from 2,000 to 4,000 mB, and the two reactions returned two carbon dust. The second recovery job will be magnesium chloride from the north blast furnace. Six dust should return the two magnesium and four buckets of chlorine used for one titanium ingot. Verify that half of the loop once the furnace can run it.
