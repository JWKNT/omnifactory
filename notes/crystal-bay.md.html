The reconstruction bay

The east face of the RF conduit at (8,82,5) has room for a horizontal Atomic Reconstructor. The survey found the machine position (9,82,5), the beam path and the target at (11,82,5) empty. The RF conduit carries Energetic Alloy. Put a cobblestone backstop at (12,82,5), remove its temporary supports and keep the floor aisle clear. Place the machine from the east and verify its facing. A lever on its south side can disable the idle beam. One empty block between the nozzle and target keeps the redstone block used for Restonia away from the machine. This bay is queued after page 107.

Page 112 asks for one Void Crystal and a whole Enori Crystal Block. It pays a nickel and opens Black Steel and Meteorite Hunter. The beam converts a block of iron directly to the Enori block. Convert a block of coal to a Void Crystal Block and unpack its nine crystals. This keeps the inputs and products on the backstop until collected. The JEI item route can also convert individual coal. A redstone block becomes a Restonia block, which unpacks to nine Restonia Crystals. Reserve four for the Redstone Gear and five for the resonating crystals used in the Manual Mill and Resonator.
