Automatic polymer delivery to the Shulker

The wiremill's ME Interface at (7,83,6) stocks one Pulsating Polymer Clay in its first configuration slot. Its south neighbor, the conduit at (7,83,7), extracts on brown when powered. That port continues to insert diamonds into ME on purple. A west-facing ME Level Emitter at (8,83,7) watches polymer in network storage. Its filter is one polymer, its threshold is 33, and its redstone mode is HIGH_SIGNAL. This gives the rotating chamber access to the reserve. For maintenance, the emitter tip is visible from above the wiremill interface. scripts/polymer_delivery.py uses the quest run's real emitter, interface and conduit screens.

The chamber takes polymer only from the top. The original side port at (11,83,8) still extracts ordinary and pristine matter. Two spare Item Conduits join the top of that bundle to the chamber lid at (11,84,7), whose downward port inserts on brown. Blocks at (13,80,5) and (13,81,5) form a step alongside the existing storage chests. They lead to the service ledge at (12,83,5), within reach of the chamber lid.

Including the filter sample, the terminal accepted 21 and storage rose from 17 to 38. Direct fuel delivery starts working when that stock crosses the threshold. The dry electronics assembler is the next construction order.

Compare polymer loads and dynamo fuel at the next review before deciding whether to dedicate a second emitter.
