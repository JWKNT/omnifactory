Supplying the first simulation chamber

The workshop made eight Energetic Blend and loaded eight gold alongside it in the blast furnace. Three Energetic Alloy ingots came out on the first collection and quest 34 paid a nickel. Two of those ingots went back into the input bus with two ender pearls. scripts/blast_job.py now finds free bus slots, so the second order fits while the first is running. Four of the six remaining energetic ingots are reserved for the loot fabricator.

Four rubber saplings and a single wooden sword made four plant fiber at the bench. Those made six string. Together with the city's twelve string there was enough to alloy sixteen string and four mortar-ground coal dust into four carbon meshes. Combine the first mesh with four Pulsating Dust and keep a carbon mesh in inventory when checking quest 684. Then convert the remaining three for the simulation chamber.

Sugar cane burns for 300 ticks in an ordinary furnace. Twelve spare cane from the stock chest powered the west and north furnaces through sixteen uraninite ores. Each gave a dust; a second firing makes Pulsating Dust. scripts/smelt_batch.py --cane measures cane for each furnace independently. The farm's boiler hopper was left to feed the turbines. Use the spare cane in the stock chest, or recover a few loose stalks from around the farm.

All four pulsating meshes are finished and stored in factory parts alongside six Energetic Alloy ingots. Quest 684 paid a nickel, unlocking the Simulation Chamber. The EBF produced its first Vibrant Alloy ingot and quest 35 paid another nickel, unlocking Numismatic Power. The second ingot was in progress at collection. Twenty-eight copper ore are in the macerator for 56 crushed ore. A two-nickel order bought 32 iron ore and a one-nickel order bought 32 coal ore. Sixteen coal ore began smelting in the east and west furnaces to bootstrap the next fuel supply.

The second Vibrant Alloy ingot was collected. Twenty-four of the new iron ore were smelted directly, eight remain for doubling. Sixteen of the iron ingots went into the west furnace for wrought iron. The first thirty copper dust from the macerator and hammer went into east and north. The remaining crushed copper can be collected with scripts/finish_iron.py copper; the helper now replaces a hammer if it breaks mid-batch.

For fresh sand during an ore order, the bench accepts cobble and an iron mortar for gravel (recipe index 1) and gravel and the same mortar for sand (index 1). The iron mortar lasted 52 crafts. This batch made 32 gravel, of which 27 went through the second pass. Use an ordinary furnace to keep spare stone for replacement mortars. The lake floor near the old sand patch is now deep enough that the bench is faster. Three sugar cane make three paper directly; the boiler showed no spare stack on inspection, so use grove logs for this circuit run.

The factory finished 32 paper, 16 charcoal and 16 glass. Another 32 iron ore were purchased for two nickels. East and north smelted them, with a 16-ore coal batch across west and ore to replenish fuel. The chamber order began with 14 wrought iron plates, seven charcoal dust and five red alloy ingots. The order planner now counts the fuel-backed portion of each furnace batch and can dispatch smelting jobs into empty furnaces. It can also make jungle slabs from rubber logs. Use scripts/factory_round.py --continuous --work-ahead deepmoblearning:simulation_chamber to continue from current stock.

All eight dark steel plates went into the dark steel hull. The chamber and its on-screen quest are complete. The chamber occupies the former temporary cobblestone charger anchor at (6,82,9). The RF conduit directly underneath reports connections to the chamber and the CEF. The chamber buffer reached 47,272 of 2,000,000 RF during testing. Both turbines briefly had no steam during commissioning, then the boiler resumed with a few charcoal. scripts/cef_battery.py --take was used to test RF delivery; the capacitor is back in the CEF with over two million EU stored. The first model and 16 polymer clay are in production. Ten uraninite ore went into the furnace bank; thirteen spare terracotta went into the macerator. Six more saplings produced nine string. See polymer-clay.md for the renewable MV route.
