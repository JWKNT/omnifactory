Filtered transfer from the blast furnace to the freezer

Quest 156 recommends sending hot metal directly between the buses. The blast output at (0,80,8) now faces down into its solid foundation. Its west side has an installed GregTech Ore Dictionary Filter. The screwdriver GUI set it to extraction, whitelist, ingotHot*. All three settings were read back from the installed cover. The installed GregTech version rejects covers on the front of a bus. Turning its front down made the west side available. The planned north blast bus will face south so its top remains available for the second filter.

Its east-facing freezer input should pull all nine. Each takes 112 ticks at 120 EU/t. Count nine cold ingots at the west output, then claim 156 on its page.

For fresh metal, one Ender IO item conduit at (-1,80,8) can extract from the filtered west side of the blast bus and insert into the freezer chest. The old double-chest proposal depended on the bus pushing from a filtered front, which cannot be built. The installer also opens the cobblestone below the south stairs for cold product access. The conduit and its first three-ingot acceptance test are still ahead. Book the bulk Kanthal only after all three reach the freezer and cool.
