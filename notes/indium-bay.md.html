Preparing indium alongside the second miner

Page 263 needs nine tiny piles of indium and pays one OmniNickel. The page links the first miner's galena to the second miner's sphalerite. Storage already had 156 galena ore and two sphalerite ore before the second launch. Five galena have returned ten crushed ore through the HV grinder. Each sphalerite ore gives four crushed. The MV washer gives three tiny sulfur from each galena and three tiny yellow garnet from each sphalerite, alongside the purified ore. scripts/indium_ore_stock.py starts that work with the ore on hand. The initial target is nine purified of each, using the permanent washer return.

The first nine pairs need 36,000 mB sulfuric acid in a chemical reactor at 150 EU/t. The second reaction takes the concentrate and 36 aluminium dust at 600 EU/t. The refinery's HV titanium reactor accepts 512 V and is already busy. The next grade is an EV reactor; its recipe uses an EV hull, an EV motor, two aluminium cables, two extreme-grade circuits, two glass and a stainless rotor. The four-plate assembler rotor recipe saves a screw and the forming tools. The extruder gives four rings per stainless ingot. Use Nano Processor Assemblies for the circuit slots. After page 263, page 265 describes the indium-saving QBit wafer route. Keep the Lead-Zinc Solution for centrifuging.

The assembly transformer accepts HV only on its west face and outputs EV only to the east, directly into the assembler. At the survey there was air below the RF trunk at (19,82,-8), and north of that at (19,82,-9) and (19,82,-10). That is the planned four-amp EV converter, fourfold aluminium cable and reactor respectively. The four-amp converter requires four End Steel Energy Conduits, two fourfold End Steel wires, an EV hull, an extreme circuit and a chest. The 16-amp version substitutes two sixteenfold wires. For the four-amp version, five of the 44 stored End Steel ingots yield the ten single wires needed across converter and conduit batch. The assembler makes four conduits from six binder, two wires and one vibrant conduit. The grid gives three. The HV converter plus transformer alternative gives one EV amp and needs two HV hulls. Use the separate four-amp feed to leave the existing EV assembler running during indium production and give further machines room on the same cable. scripts/indium_power.py and scripts/indium_reactor.py stage the hardware. Confirm voltage, loaded power and actual indium delivery after construction.
