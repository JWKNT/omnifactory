The workshop already owns Slime, Zombie, Skeleton and Spider models. Quest 686 pays ten quarters for those four together with Creeper, Witch, Guardian and Thermal Elemental. Guardian's prismarine shard can come from nether quartz and one extra matter. Witch needs a glass bottle. Use the ordinary matter from the queued Zombie and Spider simulation batches for these models.

A macerator gives four crushed ore per block if throughput becomes more important than startup speed. Keep the blue lens in the engraver kit. Use one bucket each of hydrogen and fluorine in the reactor, then the resulting acid and one Pulsating Dust in an MV reactor. The bucket of Elemental Reduction Fluid completes quest 309.
