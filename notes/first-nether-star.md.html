Budget for the first crafted Nether Star

Page 391 requires the four individual tips, the core and the finished star. Hold the five parts before assembling the star so the book can record each one. Each tip takes five Nether Quartz and two dust of one Thermal element. The core takes four more quartz, one rod each from a Blaze, Blizz, Blitz and Basalz, and a Block of Luminessence. The full bill is 24 quartz, eight elemental dust, four rods and nine Luminessence. Twelve of the sixteen Nether Quartz Ore in the quarry chest can give all 24 quartz in ordinary furnaces. The Thermal Elemental model already supplied Blizz Rods. Save one rod of each kind before grinding any more into dust.

The first Luminessence order makes four. Two more batches make eight, bringing the stock to twelve. For those extra batches reserve two Energetic Blend, two Aluminium Dust and 4,000 mB phosphoric acid after meeting the HV hardware order. Alternatively, processing nine Apatite Dust in the reactor with 5,000 mB sulfuric acid and 10,000 mB water yields 3,000 mB phosphoric acid, 1,000 mB hydrochloric acid and forty Gypsum Dust. Two such charges produce a surplus and help feed vinyl chloride. Leave room for both gases or liquids and empty the gypsum between charges.

The star pays an OmniNickel on page 391 and opens the Crystal Matrix Heavy Plating branch. A Wither model consumes a star, a blank model and seven Extraterrestrial Matter. This instance's Loot Fabricator offers Wither Bone, Drop of Evil and Wither Realm data from Wither pristine. Use the crafting line when planning subsequent stars. No star parts have been assembled yet.

At 78h20m all four star tips were in storage. The core was short only its Block of Luminessence. The factory held three Luminessence, two Energetic Blend and fifteen aluminium dust. The LuV casing also needs two Luminessence for the next four Lumium ingots. Make two more four-piece batches to reach eleven pieces across both jobs. They take four buckets of phosphoric acid. The starter reactor already has 64 buckets of water and six of sulfuric acid. Two nine-dust apatite charges can make the six buckets of phosphoric acid for those batches, leaving two buckets for the next job.

The Tier Seven mainframe needs an Improved Nether Star. The HV Chemical Bath takes one ordinary star and 2,000 mB radon at 384 EU/t for 96 seconds. The alternative in JEI is a Tier Eight Microverse mission costing Quantum Flux, Dilithium Crystals and Wither Realm Data. Use the bath at (10,84,-2) after collecting page 391's reward. ME held 1,850 mB radon and 124 Radium Salt when I checked. Four salts in the EV electrolyzer cover the shortfall and leave some radon for Improved Ender Eyes.
