Next use of MV power: wafers and presses

The first Silicon Boule is already in digital storage. This instance's cutting machine yields sixteen wafers per boule using 5 mB water, 3 mB distilled water, or 1 mB lubricant. The first MV Cutting Machine (quest 79) and MV Precision Laser Engraver (83) each pay a nickel. The cutter opens Silicon Wafers (81). The engraver opens Lenses (88). Those branches meet at Circuit Wafers (84) and then the first tier-three circuits (89). The engraver also offers a production route for Inscription Plates (103), which unlock the Inscriber quest (51).

The engraver order needs two MV pistons, one MV emitter, three Primitive Processors, one MV hull and two copper cables. The emitter itself needs two more Primitive Processors, four electrum rods, two copper cables and a nether quartz. Make electrum two ingots at a time in the alloy smelter using one silver and one gold ingot. The planner now covers the engraver and its components. Sixteen of the seventeen banked aluminium dust are in the EBF; leave the last dust available for cobalt brass. The next copper ore purchase, tree harvest and paper batch are for this stage.

The cutter is a second MV hull, an MV conveyor, MV motor, Diamond Sawblade, two Primitive Processors, two copper cables and glass. The conveyor takes two more MV motors. The sawblade needs a cobalt brass gear and four small piles of diamond dust. The ordinary crafting route for nine cobalt brass dust takes seven brass dust, one aluminium dust and one cobalt dust. Four zinc dust are in the workshop. Pin down the cobalt source before spending on the rest of the cutter.

For the second MV machine, the installed feeder can face north toward a cable at (7,81,1) and a second segment at (6,81,1) can feed the reactor's north face. The surveyed 4x Copper Cable is rated 128 V at four amps and loses 2 EU per block. The 4x Energetic Alloy Cable has the same ratings and zero loss. Add the insulated bus once its next load is ready; leave the first reactor directly powered for commissioning.

The live furnace registry accepts Cobaltite Ore and produces one Cobalt Ingot. The macerator turns that ingot into one Cobalt Dust. Of the two raw ore types checked, only cobaltite has a coin recipe: six nickels give 32 ore. At the mixer three copper dust and one zinc dust give four brass dust. Two mixer batches therefore cover one nine-dust cobalt brass batch. Save at least one aluminium dust for this mixture.
