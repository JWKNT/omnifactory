Questions for each two-hour review

Use played time. Start with scripts/strategy_review.py --evidence and the last review. Record one concrete decision and the observation that would show whether it worked. Advance the review clock with --reviewed after publishing the decision.

  • Which upcoming machine consumes the scarce part? Look at its later uses and the quest reward. Compare all JEI recipes, including each equivalent circuit in the ingredient slot. Price a realistic batch at installed voltage, with current boards, solder, by-products and reserved stock. scripts/circuit_choices.py circuitGood lists the registered circuits, the output per recipe, voltage, fluids, stock and direct quest rewards. A displayed Primitive Processor is only one of four ingredients accepted by an MV robot arm. An exact processor component can have different rules.
  • What work did I repeat since the last review? Count hand loads, trips and completed ME jobs. Check what really ran out while the player was elsewhere. Prefer removing that trip with an in-world connection or pattern. Verify a second unattended delivery. Size the next build for the actual demand several chapters ahead, leave space to expand, batch components and give slow machines another job to run alongside.
  • What am I still doing with ordinary chests, furnaces, hand tools, a personal grid or walking? Choose the most frequent example and compare the modded options already unlocked. Include the cost to build and feed them and the trips they actually save. Record when a vanilla option is still economical. Revisit that decision as volume or equipment changes.
