Recovering the west dynamo’s output

At 128h09m the two diamond dynamos both reported 8,191 RF/t. The west unit is rated for 15,000 RF/t and the east for 8,750. Each coil touches a Vibrant Alloy Energy Conduit. In this instance each connection carries 8,192 RF/t. Both queues still held more than thirty diamonds. The higher west rating can supply up to another 6,808 RF/t if the network can accept it.

The west dynamo at (7,80,8) faces up. Its bundle at (7,81,8) contains exactly one Vibrant power conduit. There are two other Vibrant conduits touching that position, to the east and south. The extraction chamber above it also needs power. A Power Buffer in the dynamo’s present output position could take power below and deliver it east, south and up. That preserves the diamond feed and the standing room at (7,80,7), which several machines share. The east dynamo can continue feeding the main grid during commissioning.

A Vibrant Capacitor Bank provides 25,000 RF/t but needs two Advanced banks, six Vibrant plates and a Vibrant Crystal. Thermal Expansion’s cell line also starts with a Vibrant bank. Mixed-tier Ender IO cable connections are disabled here.
