Use stored light fuel for board resin

The crude tower restarted at 89h44m after the first 16 buckets of sulfuric heavy fuel went into ME. Its crude recipe yields only 1,200 mB sulfuric naphtha per 1,200 ticks. ME already holds 808 buckets of clean light fuel. Live JEI shows that the chemical reactor can turn one bucket of light fuel plus two of hydrogen into one of hydro-cracked light fuel. The distillery on program zero turns that into 800 mB of clean naphtha. The existing naphtha resin recipe needs three buckets per two reinforced sheets. A 16-bucket pilot should therefore yield 12,800 mB clean naphtha, enough for eight sheets plus a remainder.

Use the free third input on the hydrogen-fed MV refinery gas reactor. Leave its export filter in place. Run the distillation pilot on the MV propene still when its input and output are empty, bank the clean naphtha, then restore program seven. The resin reactor already has both other reagents. Compare the pilot with crude production before deciding on a second continuous distillery.

The full 16-bucket portable tank stayed unchanged when clicked into the gas desulfurizer with hydrogen and residual sulfuric gas present. Keep that reactor on acid service. The east methanol reactor at (15,82,2) has a full hydrogen supply and two empty fluid tanks, so the pilot moved there.
