104 h 27 min played

Twenty seeds for a bucket of Seed Oil

The seven plots produced more than 64 canola. Page 319 paid a quarter at 104h25m. Leave at least seven loose seeds to replant the bed. Page 807 asks for one bucket of Seed Oil and opens the same choice quest as the Canola Press.

One ordinary canola seed gives 10 mB in a GregTech fluid extractor. A pass through the existing Atomic Reconstructor turns it into a Crystallized Canola Seed worth 50 mB. Twenty treated seeds cover the bucket, leaving the canola itself available for a future press. Empowered seeds yield 250 mB each, but would need the Empowerer and four more ordinary seeds per seed. Use the existing beam shelf to convert one batch. Hold the starter extractor's fluid outlet while the seed oil accumulates, take a bucket for the quest book, then restore the previous outlet setting.
