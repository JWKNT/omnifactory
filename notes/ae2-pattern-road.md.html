From the first cell to a craftable pattern

The existing ME Chest and terminal can store workshop parts. Reaching the Pattern Terminal (106) also takes both an Engineering Processor (100) and Autocrafting Patterns (104). Work on the processor and plastics branches together.

On the electronics side, the Inscriber (51) follows the four Inscription Plates, MV hull and Energy Acceptor. Keep a diamond plate, gold plate and silicon plates available for the first Engineering and Logic Processors. The Logic Processor opens ME Interfaces (109). Crafting Units (110) needs the Logic and Calculation Processors plus Grains of Infinity. That page checks for both a Molecular Assembler and a Crafting Unit. Quests 100, 102, 104, 106, 109 and 110 each pay a nickel.

On the plastics side, the book accepts sulfur from several sources. Electrolyzing sphalerite can also advance gallium for wafers. Sulfuric Acid (94) and Ethanol (505) open Ethylene and Polyethylene (95). Biomass for ethanol can use surplus grove saplings in a brewery, leaving the Snad cane feeding its boiler. Check the live brewery and distillation recipes before sizing the line. The Fluid Solidifier and plate mold then turn polyethylene into sheets for quest 96, which pays a quarter. These sheets gate patterns and also serve the later HV machine hull. Reserve room for continuous fluid processing separate from the clathrate reactor.

Working backward from the first crafting CPU gives a better opening Inscriber order. Use three Calculation Processors for the Crafting Terminal and the two slots in the Crafting Unit. Three Logic Processors cover the unit and the two core recipes. One certus crystal or one nether quartz, each with fluix dust and a Logic Processor, gives two Formation or two Annihilation Cores respectively. One pair goes into the ME Interface and the other into the Molecular Assembler. Make two Engineering Processors, one reserved for the Pattern Terminal. The revised eight-processor batch therefore needs eight Basic Circuits, eight silicon plates, three certus plates, three gold plates and two diamond plates.

The ME Interface requires five aluminium plates, an MV robot arm, another MV hull and the two cores. The Molecular Assembler requires four aluminium plates, two quartz glass, two cores and an Ender IO Crafter. The Crafter needs an Industrial Machine Chassis, two Infinity Bimetal Gears, three Item Conduits, two Resonating Redstone Crystals and a Compressed Crafting Table. Build the Crafting Unit and Molecular Assembler before converting the unit to 1k Crafting Storage. Autocrafting Patterns (104) requires the ME Interface, the Crafting Units page and Plastic Sheets. Five sheets, three fine silver wires and a Processor Assembly give eight blank patterns.

The live recipe calculation for the ME Interface, Molecular Assembler and Crafting Unit found the workshop short three Grains of Infinity, two Fluix Crystals and 24 aluminium ingots at the start of the processor order. Seven aluminium ingots were in storage. The Drive will also use aluminium, so the workshop has queued a fresh 32-ingot blast charge. Twelve terracotta are already in the macerator for that charge. The CPU order uses both pairs of cores, two Infinity Bimetal Gears and an Industrial Machine Chassis. Seven grains cover the gears and chassis together. Use the existing bedrock gallery and water pool to refill grains and fluix while circuit parts process.

After the first three advanced circuits and plastic sheets, scripts/pattern_hardware.py orders the Interface for quest 109, then presents the Molecular Assembler and Crafting Unit together for quest 110. Only then does it convert the unit to 1k Crafting Storage. Five sheets and one of the reserved Electronic Processor Arrays make eight Blank Patterns. Save seven and use one with a new ME Terminal and Engineering Processor for the Pattern Terminal. These are queued builds. The first operational autocraft will still need an attached interface, assembler, CPU and encoded pattern.

The site for the first CPU is immediately above the ME cable. The three blocks at y82, z9, x9 through x11 were air when surveyed. From west to east they will be the 1k Crafting Storage, Interface and Molecular Assembler. The existing ME Terminal faces north; the Pattern Terminal can use the south side of the same cable, reached from the pool rim. This instance disables AE2 channels. scripts/first_autocraft.py is queued to install the hardware, encode glass panes and order sixteen. The proof will be a sixteen-pane increase on the terminal after the crafting CPU accepts the request. The panes feed the vacuum-tube line.

The bedrock run supplied thirteen more grains, bringing the bank to seventeen. Three matched sets of nether quartz, redstone and charged certus produced six new Fluix Crystals in the workshop pool. The charger ejects east; if a crystal lands high on the new MV conduit, jump west from (9,80,8) to collect it. This worked for the reserve batch. The two remaining advanced circuits after patterns are earmarked for quest 90 and the assembler above the existing north cable at (7,82,1). A step at (8,80,-2) gives access to the upper deck. The east socket at (8,81,1) is reserved for the ethylene reactor.

The first three planned interface patterns cover panes, tubes and Basic Circuits.
