Lumium is on the critical path for the IV assembler. Each Nano Array uses four single wires and each Nano Mainframe eight. The four arrays and two mainframes alone need 32 wires, or 16 Lumium ingots. The first Lumium quest pays a quarter. I checked every item reward in the installed quest database for the ingot, blend, wire, tungstensteel coils, finished mainframe and IV assembler. None pays one of those items.

The installed recipe takes one Lumium Blend and 250 mB Primal Mana over 14,400 base ticks at 120 EU/t and 4,500 K. Sixteen ingots need four buckets of mana. Page 235 points to fluid extraction of Mana Dust and pays a quarter. A batch of four blend takes four Tin Alloy Dust, two Sterling Silver Dust, two Luminessence and one Energized Clathrate. The grid and mixer have the same output. Quartz and 288 mB molten glowstone make the clathrate. The earlier projector build already made Luminessence from phosphoric acid. Verify leftovers before restarting those lines.

The mission cargo alone will go much farther through the HV macerator. One ore makes four crushed. Each crushed makes one impure pile and each pile makes one clean tungstate in the centrifuge. Both grinding steps can produce manganese, and the centrifuge gives lithium by-product. The furnace shortcut only yields two. The native tungsten electrolysis tender now orders against finished dust actually in storage. It no longer counts unground ore as immediate furnace feed. The first 16 ore produced 64 crushed. After that batch the network still held 134 Tungstate Ore and 192 Scheelite Ore from the same mission. The scheelite can cover the remaining tungsten bill without another launch. Use tungstate first for the shorter electrolysis cycle. The four buckets of Primal Mana will take sixteen Mana Dust in the fluid extractor. A single pristine Thermal Elemental payout can supply 64 snowballs for the cryotheum component.
