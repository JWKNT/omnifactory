Solder for the three processor arrays

Live JEI lists both molten tin and soldering alloy for all three LV assembly stages. One circuit assembly takes 144 mB tin or 72 mB solder. The first three arrays require 51 operations. With solder, 26 ingots cover the 3,672 mB bill. The workshop will make three alloy batches, yielding 30 ingots from 27 tin and three antimony. This saves 24 tin versus the original 51-ingot plan and leaves solder for later circuits.

Four nickels buy 32 stibnite ore. Three raw ore fired in ordinary furnaces give the three antimony ingots. A 16 EU/t alloy smelter accepts the two metals directly. The longer ore-doubling and separation routes are available when antimony throughput matters. The local stocks had no stibnite or antimony on survey; the oven quest supplied a quarter toward this purchase.

After quest 82 the workshop can put the solder order through its existing machines. The new fluid extractor under the LV assembler will pour upward. Draw the first bucket from the assembler for quest 498, collect the nickel, then put the same fluid back. The quest opens the antimony bounty. The future circuits and first solder bucket are still pending.
