Budget the first oil fractions together

JEI gives all four hydrodesulfurization recipes 160 ticks at 30 EU/t. A single batch takes two buckets of hydrogen and yields one bucket of hydrogen sulfide. It also returns the desulfurized fraction at its input volume. The inputs per batch are eight buckets of sulfuric heavy fuel, twelve of sulfuric light fuel, twelve of sulfuric naphtha or sixteen of sulfuric gas.

Ten tower charges made from thirty buckets of ordinary oil should fund one batch of every recipe. The naphtha has no spare volume, so retain the bucket used for quest 160 or replenish its oil before committing twelve buckets. The water-fed chemical reactor recently held 59 buckets of hydrogen from sulfuric acid production. Using eight buckets of this hydrogen will serve all four fractions and give acid production headroom.

Page 166 opens Allyl Chloride. Pages 164 and 165 together open Diesel. Page 167 checks hydrogen sulfide, the byproduct of all four conversions. Give naphtha priority for the epoxy line. The reactor itself needs eight seconds per batch at LV, while the tower needs ten minutes to produce twelve buckets of sulfuric naphtha. For this line, continuous oil and hydrogen delivery and separate product outlets are the larger gains.
