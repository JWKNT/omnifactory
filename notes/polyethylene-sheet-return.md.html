A one-conduit return for polyethylene sheets

The north caster at (5,81,−6) made a full stack and stopped with 6,792 mB polyethylene still in its inlet. Meanwhile the network reported missing sheets on the blank pattern and Refined Circuit orders. One item conduit on the drum at (4,81,−6) can reach both the caster to its east and the existing ME interface to its south. The caster keeps its plate mold. Its east-side plastic supply and the circuit assembler's reserve share the existing reactor.

The conduit is installed. Enabling extraction from the caster raised ME from zero sheets to sixteen during observation, then to 47. The previous 64 were collected by hand and can be banked for the new patterns. The return tender requests blanks in lots up to sixteen when fewer than eight remain and the sheets are funded.
