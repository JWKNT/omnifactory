Zinc foil for machine filters

The storage survey found ten zinc dust, six emeralds and one lapis lazuli. The filters for quests 788 and 789 need thirty zinc foil altogether. The Cluster Mill is the only live JEI route for foil. Eight zinc dust smelt into eight ingots, which compress to eight plates and mill to 32 foil. The three ordinary covers use one steel plate, one emerald plate and one lapis plate. The Smart Item Filter takes two further emerald plates and one Basic Circuit. Emerald plates substitute for olivine in this instance. Both quests pay a nickel and leave the covers intact.

The electronics order will make the filters after the Cluster Mill has produced the first capacitor and transistor batch. Keep the Smart Item Filter for a robot arm at the shared electrolyzer. In Supply Exact mode that pair can admit the right quantity of each recipe ingredient, avoiding half batches that occupy the machine input. The covers, arm and automatic feed still need construction.

Quest 789 paid its nickel after the workshop held the Item Filter, Ore Dictionary Filter and Fluid Filter together. All 24 zinc foil for those three covers came from six plates. Two more zinc ingots have been compressed for the Smart Item Filter. Both the emerald substitution and the lapis plate worked in the terminal.

The Smart Item Filter also paid quest 788. Its terminal craft used two emerald plates, six zinc foil and one Basic Circuit. The four covers are available for future automation.
