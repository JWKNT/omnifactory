What the first fusion ring costs

The first eleven cold Draconium Ingots already have an assignment, two plates for the crystal array and nine ingots for the reusable lens. The next ingot can supply two single Draconium Wires in the wiremill. Freeze one wire with 144 mB molten nether star for the first lossless four-amp Superconductor Wire. Page 608 pays a nickel and opens the coil quest. Keep the east freezer on Cryotheum for its first metal batch. Give the coating recipe a separate fluid hatch or freezer before repeating it.

Every double Superconductor Wire takes two singles. Both coil recipes take eight doubles. Sixteen coils on page 243 represent 128 Draconium Ingots and 256 Nether Stars. The MK1 fusion ring needs 32 such coils, or 256 ingots and 512 stars, before counting any converted into a Fusion Coil for page 802. That conversion takes four Energy Flow Circuits or equivalents, two MV field generators and two neutron reflectors. Page 287 also asks for eighty LuV casings, two fluid inputs, one fluid output and sixteen LuV energy hatches. It pays ten quarters and opens Europium and the Plasma Turbine.

This sets a production target for three systems. Macerating one Ender Draconium Ore yields three dust. For each ingot the dust route takes two buckets of Nitro-Diesel and two of Cryotheum. The Tier Seven dragon flight returns 256 scales, enough for 128 hot ingots at four scales and eight buckets of diesel per pair. Cold metal still costs two buckets of Cryotheum per ingot. Test sustained output from the oil and elemental powder lines before a bulk heat. A single specimen of the craftable Nether Star is already reserved for the Wither model. Scale the native recipes for all four tips and the core. The implosion recipe returns three stars from four dust and JEI's macerator gives one dust per star. It does not multiply that stock.
