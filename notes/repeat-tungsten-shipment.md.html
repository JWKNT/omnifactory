The 5×5 table assembled the repeat Tier Two miner from the MV diesel generator, the third Electrical Steel Thruster and the other staged components. The small projector used two Quantum Flux and 12,000 mB rocket fuel. The mission ran for 600 ticks at 1,000 EU/t. Its full shipment reached ME across multiple unloading trips.
