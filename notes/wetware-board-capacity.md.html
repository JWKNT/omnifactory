Reserve production time for Wetware Circuit Boards

The Energy Flow Circuit opened the LuV circuit comparison on page 770. Keep using the LuV assembler for Master circuits through the iridium compressor. Page 332's alternative produces four Wetware Processors per board, but it requires a ZPM assembler at 120,000 EU/t, ASoCs and Naquadah Alloy wire. A board puts four of these circuits in reach once the rest of that machinery is ready.

One Dragon Egg, ten buckets of Sterilized Growth Medium and 6,000 ticks at 25,000 EU/t produce 64 Draconic Stem Cells. Each Wetware Circuit Board consumes eight cells, one multilayer board, another four buckets of medium, and 6,000 ticks at 3,000 EU/t. One egg therefore covers eight boards and uses 42 buckets of medium before final assembly. A single board reactor at recipe speed needs forty minutes for those eight. Leave room beside the medium supply for a second board reactor with its own ME input and return. The egg reactor and board reactors can run in parallel. Pages 327 and 242 pay a nickel and a quarter respectively.

The repeatable egg supply starts with the Tier Six miner. Its medium-projector Dragon Lair trip returns 32 eggs for one ship, 32 Stabilized Plutonium, 16 Quantum Flux and 16 Dragon Lair Data. Tier Seven brings back eggs, hearts, scales and Dragon's Breath together. The first End egg can satisfy Egg Thief alongside a torch. Keep that specimen until the page registers it. Tie the wetware production start to the Tier Six cargo and the medium quest on page 537. The breeder and Tier Five Naquadah mission remain on the path to that batch.

At 138 hours the Tier Six miner was built. Plan the first two boards around the reactor's three-bucket return. Ten buckets go into the stem-cell run, then four into the first board. Reheat its three returned buckets and add one more for the second board. Fifteen fresh buckets cover that sequence. The LV mixer makes four buckets of raw medium per mince meat, four sugar and one tiny salt with distilled water. The HV reactor alternative spends one mince meat for each bucket. Four mixer batches cover the pilot. An LV fluid heater turns 500 mB raw into 500 mB sterilized at 24 EU/t for 30 ticks. A distillery or distillation tower turns 576 mB ordinary water into 520 mB distilled.

The stem-cell run requires 150 million EU over 6,000 ticks. The installed Thermal Expansion capacitor capacities are 10, 40, 90, 160 and 250 million RF from basic through resonant. A single resonant capacitor can store 62.5 million EU in a CEF. Two resonant plus two reinforced give a nominal 170 million EU before the converter's internal buffer. Four Signalum give 160 million EU. Make and charge that reserve before starting a long LuV recipe. At the survey we had three Enderium Ingots, one Signalum Ingot and three Cryotheum Dust. The Signalum capacitor uses two Signalum Ingots, three redstone and one Cryotheum around a reinforced capacitor. The resonant upgrade similarly uses two Enderium and one Pyrotheum. Prefer upgrading an empty cell so the recipe need not carry stored energy across the crafting grid. The grid will still need replacement generation to refill the buffer between batches.
