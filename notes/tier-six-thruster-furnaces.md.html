Three Diamond Furnaces for the Tier Six thrusters

Each Resonant Thruster takes four Enderium plates, three Red Alloy plates, a Redstone Reception Coil and a Numismatic Dynamo. The basic dynamo recipe takes a Diamond Furnace, two Vibrant Alloy plates, two Vibrant Bimetal Gears and two coils. The Resonant Conversion Kit recipe starts from a dynamo. The basic machine satisfies the thruster recipe. Leave both working generators in place.

Make the Obsidian Furnace for Genesis page 18 first. Once its reward has been claimed, JEI's shapeless recipe gives both Diamond Furnaces back for the one Obsidian Furnace. The three thrusters therefore need three diamond upgrades across both projects, costing 24 diamonds in total. At 130h55m the factory held two Silver Furnaces and a plain furnace for the third. Protect the 32-diamond reserve in each generator. Feed the upgrades only with surplus while the simulations are fueled. Other uses of an Obsidian Furnace can reuse the same reverse recipe until it goes into a machine.

The Obsidian Furnace was crafted from seven obsidian and the pair of Diamond Furnaces. Page 18 paid an OmniNickel. The reverse craft returned both Diamond Furnaces to workshop stock. They now count toward the three Lapidary Dynamos for the Tier Six thrusters.

At 133h09m both Quantum Chests and all 19 heavy plates were complete. For the first spare Numismatic Dynamo the workshop already has an Excitation Coil and a Diamond Furnace. Two Vibrant Bimetal Gears and two plates together need ten Vibrant Alloy Ingots. The two underlying Energized Bimetal Gears cost eight additional Energetic Alloy Ingots. From raw materials that is eighteen gold, eighteen Energetic Blend and ten ender pearls, plus the iron gears and Grains of Infinity. The workshop has 105 gold, 44 pearls, two blend and enough glowstone and redstone for the remaining blend. A Silver Ingot can be lathed for the transmission coil's rods. Use the machinery and patterns that made the east generator to build the spare on the bench. The two installed generators continue supplying the factory.

At 135h30m the first spare Numismatic Dynamo was in storage. Its two Vibrant Bimetal Gears came from a single ME order. For the first Resonant Thruster the remaining part is the Redstone Reception Coil. Each coil takes three Electrum Rods and four redstone. Rods run two per ingot on the lathe. Both installed generators stayed in service.
