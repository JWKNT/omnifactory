Fuel and realm data for the first crystal expeditions

The gemstone and ore missions together take twelve Quantum Flux. The ore mission also takes four Wither Realm Data and 64 Petrotheum Dust. Save the two loose Flux. Five Extraterrestrial Matter and four Solidified Experience make four Impossible Realm Data. A mature Enderman model provides another route: one pristine matter in the Loot Fabricator gives one Impossible Realm Data. One of these and eight Hellish Matter make one Wither Realm Data. Wither-model extraction is also available but needs Pulsating Polymer Clay. Use spare Enderman production after the pearl reserve recovers. The two Shulker fuel routes are still needed for diamonds.

The rod maceration route gives four Basalz Powder per rod, double the crafting-grid yield. Fifteen rods and 35 Obsidian Dust are on hand. One more rod, or four more powder, covers one 64-dust ore mission. Thirty-two obsidian dust and 32 redstone then make the 64 Petrotheum with the powder. At 97h11m the factory had only twelve redstone. Wait for surplus before making the full stack. There is no direct quest reward for realm data or petrotheum.

The four Reinforced Mining Lasers for the two miners also need eight redstone blocks. One 32-ore coin purchase can produce 192 loose redstone through the registered six-output furnace recipe. Use that to cover the lasers and the Petrotheum while the Witch chamber supplies glowstone. The four lasers use the current 5×5 table. Together they need 16 solidified argon, 36 glass, eight quartz blocks, eight loose quartz, eight Advanced circuits and four Basic Capacitor Banks. The argon bounty already supplied 64. A bank needs four dark steel plates, four Basic RF Capacitors and a Power Buffer. Reserve the first twelve Quantum Flux for the two launches.

The Power Buffers inside the four banks account for another four redstone blocks and sixteen red alloy plates. Including the eight blocks in the lasers themselves, reserve 108 loose redstone for the blocks before counting the red alloy and Petrotheum. The SMD recipe makes three Basic RF Capacitors per craft. Six SMD capacitors, twelve bare tin wires and eighteen electrical steel nuggets therefore make eighteen RF capacitors. The vacuum tube recipe yields one per craft. Use the SMD route for the four banks' sixteen capacitors. The five-square table consumes four solidified argon per laser. The arm campaign's redstone order returned 96 redstone from sixteen ores. The workshop can order the other sixteen ores as the mission stock is spent.

The gem expedition also needs 20,000 mB of rocket fuel. After the repeat lunar cargo, the fuel reactor can make four buckets each of dinitrogen tetroxide and dimethylhydrazine. The mixer yields 6,000 mB per pair, enough for the flight with four buckets to spare from an empty hatch. The JEI manifest totals 32 exquisite emeralds, 96 exquisite diamonds and 64 exquisite rubies, plus 64 each of Nether sapphire, gold and silver ore. Show one of each exquisite gem on page 77 before allocating emeralds to the crystal chip furnace. The small projector already sends cargo to ME through its north item conduit.
