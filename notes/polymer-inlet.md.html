Feeding the polymer smelter

At 31h40m the current review interval included fourteen pairs of player-loaded clay and Pulsating Dust at the polymer kiln. The wiremill ME Interface already touches the kiln's east face. It supplies polymer to the Shulker conduit, and the kiln exports finished polymer back to it. Its first nine pattern slots are full. The Pattern Expansion Card costs a whole additional ME Interface plus an Advanced Card.

Test an LV Robot Arm on the kiln's east face in import and Keep Exact mode. The installed GregTech version counts destination items separately by filter entry in this mode. The ordinary Item Filter is already in ME. Configure one clay block and one Pulsating Dust as separate entries. Add one of each to the interface stocking slots alongside the existing polymer. Enable input on the kiln's east output face. The arm needs three tin cables, two steel rods, two LV motors, one LV piston and one basic-grade circuit at either the bench or the assembler. Neither the arm nor the controller has a direct quest reward. Use a stocked qualifying circuit first.

The dust reserve also pays for meshes. A Machine Controller cover costs two aluminium plates and one redstone in the assembler at 16 EU/t. It can control the robot arm independently of the kiln. Install it on top with a downward-facing ME Level Emitter on the cable above it. Set the emitter to surplus Pulsating Dust and size the threshold for the arm's one buffered dust and the interface sample. Test the switching point with a counted withdrawal. Verify two distinct batches while doing another job, an intact dust reserve, delivery of polymer to ME and Shulker, and another successful wiremill craft. Until that test the kiln is hand-fed.

This would free the single crafting CPU during sustained simulation. If the polymer trips still dominate the next review, fit the inlet before expanding simulation to a third chamber.
