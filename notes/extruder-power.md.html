An MV extruder and the first hardened dynamo

Page 540 asks for the Advanced Extruder and the Large Pipe shape. It pays a nickel and opens the Distillation Tower. The extruder takes sixteen single cupronickel wires packed into four quadruples, an MV piston, an MV hull, a medium steel pipe and two MV circuits. The large pipe and gear shapes each use an Empty Shape Plate and a wire cutter. Keep the large pipe shape after showing it and fit the gear shape for the first production run.

The spaces north of the present MV wafer cables are the standing positions for the cutter, engraver and both electrolyzer and assembler GUIs. Keep them open. The queued installation uses a short extension of the four-amp HV row instead. At (11,82,3) the High Voltage Transformer starts in step-down mode, receiving one amp at 512 V on its front and delivering up to four amps at 128 V on the opposite face. It costs one MV hull, one single gold cable and four single copper cables. Mount it against the HV assembler before energizing the new branch, face it north, and put another double gold cable at (11,82,2). The Advanced Extruder at (11,82,4) then gets MV from the south output. Check its voltage and stored energy before loading it. The row still has room to extend farther east.

Four Invar Gears and four Invar Plates go into the Hardened Upgrade Kit with one Electrical Steel Block. The extruder uses sixteen Invar Ingots for the four gears at 64 EU/t. The LV alloy smelter needs 32 with the gear mold. Casting uses sixteen but shares a solidifier with the polymer lines. Twenty ingots cover the extruder gears and the four plates together. Page 528 pays four nickels. The first kit goes to the diamond dynamo, whose basic maximum is 2,500 RF/t. Read the new maximum after applying it. The extruder, kit and transformer are planned after the titanium and crystal machinery.

The bauxite batch produced sixteen aluminium dust and six rutile dust. With the stainless charge collected, all sixteen aluminium dust went into the blast furnace. The controller began the first ingot at 120 EU/t for 400 ticks. This charge is earmarked for the extruder and transformer hardware.
