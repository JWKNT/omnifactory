What is holding back simultaneous work?

I took this review about ten minutes early, as the EV power bay came online and the refinery layout was changing. The quest book stands at 273/644, up four since 49h23m. The EV assembler is charged, a whole stack of glass fibers is ready for coating, and the lapidary dynamo gained 1,250 RF/t of capacity.

Since the last review there were 112 player-loaded stacks. The HV reactor took 16, down from 28. Chlorine and titanium tetrachloride fell off the list of the five most frequently carried fluids after the return pipes and export bus went into service. Sulfuric acid and hydrogen each took seven trips, oxygen and methanol six each, and nitrogen dioxide five. The lathe received titanium six separate times. More orders run independently when the ingredients reach their machines without me. All three CPUs were idle in 225 ten-second samples, one was working in 77, and two or more were never observed. There were 37 free item types on the drives.

Give the epoxy line its own reactor directly under the resin reactor. Its air centrifuge is already running, and the second air inlet is installed. After configuring both automatic outputs, stop carrying nitrogen dioxide there and measure the resin tank rising. Count oxygen trips separately because air separation alone supplies too little oxygen. Keep the water-fed paper bath in service and build a second bath for the stack of fibers. The shared pump batch for that bath and the Water Mill uses three medium steel pipes from nine ingots. The silicone assembler route uses 72 mB per pump. Make the rotors in the soldered assembler and put the finished machines where pipes can serve them.
