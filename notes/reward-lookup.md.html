Finding quest alternatives to recipes

Run python3 scripts/reward_lookup.py minecraft:iron_ingot to find matching rewards across all chapters. Add a metadata number for GregTech items. Each match shows the quantity, whether the reward offers a choice, and prerequisite quests. Neither ordinary iron ingots nor wrought iron ingots are listed as rewards, so the opening mine needs to supply both.
