What the first diamond dynamo costs

Quest 781 checks the Numismatic Dynamo and its Lapidary Calibration augment together. The augment needs eight diamond plates and one emerald. The LV compressor makes each plate from one diamond. The dynamo needs a Diamond Furnace; the upgrade from a Gold Furnace takes a further eight diamonds. Reserve at least seventeen diamonds across the Shulker model, furnace and augment, plus the first diamonds to burn. A Shulker pristine yields six diamonds. The existing steam plant can continue powering the workshop while the new system starts.

The dynamo also needs two vibrant plates and two Vibrant Bimetal Gears. Each gear takes four vibrant ingots and an Energized Bimetal Gear, which itself takes four energetic ingots and an Infinity Bimetal Gear. Total alloy budget is ten vibrant ingots and eight energetic ingots. Two vibrant ingots are banked. Four of the six banked energetic ingots are reserved for the Loot Fabricator. With that reservation, make fourteen more Energetic Alloy, then convert eight of those to vibrant using eight pearls. This is in addition to pearls for data-model progression. The Excitation Coil needs five red alloy plates and two Redstone Conductance Coils. Use the JEI output with the furnace and gears when crafting the basic dynamo, since several tier conversion recipes also show up for the same item ID.

For later obsidian, quest 352 suggests a Fluid Solidifier. Its block mold turns 1000 mB of lava into one obsidian in 1024 ticks at 16 EU/t. The LV Chemical Reactor makes 1000 mB lava from a magma block in 120 ticks at 30 EU/t. Pristine Blaze Matter can make 32 magma blocks in the Loot Fabricator. Before that model is available, the camp pool is still the earliest source for the second dark steel hull.

The workshop planner now expands thermalexpansion:dynamo@5 and thermalexpansion:augment@720 all the way through their gears, coils, furnace upgrades and plates. The EBF inputs are separate orders. After the MV wire, the bank has one energetic ingot and two vibrant. Make at least fifteen fresh energetic ingots: eight become vibrant and seven join the saved ingot for the two Energized Bimetal Gears. Sixteen Energetic Blend is a convenient mixer batch. With seven tricalcium phosphate dust in storage, the mixer can produce eight glowstone dust from four phosphate and four gold dust. Eight glowstone and eight redstone give sixteen blend. The EBF takes one gold ingot and one blend per energetic ingot, 400 ticks at 120 EU/t. Conversion to vibrant takes one pearl and one energetic ingot, 600 ticks at 120 EU/t. Budget eight extra pearls after the five reserved for Shulker matter.

The workshop's four regular furnaces and the old camp's gold-named station are ordinary furnaces. The gold furnace for the dynamo is built through iron, copper, silver, gold and finally diamond upgrades. A dollar breaks into four quarters, covering both a 32-ore gold batch and a 32-ore emerald batch. Three nickels buy 32 silver ore. Coal and charcoal can fire just the immediate requirement, leaving the rest of the ore in storage. The furnace line consumes eight ingots of each upgrade metal and eight diamonds. The calibration augment uses eight more diamonds and one emerald.

The calibration augment is crafted. Its eight compressed diamond plates and one emerald have been spent. The two starting vibrant ingots are now plates, and the first sixteen-energetic EBF batch is underway. The copper, silver, gold and diamond furnace upgrades each pay a nickel on quests 15, 16, 17 and 20. scripts/dynamo_furnace.py starts with the workshop iron furnace in the backpack, shows each upgrade to the quest book and takes its reward before crafting the next one. Run it before a combined dynamo order can consume those intermediate furnaces. The four blueprints use eight copper ingots, eight silver, eight gold and eight diamonds in that order.
