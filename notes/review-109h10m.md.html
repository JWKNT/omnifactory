Paying for the next two miners

Over this interval the player loaded 93 ingredient stacks. The HV ore mill took twelve, the alloy smelter ten and the macerator nine. Four small Pulsating Dust loads and four iron loads went to the alloy smelter. The repeat tungsten flight needs five Pulsating Crystals between flux and thrusters. The first tier-five flight raises demand by another nineteen. Three crystals have already gone through the existing MV autoclave. Route its output into the washer's ME return overhead. Use the EV electrolyzer's Enderpearl Dust recipe and the smelter's iron recipe through ME, checking which ingredients the planner actually names. Verify two consecutive orders by returned stock or by the downstream jets they fund. Reusing the extractor and autoclave saves two hulls, a pump and another machine aisle. Leave enough room at the roof for a service perch.

A dedicated hatch supply should stop those trips as flights become frequent. Keep a measured 12-bucket reserve for the next launch.

The first new Elite Quantum Processor reached storage. Five Elite circuits fund the IV engraving machine and its emitter. Use the installed IV line for this first five. Ten Engraved Crystal Chips from the fifth furnace put the four-output Crystal Circuit recipe within reach. Its 10,000 EU/t assembly comes after the IV laser produces the CPUs. Reprice the eight-output SoC route when its component and power supply exist.

MV takes twenty-four; EV takes six and needs a dearer hull, motors and power connection. Ordinary HV centrifuging still has boron and platinum work. Reserve the thermal machine's top face for its ME interface. Check the ore cargo, the second crystal delivery and the breeder floor at the next review.
