Chlorine from an EV salt order

The HV titanium reactor consumes four buckets of chlorine per ingot. The MV electrolyzer often has a partial bauxite charge, and the LV electrolyzer also makes carbon and quartz. The EV electrolyzer at (18,82,−9) already had high voltage, automatic item return to the west and an unfiltered fluid import bus on its south side. A fiber-reinforced board stranded in its only input slot was recovered. The adjacent multilayer-board interface now points east into its chemical reactor.

The ME Interface at (17,82,−9) points east into the EV electrolyzer. Its new pattern takes two Salt and lists one Sodium Dust as its item output. Chlorine goes separately through the south fluid import bus. One Salt Ore smelts into three Salt on the bulk electric furnace pattern. An order for 12 sodium used 24 salt. The CPU cleared and stored chlorine rose from 6,000 to 18,000 mB. This supplies the same 12 buckets as the slower manual electrolyte charge and leaves the LV cell free for carbon.

The workshop orders another batch when the chlorine in ME and the HV reactor combined falls below 24 buckets. Check the running tungsten job before loading that shared EV cell. Once the salt pattern is installed, leave it to the network. A loop from stored chlorine to the HV reactor inlet would also eliminate the remaining portable tank trips.
