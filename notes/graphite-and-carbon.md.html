Graphite for titanium, carbide and the carbon bounty

A five-penny ore order bought 32 graphite ore. ME can smelt and grind graphite and electrolyze each dust into four carbon dust. Titanium has already used the first of this material. The two crystal missions need nineteen more tungsten carbide smelts after the first pair of ingots. Each smelt uses one carbon dust.

The carbon element page requires 64 carbon ingots and pays one quarter. Ordinary furnace smelting turns carbon dust into ingots one for one, so the Multi Smelter can do a whole stack in one charge. Stock dust through the existing native graphite route in batches of up to 32. Charge the furnace only with at least sixteen dust still available and enough graphite left from the original order to fund later jobs. Bring the stack to the quest book before using any ingots.
