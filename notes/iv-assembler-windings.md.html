Copper for the first IV assembler

The initial IV assembler needs ten IV motors. Each arm contains two motors plus a third in its piston, and each conveyor needs two. The table recipe for each motor uses four sixteenfold annealed copper wires. Forty such bundles mean 640 single wires, or 320 annealed ingots through the wiremill. Just annealing them will consume 320,000 mB oxygen. The copper Spider line has already produced 110 ingots in ME including the earlier stock. At the survey the west annealing hatch and its 256,000 mB distribution drum were both empty. Buying another wiremill alone cannot solve that shortage.

The existing ME wiremill can make the single wire, and ME already makes eightfold bundles. Two eightfold bundles make a sixteenfold bundle on the Molecular Assembler without a tool. The component shelf will carry that recipe as well as HV motors and belts. Keep eight eightfold bundles available for current EV motors. Give surplus to IV windings. Monitor actual oxygen delivery to the existing west furnace's pipe. Page 580 recommends keeping older furnaces on stock production as new furnaces take higher-temperature orders.

The IV hull assembler recipe saves one tungstensteel plate per hull by using 288 mB polyethylene. The IV assembler additionally needs two master circuits. Each Nanoprocessor Mainframe takes two Nanoprocessor Arrays, four tungstensteel frames, eight Lumium wires, twelve RAM, twelve SMD diodes, sixteen transistors, twenty capacitors and twenty-four resistors. The EV assembler qualifies at 2,000 EU/t and 72 mB solder. Page 524 pays a dollar. Page 529 pays a quarter for the IV assembler. Its 3,000 EU/t recipe makes four Quantumprocessors from one multilayer board, one QBit CPU, one Nano CPU, two fine platinum wires, four capacitors and two transistors. The board and copper lines are needed long before the machine is finished.

The route for water-electrolysis oxygen can start at the Fluix cable at (5,83,0), pass through (5,84,0) and (6,84,0), and end at (6,84,1) pointing down into the drum. All three positions were clear at 62h22m. scripts/blast_oxygen_pipe.py waits for a working compact source, the acid reactor's dual feed and 16,000 mB in ME before fitting this export. The proof is an increase of at least 8,000 mB across drum and hatch. The west furnace can then reserve 320 annealed ingots' worth for IV and 32 for EV while accounting for completed wire bundles and motors.

The full assembler bill uses 34 single tungsten cables and two platinum cables. Insulating those takes 1,728 mB silicone. Add one tungsten cable and another 48 mB for the step-up transformer. The JEI recipe for the Insane Voltage Transformer takes an EV hull, two small coils, four aluminium cables and one tungsten cable. Four EV amps supply one IV amp in step-up mode. The IV CEF alternative takes an IV hull, an elite circuit, two four-wire Lumium bundles, four Lumium energy conduits and a chest. Build a separately budgeted EV feed for the transformer. The grid and 7,680 EU/t assembler recipes use the same motor ingredients, so the Molecular Assembler can start the motor order before IV power exists.
