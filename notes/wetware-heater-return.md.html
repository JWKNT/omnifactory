Return sterile medium from the heater

Each board returns 3,000 mB raw medium to the LuV reactor. Send a portable tank straight from the reactor to the program-one heater at (4,82,-6). That heater makes 500 mB of sterile medium per operation. Short-circuiting the intermediate ME deposit and withdrawal leaves more time for the assembler and the Assembly Line.

A live Fluix cable already occupies (4,82,-5), directly south of the heater. Its north multipart face is free. Fit a Fluid Import Bus there and verify that the cable stays active and sterile medium reaches ME. An import bus costs two lapis plates, an MV piston and an Annihilation Core. A full fluid interface needs five plates, an MV pump, an MV hull and both cores. Keep the full interface for the solder melter. The heater has a single product and its EU input already works. Inspect storage and the heater together after the first recycled batch. Keep the portable return available through commissioning.
