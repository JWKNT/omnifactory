Diamonds for the lathe and pulsating dust line

Three ordinary diamond ore were exposed on the edge of a lava lake at (-147,11,125), (-147,11,126) and (-146,11,126). Mined all three with an iron pick and collected three diamonds. The lead cave at (-186,40,165) connects to this lower cave by a long walking route; the final ledge is (-148,17,122). A surface scan showed basalt and stone below the south side of the ledge. Walking south at x=-147.53 landed safely on stone at (-148,12,125), with a one-heart fall. Another exposed diamond ore was seen at (-180,12,109) if more are required.

Buying diamond ore would have cost two omniquarters, fifty coin units. Use one of the three recovered diamonds for the LV lathe and save the others for later. Quest 29 accepts an ender pearl made by alloying a diamond with pulsating dust when no cheaper pearl is available.
