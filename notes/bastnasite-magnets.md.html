bastnasite-magnets.md

140 h 24 min played

The tenth ZPM motor needs two more long magnetic neodymium rods. Direct neodymium ore has run out. We have 23 Bastnasite Ore, and replacing direct ore would cost six nickels. The HV mill gives four crushed bastnasite per ore, with a chance of additional neodymium dust. The HV thermal centrifuge returns three tiny neodymium dusts per crushed ore. Three crushed ore therefore guarantee one full dust, even when the mill misses its bonus. The first mill charge is capped at six ore. The roof centrifuge can share this work with uranium separation. The west furnace, rod lathe and polarizer already handle the rest.
