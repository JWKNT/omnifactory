What the first assembler feeds

Quest 86's cheaper tier-one Electronic Circuit uses resistors, fine copper wire, capacitors and a phenolic circuit board in the LV assembler. JEI lists 144 mB tin or 72 mB soldering alloy per circuit at 16 EU/t. Quest 82 ties the boards to phenol from coal coke in a Pyrolyse Oven and the LV Chemical Reactor. The oven needs a second set of sixteen coils. Keep the blast furnace available for aluminium and energetic alloy while building it. Capacitors need the LV Cluster Mill, whose construction order includes six motors. Make motor and insulation batches with the next machines in mind.

A dry recipe that can commission the assembler is four fine copper wire plus one coal dust for twelve resistors. The bench makes three resistors per coal dust and uses paper. Allow room for a fluid extractor or other molten tin feed alongside the assembler. The electrical steel from the first clay silicon also opens the Deep Mob Learning branch. Quest 781 calls for a Numismatic Dynamo and Lapidary Calibration augment, sustained by diamonds from mob simulation. It depends on Vibrant Alloy and Grains of Infinity. A mixer and the existing blast furnace start the energetic alloy branch on the way to that power supply.

Build the first LV pumps in pairs. The autoclave and the Pyrolyse Oven controller each consume one. The chemical reactor quest requires the pump quest, but the reactor recipe takes a separate tin rotor and motor. The pump quest pays a nickel, the reactor pays a quarter, and the autoclave pays a nickel. The bench turns six compressed bronze plates into exactly two medium bronze pipes. For each rotor it uses four tin plates, a tin screw and a tin ring. The lathe gives two rods per tin ingot and one screw per bolt. Use the paper-ring pump recipe with a knife until molten rubber is available. These routes are in scripts/lv_order.py. Inspect the stock-adjusted order before committing more metals.
