Factory review at 71 hours

Since the 69h05m checkpoint the transfer log counted 63 player-loaded stacks and four ME orders verified by a stock increase. Six small loads were Lumium ingots going into the same wiremill. The HV assembler and MV wiremill each took eight ingredient stacks. Four hand transfers moved 64,000 mB oxygen. Three crafting CPUs were all occupied in 48 of 296 samples, and all were idle in 88. The two drives had thirteen spare item types across six cells. These counts cover logged actions and sampled CPU states.

The fourth furnace, first Nano Mainframe, multi-layer board and paid IV hull have made the IV assembler feasible. Spending the whole round chasing its next circuit left the factory unable to open the terminal when the diamonds ran out. A steam-fed storage core now gives a way to recover. The second Shulker was built from stocked materials and one east-queue diamond. It has already completed simulations in the copper bay while the first Shulker ran. Keep the copper model available and return it below 48 ingots. Verify that the new bay produces diamonds before budgeting that output. Ferroboron and Tough Alloy use the idle MV kiln and existing boron and lithium. Their quest rewards pay while the cuprate line is under construction. A separate breeder area will have room to duplicate reactors when later material chains need them.

JEI gives the IV arm a 7,680 EU/t assembler option and a grid recipe with the same motor and cable bill. Use the molecular assembler and the older Microprocessor Mainframe as its elite circuit. Save the new Nano Mainframes for the two master slots in the IV assembler. Its quest pays one OmniQuarter. An array consumes four Nano Processor Assemblies and four Lumium wire. Each mainframe then consumes two arrays and eight more wires. The EV soldered recipes run on existing equipment.

I retired the installed tungstensteel-wire pattern after all sixteen coils were built and installed the Lumium wire pattern in its slot. The MV wiremill gives two wires per ingot. The extruder gives the same two at six times the base EU/t for twice the base duration. A duplicate mill would need power, a feeder and an interface while this one already returns output to ME. Use the shelf space now, then buy expansion capacity when another coil run is booked. The next acceptance test is an order delivered through that pattern without a hand load.

The north bulk bus now gives the kiln a continuous clay supply. Keep these large single-item stores on a controller and use the remaining cell types for parts. The four-kilobyte CPU should take the larger machine recipes and leave the smaller units available for stock orders. Coal-fired furnaces have a specific recovery advantage when the grid is dark. Routine graphite, metal and board work belongs on the powered machines. The fourth furnace needs both filtered output routes so the player stops walking single ingots to the freezer or wiremill. Scale the routes down the new east aisle while the older three furnaces keep their existing outputs.
