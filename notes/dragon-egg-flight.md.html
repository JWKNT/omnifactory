First Dragon Egg flight

The two wetware boards need a supply of Dragon Eggs. Page 279 follows the Tier Six miner. The large-projector alternatives consume a Tier Seven or Tier Eight miner. The plutonium and flux are already funded. At 137h20m ME held six Impossible Realm Data. One data and eight Extraterrestrial Matter make one lair data. The player can cast the rest from Liquid XP while the Shulker chambers produce fuel. Pristine Ender Dragon Matter would also work but would occupy a simulation bay.

The formed projector currently reports 16,384 EU/t from its single IV input hatch. The flight needs 18,750 EU/t for 1,250 ticks, or 23,437,500 EU before wire losses. The pack structure in instance/scripts/Multiblocks.zs permits two energy hatches on casing positions and requires fifty ordinary casings. Compare the existing north IV source and a local converter before setting the route. Leave the LuV Assembly Line working during construction.
