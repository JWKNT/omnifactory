First Dragon Egg flight

The two wetware boards need a supply of Dragon Eggs. Page 279 follows the Tier Six miner. The medium projector's JEI entry spends one ship, sixteen Quantum Flux, 32 Stabilized Plutonium and sixteen Dragon Lair Data for 32 eggs. The large-projector alternatives consume a Tier Seven or Tier Eight miner. The plutonium and flux are already funded. At 137h20m ME held six Impossible Realm Data. One data and eight Extraterrestrial Matter make one lair data. The player can cast the rest from Liquid XP while the Shulker chambers produce fuel. Pristine Ender Dragon Matter would also work but would occupy a simulation bay.

The formed projector currently reports 16,384 EU/t from its single IV input hatch. The flight needs 18,750 EU/t for 1,250 ticks, or 23,437,500 EU before wire losses. The pack structure in instance/scripts/Multiblocks.zs permits two energy hatches on casing positions and requires fifty ordinary casings. The south IV hatch had 126,924 EU, and all thirteen positions on the old cable route were air at the last survey. Its old commissioning receipt is historical. Check the source and both hatches under load before sending the ship.

Plan for a second IV hatch and a shared four-amp IV feed. Two IV hatches also worked at a smaller scale on the small projector's paired HV inputs. Bare double tungsten wire carries four amps and loses voltage along a long route. Four-strand Lumium has no loss but costs more metal. Compare the existing north IV source and a local converter before setting the route. Leave the LuV Assembly Line working during construction.

The first six Dragon Lair Data reached storage. The ball mold for the remaining XP was in the parts chest at (10,80,4), while the block mold occupied the MV caster. The XP tender can now start extraction while the caster finishes another job and exchange the mold once liquid is ready. For the projector, put the second IV hatch at (23,80,-27), immediately east of the original. Four-amp insulated double tungsten fits the old route and a one-block fork. The controller should report 32,768 EU/t with both hatches installed. Check the north IV source has 24.5 million EU before departure and claim the ship’s quarter on page 254 first.
