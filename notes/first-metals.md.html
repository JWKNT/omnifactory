Metals for the first circuits

More Materials asks for 76 redstone, 36 copper ingots, 20 tin ingots, 16 coal and six gold ingots. The iron mine supplied seven gold ore. Two copper ore blocks were exposed near (-196, 55, 196), and tin appeared at (-171, 58, 188). These are LibVulpes ores; check the actual furnace output before committing to a large batch.

The most attractive opening coin purchase is redstone. JEI lists five omnipennies for 32 GregTech redstone ore. Furnace smelting gives six dust per GregTech ore, so thirteen ore cover the first 76 dust. A single omninickel breaks into five pennies. Copper, tin and iron ore cost two nickels for 32. Keep one ore from any purchased batch as a possible sample for the scanner's block module.

Rubber trees at (-136, 67, 168) and (-120, 65, 200) drop usable rubber while chopping the logs with a stone axe. The initial trip yielded thirteen rubber. The Rubber Sheets quest additionally asks for a plantball and a slimeball. JEI accepts eight saplings, eight vines or eight leaves for a plantball.
