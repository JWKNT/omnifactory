The Tier Six miner's two IV field generators

Each generator takes four Elite circuits and an Improved Nether Star. The table uses four single Lumium wires per generator. The IV assembler can substitute molten Lumium for the wires. The workshop has two wires and two Crystal Circuits, and will need eight Elite circuits in total.

The LuV assembler gives four Crystal Circuits per batch. Two batches need two multilayer boards, two Crystal CPUs, two Nano CPUs, four fine niobium-titanium wires, sixteen SMD resistors, eight SMD transistors and 144 mB solder. At 133h01m all the item costs except transistors were in stock; the workshop had two transistors. The eight-output alternative needs a ZPM assembler and a Crystal SoC. Quantum Processors, Nanoprocessor Arrays and Microprocessor Mainframes also qualify, but each recipe returns one and uses more parts. Use the existing four-output LuV pattern.

One MV assembler run produces 32 transistors for six Fine Annealed Copper Wires, one Gallium Plate and 288 mB polyethylene. The wiremill turns the one stored annealed copper wire into four fine wires, in addition to the two already in ME. There are 112 tiny piles of gallium. Nine tiny piles make one dust; smelt the dust and compress the ingot. This starts the next circuit lot without having to wash more ore.

At 133h16m the electric furnace held four finished Gallium Ingots. The basic wiremill held four Fine Annealed Copper Wires and two more were in ME. These cover the first 32-transistor batch once the compressor returns a Gallium Plate and the MV assembler receives its polyethylene.

The four fine wires were collected. The MV assembler holds 108 mB polyethylene, 180 mB short of a transistor batch. Its source reactor contains 64 buckets of oxygen and 120 mB ethylene. Another 200 mB ethylene are in fluid storage. Use the existing hull refill and assembler pipe for the transistor lot as soon as the six wires are available. Give the assembler the next polyethylene before drawing another sheet batch.
