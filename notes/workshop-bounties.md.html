Bounty board by the factory stores

The recovered camp board stands at (16,80,6). Stand at (16,80,7) to read it. scripts/bounty_board.py opens the board and saves its current contract tooltips to run/bounty-offers.json. During a factory order, create run/check-bounty-board to request a visit between jobs. Each offer shows its own time remaining. Reopen the board before taking one.

The first survey found ten live offers. Four LV pistons would have paid forty pennies, and 23 stone widgets would have paid fifty pennies plus ten nickels. A single-material offer for sixteen wooden widgets paid 32 pennies. All were left on the board while the engraver needed motors and workshop time. A wooden widget takes two saw-cut pieces and a stick gear. Together its pieces take twelve planks and four wooden slabs. A future large wood or smooth-stone line could build widget reserves before the short contracts appear.
