What held up the factory at 59 hours

The three crafting CPUs all had work in only four of 520 ten-second samples. All three were idle in 377. The player loaded 116 ingredient stacks over the last two hours, down from 136. The HV chemical reactor alone took 16 stacks, including four small carbon loads. A fourth CPU would have helped in at most four measured intervals. Giving the existing CPUs access to the titanium reactor takes priority.

The heavy press gives a working comparison. Its ME interface accepted a processing pattern and the press turned ten titanium plates into five heavy plates, bringing stock from one to the six required for the second miner. The MV lathe turned 21 aluminium ingots into 42 rods. It still needs an interface. The furnace, freezer and salt return for titanium already have their own paths. The next available interface goes on the input side of the HV titanium reactor. Reserve one rutile, carbon and cold ingot to encode the job. Measure an order against cold ingots arriving in ME, then repeat it while the player works elsewhere.

The two new robot arms used Refined Processors from a completed order. Four Microcircuits per epoxy board remain attractive for recurring MV parts. Epoxy is currently the missing ingredient. Page 53's component stock advice applies to boards as well as finished circuits. Page 108's Interface Terminal could save walks to existing pattern inventories. It costs another interface, Engineering Processor and Bright Illuminated Panel, so fit the productive ports first. The ME drive still has 70 free item types across six cells.

For indium, a duplicate HV single-block reactor cannot run the 600 EU/t reduction. Reserve the east deck for a dedicated EV chemical reactor and four-amp converter. The EV hull assembler recipe saves a titanium plate. The eight-output aluminium wire batch supplies the new feeder as well as later EV hulls. The HV ore mill already delivered ten crushed galena from five ore. The first tier-two cargo should supply sphalerite for the paired concentrates. Read indium-bay.md before committing more metal.

Fuel and refinery chemistry still share a reactor and portable containers. Following page 730, measure direct filtered connections where two installed machines exchange the same fluid repeatedly. The forestry side quest offers a Farming Station to replace trips with the lumberaxe. Compare wood demand again after closing the titanium and rod routes. At the next review, count reactor hand loads, verify repeated native deliveries, and check how often all CPUs were actually busy.
