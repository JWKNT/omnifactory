Two Basic Quantum Chests for the Tier Six miner

The miner recipe uses two Basic Quantum Chests. Each chest needs an MV hull, three aluminium plates, four Good circuits and one MV field generator. The generator takes four more Good circuits, four Energetic Alloy wires and an Eye of Ender. Its assembler alternative replaces those wires with 288 mB of molten Energetic Alloy. The workshop already has eight wires and three eyes, so use the crafting table.

Sixteen Microcircuits cover both chests and both generators. The installed IV SoC recipe returns eight Microcircuits per Epoxy Circuit Board, one SoC and two Fine Red Alloy Wires. At 132h18m there were 21 SoCs, 76 fine wires and no boards. The older four-output recipe also spends transistors, capacitors and resistors. Use the two-board route.

The crop reactor already holds a bucket of glycerol. The resin reactor has 10,600 mB naphtha and 4,000 mB nitrogen dioxide but only 24 mB epichlorohydrin. Send one bucket of hydrochloric acid to the glycerol reaction. When both the board and cable lines need acid, make an extra bucket in the HV reactor. Draw from the silicone reactor once it has at least three buckets and leave two for its next silicon charge. The foil coater already holds 750 mB sulfuric acid. Verify the crop reagent and cast epoxy sheets before committing the circuit order.
