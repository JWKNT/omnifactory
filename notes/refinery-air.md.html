Air and nitrogen for the refinery

The first twenty saltpeter dust came from a 95-dust stock made renewable by Thermal Elemental matter. Four MV electrolysis cycles produced four buckets of nitrogen, twelve of oxygen and four potassium dust. The shared oxygen network started moving the oxygen before the player reached its GUI. A portable tank drew sixteen buckets from the full oxygen inlet at the PVC reactor instead. The programmed MV chemical reactor at (8,81,1) finished four buckets of nitrogen dioxide. The naphtha recipe needs exactly that amount for the twelve-bucket reserve.

Page 174 explains how to install an air collector and pays a nickel. Page 49 already registered the centrifuge and awaits 174. Check pages 173 and 176 with buckets after their prerequisites open. An MV collector produces two buckets per second and can use the tower's new four-amp branch. LV yields one per second and HV four, but the HV pumps and new voltage feed would not improve the present freezer throughput. Keep the top of (11,82,-13) open and reserve (11,82,-14) for an air tank.

The collector needs four iron bars, a zinc-foil item filter, two MV pumps, a hull and a good circuit. Reuse the ME zinc foil pattern and the dedicated solder-fed HV assembler for the bronze rotors. Make two casings and two hulls together. The permanent cryogenic line will need a separate freezer and a second distillation tower for noble gases. See cryogenic-air.md.
