Two missions for the first Crystal CPUs

The small projector’s gem expedition needs one tier-three miner, one Gemstone Sensor and four Quantum Flux. It is the only JEI recipe for Exquisite Emerald. The sensor’s IV sensor already needs an osmium rod. Make that sensor in an assembler to spend one tungstensteel plate, instead of the four needed at the bench. The medium projector’s tier-four ore mission takes eight Quantum Flux, 64 Petrotheum Dust and four Wither Realm Data. Both osmium and iridium ore appear in that cargo. The quest book also requires the tier-four miner on the path to page 77.

Together the two miners consume 20 Tungsten Carbide Heavy Plating, six Signalum Heavy Plating and four Reinforced Mining Lasers. One EV compression costs 2,000 EU/t for 200 ticks and takes two carbide plates. Signalum needs 8,000 EU/t for 200 ticks and two plates. Page 121 pays a quarter for the IV compressor. There is no separate retrieval task for an EV compressor. Both presses were still unbuilt at 96h59m. Build one IV compressor to press both materials. An IV engraver and an IV compressor together take four IV pistons, two IV hulls, four platinum cables and five Elite circuits. The engraver also needs an IV emitter. Keep the north line’s fittings and arm circuits together until the LuV assembler is funded. The open roof tile south of the planned power trunk at (22,86,−17) can serve the compressor. Prove available power before running presses and engravers together.

The 7×7 table unlocks both miners and the medium projector. Page 407 calls for its controller, 54 projector casings, 26 space-themed blocks, 36 dark ineffable glass, four vents, a Quantum Compressor and two buses or hatches specified on the page. Its quarter reward follows item detection. Verify the formed structure and IV power separately before loading the first miner.

The IV laser engraver is machine 434. Its IV emitter takes four osmium rods, two Elite circuits and an Eye of Ender. Program 1 in the IV assembler saves a tungsten cable compared with the crafting table. Its other circuit slots also accept the upgraded Quantum Processor. The emitter and sensor together need five osmium rods. Obtain the ore from the planned medium-projector mission. The alternative from washed chalcopyrite or pentlandite spends one bucket nitric acid per tiny sludge pile. Nine tiny sludge make a dust, and the centrifuge lists only a tiny osmium pile per sludge dust. Even before accounting for chance, one osmium dust through this route would take at least 81 buckets of acid. The mission delivers 16 osmium ore and 128 iridium ore together and is part of the quest path.

The two 7×7 recipes together use three basic guidance systems, three titanium chests, two tungstensteel chests, two ordinary engine cores and two Signalum engine cores. The latter require eight Signalum plates and eight Signalum rods in addition to the twelve plates for Signalum Heavy Plating. Each engine frame has four rods and four plates. The alloy smelter completes the four cores using twelve redstone blocks. The tier-three ship takes the three Reinforced Thrusters and a Turbo Diesel Generator. The tier-four ship takes four Energetic Thrusters and two Basic Reactant Dynamos. The three Reinforced Thrusters consume three more Reactant Dynamos. The floor at (18,79,−4) is cobblestone with two free blocks above it for the elite table, beside the operating 5×5 table. The ship tender reads both JEI bills before ordering shared components.

The running blast recipes put hot iridium at 2,719 K, hot osmium at 3,306 K and hot Osmiridium at 2,900 K. The east Nichrome furnace already reaches all three. Mill two ore of each type, process the four crushed ore through the mill and centrifuge, then chill the ingots. Three iridium ingots and one osmium ingot give four Osmiridium. Show all three cooled metals together for page 253 and its quarter. Three more osmium ingots through the lathe yield six rods, enough for the sensor and emitter. Keep the fifth furnace focused on the engraved crystal chip.

At 100h23m both upgraded Quantum Processors, both IV pistons, two platinum cables and the new IV hull became the Advanced Compressor IV. Page 121 detected it. Two tungsten carbide plates are ready. Use the south roof cable for the first heavy plate, then add its east ME interface.

The IV compressor is now on the south feeder at (22,86,−17). It charged to 262,080 EU. Two standard carbide plates made one heavy plate, drawing 8,000 EU/t for 72 ticks on the IV machine. Page 246 detected the plate at 100h37m. Fifteen heavy plates go to the tier-three ship, five go to the tier-four ship, and the gemstone sensor needs three additional ordinary carbide plates.

The four Energetic Thrusters for the tier-four ore ship each contain an Electrical Steel Thruster, which itself contains a Conductive Iron Thruster. Count completed upper stages before building lower ones. The middle stage needs a Pulsating Crystal and the upper stage a Vibrant Crystal. These are separate from the twelve Quantum Flux needed for the two missions. Reserve four extra pulsating crystals after the flux allocation. The installed MV crystal autoclave makes a pulsating crystal with a diamond and 144 mB pulsating iron, or a vibrant crystal with an emerald and 144 mB vibrant alloy. The same machine made the travel staff's vibrant crystal. Finish the present five-diamond charge first, then run the emerald lot once its tank has room for the other alloy. The diamond dynamos keep priority during a fuel shortfall.

At 103h21m the first Reinforced Mining Laser was complete. The two ships also need two MV and two HV field generators. All eight Vibrant Alloy wires for HV are already made. The assembler would spend 288 mB of the corresponding alloy per generator. The bench uses four wires, also two ingots through the wiremill, and accepts the same circuit grades. Keep the eight existing wires and mill four Energetic Alloy ingots for MV. Use two ordinary Eyes of Ender for MV and bathe two more with 1,000 mB radon for HV. The eye bath was empty, ME held 450 mB radon, and 244 Radium Salt were on hand. The EV electrolyzer used for the first mainframe can supply this repeat charge. The field-generator tender checks the eye bath, feeds the reactor and shapes the wire while the projector shell runs.
