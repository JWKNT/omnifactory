Measuring the ME Controller

The network ran at 838 AE/t idle with 457 nodes before the controller project. Page 816 says a controller can reduce power consumption with channels disabled. Measure idle draw and node count on the same network before and after installation at (11,85,10) above the third drive. A new controller and a new energy acceptor keep the original power inlet on the grid during construction.

Leave four Dark Steel Plates for the Vat. A Fluix Seed uses sand and Fluix Dust. Two pure crystals already exist and can provide the sample for a Growth Chamber processing pattern.
