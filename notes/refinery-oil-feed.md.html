Oil supply for the remaining EV circuit boards

The tower produces 1,200 mB of sulfuric naphtha per three buckets of crude in sixty seconds. Fifty tower cycles therefore need 150 buckets of crude, with about fifty minutes available to build the rest of the EV components while they run.
