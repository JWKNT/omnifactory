22 h 28 min played

Meter diamonds into the Numismatic Dynamo

The Loot Fabricator already returns gems to ME storage. The dynamo still receives diamonds by hand. Feed its east face from an ME Export Bus and leave a reserve in the network for tooling and emergency starts. The dynamo's top is an RF connection; the east face exposes the fuel slot. The survey found air at (8,80,8), immediately east of the dynamo, and at (8,80,9), directly below the ME Energy Acceptor. These two cable locations can carry a west-facing export bus toward the dynamo and a north-facing ME Level Emitter aimed at the bus. Check that the floor aisle still reaches the battery and dynamo after placement.

The only JEI export-bus recipe takes two aluminium plates, a Formation Core and an MV piston. The motor and piston patterns are therefore useful here as well as for interface robot arms. Fit a Redstone Card and configure the bus to export only diamonds on a high signal. The emitter takes a redstone torch, one aluminium plate, one Calculation Processor and a stainless rod. A Redstone Card is a torch and a Basic Card. The Basic Card itself takes three iron plates, three fine copper wires and another Calculation Processor. No chapter quest requires either device. Channels are disabled in this instance.

Use the level emitter to permit export at 33 or more diamonds in ME; start without an acceleration card. Test at 32 and 33 and observe the count remaining, the dynamo fuel slot and an actual burn. The emitter and export bus may update on different ticks. Raise the threshold if the measured overshoot would consume the working reserve. Keep the existing low-power tender until a Shulker chamber, polymer supply and diamond return have together sustained a full fuel cycle. This layout and control scheme are planned; the hardware has not been installed.
