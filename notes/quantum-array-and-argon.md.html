The Quantum Array needs the air plant

Page 269 pays two OmniQuarters and opens the Quantum Mainframe on page 531. The array takes four upgraded Quantum Processors, four Power ICs, eight each of SMD diodes and transistors, four Signalum wires, two chrome plates, one multilayer board and one Improved Ender Eye. The IV assembler uses 72 mB of solder or 144 mB of tin. Keep the new line on solder. Page 531 also requires the Assembly Line and craftable Nether Stars. Reserve room for two 12-unit lines when expanding the platform.

Two jobs can run alongside circuits. The HV Chemical Bath takes one Eye of Ender and 500 mB radon in 24 seconds at 384 EU/t. Eight ordinary eyes are in storage. The other JEI source needs a Tier Eight ship. Build the existing planned HV bath first and use one bucket for two eyes. Claim page 54 for a quarter.

The fourth furnace can share its Primal Mana supply with Signalum. The mixer makes four blend from four Annealed Copper Dust, two Ardite Dust, two Red Alloy Dust and one Destabilized Clathrate. Running all four costs one bucket of mana. A wiremill gives two wires per ingot.

One Power IC Wafer cuts into all four Power ICs. The HV engraving route uses a glowstone-doped wafer and blue lens. No quest reward supplies the wafer or the ICs. One boule makes 32 wafers, from 64 silicon dust, eight glowstone and eight buckets of argon. Start the cryogenic air line before running out of other jobs. Page 171 requires a controller, 52 frost-proof and 84 solid steel casings, five HV fluid output hatches, one HV energy input hatch and one HV fluid input hatch. The assembler makes either casing three at a time from six plates and one matching frame. Include four additional frost-proof casings if crafting the controller from a screen and four circuits. Build one tower first and reserve space for the second. The noble-gas recipe itself is 750 EU/t. Budget EV power for that tower. Check gas storage and actual airflow before doubling any machine.
