Measuring the quest book

The pack database contains 822 records. There are 644 distinct quests on the eleven chapter maps. All 644 have titles. Another two have titles but no chapter placement (97, Keeping Stuff In Stock, and 805, Genesis). The remaining 176 have neither a title nor a chapter. Some quests appear on more than one map. Adding up chapter totals therefore double counts them.

Use scripts/book_standing.py to compare the chapter maps with live completion IDs. At the first Sulfuric Acid checkpoint, 111 of the 644 chapter quests were complete. Including the second Genesis entry gave the previously reported 112 of 822 records. Keep both counts available. Check the End Game and Progression pages in the live book when verifying the finish.
