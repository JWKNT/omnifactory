Steel for the first plastic molds

Quest 548 pays a quarter for holding three Empty Shape Plates together with the block, gear and rotor molds. Each mold takes one blank; six blanks cost 24 steel plates. Keep one of the three uncut blanks to turn into the solidifier's plate mold after claiming the reward. The remaining blanks will also serve the plastics line.

The shop counted ten crushed iron and eight raw iron ore. Crushing seven more raw ore yields fourteen crushed iron. Hammering and smelting the combined dust supplies the 24 iron before the second firing to wrought iron. JEI lists three fresh steel alloys, each using one wrought ingot and one coal dust, charcoal dust or carbon dust. The existing smelter takes 200 ticks per ingot. The other ingot recipes recover existing steel. The book offers no steel or shape plate as an item reward.

Use the circuit batch to get the first sixteen charcoal cooking. One coke fires sixteen surplus rubber logs; sixteen more logs remain from the current stock. Grind the charcoal in the LV macerator and use it for steel while ordinary coal dust serves the resistors. The compressor can press steel while the assembler runs processors. The first 24 steel plates are reserved for the mold quest.

One Block of Steel and 42 mB of water make nine Steel Plates in the cutting saw. The MV cutter can take nine ingots at a time while the compressor presses smaller batches. The block recipe spends the same steel as nine individual compressions. The alternative fluids are 31 mB distilled water or 10 mB lubricant per block; the cutter already holds ordinary water.

The first sixteen charcoal finished firing and the macerator made all sixteen Charcoal Dust. The diamond hammer processed all 24 crushed iron from existing stock and the new seven-ore batch. The first ten ordinary iron went into a coke-fired furnace for wrought iron. The mold order became the main workshop order after the three arrays finished.
