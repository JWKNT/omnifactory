Oxygen storage for chrome and aluminium

The MV electrolyzer holds 57,000 mB oxygen after the latest ruby charge. Its other occupied outputs hold the four buckets of chlorine reserved for titanium, 20,000 mB hydrogen and 1,000 mB fluorine. A bauxite charge gives 16 aluminium dust, six rutile and another 11,000 mB oxygen. The planned 43 chrome from ruby for the Kanthal coils would add 129,000 mB oxygen.

Build an Iron Drum at (4,81,-6), above the coating acid drum. The solidifier has its own socket one block east. Both drums can be reached individually from (4,80,-7). The Iron Drum holds 256,000 mB in this instance, compared with 16,000 in each stone drum. JEI lists a crafting recipe using six wrought iron plates, two heavy pressure plates and a cauldron. Keep the lake-washing cauldron installed and make a second one.
