Oxygen storage for chrome and aluminium

The MV electrolyzer holds 57,000 mB oxygen after the latest ruby charge. Its other occupied outputs hold the four buckets of chlorine reserved for titanium, 20,000 mB hydrogen and 1,000 mB fluorine. A bauxite charge gives 16 aluminium dust, six rutile and another 11,000 mB oxygen. The planned 43 chrome from ruby for the Kanthal coils would add 129,000 mB oxygen.

Build an Iron Drum at (4,81,-6), above the coating acid drum. The solidifier has its own socket one block east. Both drums can be reached individually from (4,80,-7). The Iron Drum holds 256,000 mB in this instance, compared with 16,000 in each stone drum. JEI lists a crafting recipe using six wrought iron plates, two heavy pressure plates and a cauldron. The latter two items take eleven ordinary iron plates in total. A recycled drum also appears in JEI. The book awards none of these components. Keep the lake-washing cauldron installed and make a second one.

The iron comes from the Zombie model and earlier doubled ore. Reserve twelve iron for stainless. Use an empty portable tank to draw 16,000 mB from the oxygen widget, then empty it into the new drum. Check both quantities before repeating. Keep the drum for later blast-furnace oxygen. Begin with one transfer; another is useful whenever the oxygen output rises above 48,000 mB. This project can use the spare furnaces, compressor and cutter while the electronic arrays are being assembled.
